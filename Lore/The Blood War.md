# The Blood War

The endless war between the devils of the [[Nine Hells of Baator]] and the demons of [[The Abyss]].

[[Asmodeus]] argued at his trial that this is precisely why his corruption of mortal souls is necessary: the souls he converts into devils form the armies that hold the demons back. Without him, the demons would overrun the universe. [[Primus]] found the argument persuasive enough to acquit him.
