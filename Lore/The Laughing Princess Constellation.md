# The Laughing Princess Constellation

A constellation over [[Estrana]], and the key to finding [[The Abbey]]. A map [[The Good Ones]] picked up early in the campaign puts the abbey and the lost temple in the same place, beneath these stars, so reaching it means waiting for a clear night to pick the constellation out. See [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]].

**It shares its name with [[The Maiden]].** [[Yosco]] identified her as the Laughing Princess, and the party takes the two to be connected: the place they are walking toward is named for the same figure who is owed 10,000 gold and an apex griffon heart, and who said she would meet them at [[Yorrisk]].

[[Gribnik Mossfoot]] dreamed of the constellation, and came out of the dream with the name [[Orithian Blackspire]] and some link to [[The Drow]]. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]].
