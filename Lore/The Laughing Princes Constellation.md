# The Laughing Princes Constellation

A constellation over [[Estrana]], named after the twin sons of [[Orithian Blackspire]], who led a mutiny against their father. It is also the key to finding [[The Abbey]]. A map [[The Good Ones]] picked up early in the campaign puts the abbey and the lost temple in the same place, beneath these stars, so reaching it means waiting for a clear night to pick the constellation out. See [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]].

[[Gribnik Mossfoot]] dreamed of the constellation, and came out of the dream with the name [[Orithian Blackspire]] and some link to [[The Drow]]. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]].

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
