# Panrelta Timeline

**This campaign is set ten years after the end of Campaign 1** — in **year 10 of the new era** dated from [[Aeralath]]'s defeat in 1017 A.P. The era has no recorded designation yet. See [[Estrana]] for the calendar and [[Timeline]] for everything that came before.

Events in recorded order. [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined partway through the campaign, so the earliest sessions are unrecorded and the numbering below is **recorded order, not true campaign order**.

## Ten years earlier
[[The Sons of Thunder]] won [[The Great War]] and then [[The Demon War]], defeating [[Aeralath]]. The victory is marked throughout the material plane by [[The Dawning]] — **the tenth of which is now approaching.**

In that same year [[Bathoz Manefell]] brokered the Leonin–dragonborn truce and, with [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] newly crowned king of [[Grekosha]], opened the road that became [[The Pilgrimage]].

Where the Sons of Thunder are now: [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] with his tribe, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] king of [[Grekosha]], [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] champion of [[Torm]] in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]], [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] founding a college in the restored [[Blackwaste]], [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] chief inspector in [[Whitehall]]. [[Dolghast]] has collapsed into a refugee crisis.

## On the pilgrimage, before the parties met
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] and [[The Good Ones]] were walking the **same pilgrimage as two separate groups**, and did not meet until [[Stonehold]].

**[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s group**
- [[Thalrik's Road to Stonehold]] — out of [[Hor Garam]] with [[Dwendall Dhormas]], through [[The Shiftweave]] to the [[Tulari Hot Springs]], joined by [[Sven]], [[Hansel]] and [[Clara]], then the bulette on the road to [[Saltport]]. [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] made his pact with [[Torm]]; [[Dwendall Dhormas|Dwendall]] stopped at [[Saltport]]

**[[The Good Ones]]**
- [[The Centaur Poachers]] — found roughly forty butchered centaurs, hunted the poachers down, and met [[The Maiden]]. She spared them for 10,000 gold and the heart of an apex griffon

## The parties meet
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined [[The Good Ones]] in [[Stonehold]]
- The party encountered [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] at a dinner in [[Stonehold]] — undated

## Recorded events
1. [[Raid on the Thieves Guild]] — in [[Stonehold]]; [[Ky'ar Lastet]] leads [[The Good Ones]] to [[The Bettermans Guild]]
2. [[Liberation of Khulask Hollow]] — [[Crucius Ilyanich]] overthrown; [[The Drow]] burn the town; survivors evacuated
   - [[The Apex Griffon Hunt]] — killed in [[Dolghast]] on the journey to and from [[Khulask Hollow]], for [[The Maiden]]
   - [[The Shade Sent for Beemo]] — afterward, before the mines: a shade made by the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] attacks, sent to kill [[Beemo]]
   - [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]] — then, still before the mines: [[Torm]] names [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] the Golden Pilgrim, [[Diggory Psalm]] delivers the [[Hand of Valor]], and the price of the pact is revealed
3. [[Exploration of the deep mines]] — [[The Rockspeaker]], the purple worm, [[The Abyssal Orb]], and the [[Giant Memory Gemstones]] dreams
4. [[The Collector on the Road]] — and the next morning, [[Gegga]] kills him. He was a servant of [[Malar]]
5. [[Return to the Bettermans Guild]] — [[Aisha]], the [[Mossclaw]] raids, the feast, [[Torin Turen]]'s confession, the [[Sylvan Parcel]], and [[Yorrisk]] identified
6. **[[Torin's Flight into the Sagethorn]]** — *most recent*. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] rides out alone and is found digging up a soldier of [[Bane]]

Running alongside 3–5: **[[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]]** — [[Mirna Girbwood]] and the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] circle casting on the party's sleep, escalating each night

## Loose threads

### Immediate
- **[[Torin Turen]] is alone in the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]**, drained, feeling another presence where [[Illmater]] should be, digging up a soldier of [[Bane]]. The party thinks a hag lured him out and expects to have to fight [[Bane]]
- What the **black grail above a stone tomb** in [[Torin Turen|Torin]]'s recurring dream is, and why the green flames burn longer every night
- [[Ky'ar Lastet]] has the camp and wants the guild. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] endorsed him, but the ambition predates the endorsement
- The [[Mossclaw]] raids on [[The Bettermans Guild]]'s camp, and the one with the black mohawk who is expected back with reinforcements
- One of the guild's **stonemasons never came back** from the deep mines

### The hags
- **[[Mirna Girbwood]]**, a dusk hag and [[Beemo]]'s aunt, is in the party's dreams. **The whole coven is circle casting it together.** Spelling of her name unconfirmed. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]]
- She appears to everyone **except [[Beemo]]**, who is the one they want dead. No explanation
- "I like this mind of yours, I'll stay a while" — whether she is now lodged in someone, and whose
- **[[Torin Turen]] called [[Beemo]] "little shadow"** — a name only hags have used for him — and then said under zone of truth that he does not know why he knows it and has never spoken to a hag. He was telling the truth, so **something else put it in his head**. The other presence he feels where [[Illmater]] should be is the obvious candidate
- The [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] are trying to kill [[Beemo]], and may be trying to resurrect a dragon. Both he and the shade they sent after him are their creations — one "successful", one not, and it is unclear which is which
- **Whatever the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] are planning is happening in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]]** — the same range where [[Diggory Psalm]] built his temple to [[Torm]]. It went unseen by both [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and [[Torm]] until [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] warned them

### The Maiden and Yorrisk
- The party is supposed to meet back up with [[The Maiden]]. **The debt is still outstanding** — they have not seen her since [[The Centaur Poachers]], so the 10,000 gold is unsettled and the apex griffon heart undelivered, though the griffon itself is dead
- **[[Yorrisk]] is identified** — a developing town that [[House Keroskav]] of [[Grekosha]] and Iron Oak [[House Dufrey]] are at war over. [[The Maiden]] said she would meet the party there, and "upon the dawning" points at the tenth [[The Dawning|Dawning]], which is approaching. **That is a place and a date**
- Whether the battle for [[Yorrisk]] the hag showed [[Thol]], [[Beemo]] and [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] is memory, prophecy or fabrication
- [[The Collector]]'s cart held centaur hooves, which suggests [[The Maiden]]'s poaching operation is still running. He is dead now — [[Gegga]] killed him

### From the Collector
- **Boxes of black dragon scales** in his cart, packed like a smuggling run. The only known black dragon kill was the [[Blood Raven|Blood Ravens]]', so the scales are probably from that one. Who was buying is unknown
- The **[[Black Blood Pack]]** he swore would come for them, and his service to [[Malar]]
- What to do with the remaining animal parts, and what [[Beemo]]'s experimental brew does
- [[Starfall Clearing]] was his destination — never explained
- His corpse is in the bag **with [[The Abyssal Orb]]**

### Longer running
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] is to be invited to [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet|the Knights of the Golden Gauntlet]] eventually — [[Torm]] has confirmed it is coming
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] has been directed to [[Temple Trueheart]] to "see what awaits those of the golden soul"
- **Thalrik's pact with [[Torm]] cost him his soul.** What that means in practice is unknown
- The identity of the colossal golden dragon in [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]] is unrecorded
- A colossal thunder dragon, possibly [[Felgedrine the Inevitable]], may live in [[Dolghast]] — and someone there carries a storm staff the party recognized
- [[The Abyssal Orb]] is unidentified and came out of a creature from [[The Abyss]], the same plane [[Aeralath]] is from. The party means to hand it to [[The Sons of Thunder]]. An "ichor deep" entity is said to live in somewhere recorded as "shae de claw", possibly controlled by something recorded as "zogmoi"
- Why [[The Garden of Dreams]] matters so much to [[The Rockspeaker]]
- [[Gegga]] has a mask stuck to her face that she cannot remove
- [[Arthur Van Hark]] is probably dead, but the party is not certain. **Corrected in session 28:** it was [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] who tried to revive him, not [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], and the revive spell's requirements were not met
