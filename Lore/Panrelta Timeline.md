# Panrelta Timeline

This campaign is set ten years after the end of Campaign 1, in 10 D.E., the tenth year of the Dawning Era dated from [[Aeralath]]'s defeat in 1017 A.P. See [[Estrana]] for the calendar and [[Campaign 1 Timeline]] for everything that came before.

Events are listed in recorded order. [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined partway through the campaign, so the earliest sessions are unrecorded and the numbering below is recorded order rather than true campaign order.

## Ten years earlier
[[The Sons of Thunder]] won [[The Demon War]] and defeated [[Aeralath]]. The victory is marked throughout the material plane by [[The Dawning]], and the tenth of those is now approaching.

In that same year [[Bathoz Manefell]] brokered the Leonin–dragonborn truce, and with [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] newly crowned king of [[Grekosha]], opened the road that became [[The Pilgrimage]].

Where the Sons of Thunder are now: [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] is with his tribe, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] is king of [[Grekosha]], [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] is champion of [[Torm]] in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]], [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] is founding a college in the restored [[Blackwaste]], and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] is chief inspector in [[Whitehall]]. [[Dolghast]] has collapsed into a refugee crisis.

## On the pilgrimage, before the parties met
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] and [[The Good Ones]] walked the same pilgrimage as two separate groups. They didn't meet until [[Stonehold]].

[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s group

- [[Thalrik's Road to Stonehold]]: out of [[Hor Garam]] with [[Dwendall Dhormas]], through [[The Shiftweave]] to the [[Tulari Hot Springs]], joined by [[Sven]], [[Hansel]] and [[Clara]], then the bulette on the road to [[Saltport]]. [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] made his pact with [[Torm]]. [[Dwendall Dhormas|Dwendall]] stopped at [[Saltport]]

[[The Good Ones]]

- [[The Centaur Poachers]]: found roughly forty butchered centaurs, hunted the poachers down, and met [[The Maiden]]. She spared them for 10,000 gold and the heart of an apex griffon

## The parties meet

- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined [[The Good Ones]] in [[Stonehold]]
- The party encountered [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] at a dinner in [[Stonehold]] (undated)
