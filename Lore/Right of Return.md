# Right of Return

A blessing placed on everyone who sets out on [[The Pilgrimage]]. It lets the bearer return to life at 1 hit point the next time they die, meaning actual death (three failed death saves), not merely dropping to 0 hit points. Once spent, it cannot be recovered.

## The enchantment
The wording of the enchantment changes slightly between translations, depending on where the pilgrim begins. Pilgrims who start with the Leonin tribes at the [[Tulari Hot Springs]] receive the Leonin version, as [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] did. Those who start with the dragonborn clans at [[Ishtir Marsh]] receive the Draconic version, as the rest of [[The Good Ones]] did. Both grant the same fortitude.

Breaking the tenets of the pilgrimage risks forsaking the blessing.

## In play
[[Kalara Mainfell]] granted [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s at the start of his pilgrimage. It was spent beneath [[The Abbey]] when [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] killed him and he rose again, and again when [[Falstad Firebeard]] was killed by power word kill and returned at once. See [[The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire]].

[[Diggory Psalm]] asked [[The Good Ones]] about it at [[Temple Trueheart]]. None of them really understands its true nature.

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
