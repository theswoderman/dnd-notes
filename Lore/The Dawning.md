# The Dawning

**The celebration marking the defeat of [[Aeralath]] and the end of [[The Demon War]].**

Observed throughout the material plane. It commemorates the moment [[The Sons of Thunder]] saved existence itself, and — just as importantly — the unification of so many different peoples who stood together to do it.

**The tenth Dawning is approaching.** That is the point in time the Panrelta campaign is set against.

## Why it matters
- [[The Pilgrimage]] was created to honor the unification the Dawning celebrates
- [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] founded a restaurant after the first Dawning
- [[The Maiden]] told [[The Collector]] she had acquaintances "on yorrisk upon the dawning". **Both halves are now identified.** [[Yorrisk]] is a developing town contested by [[House Keroskav]] and [[House Dufrey]], and she had separately told the party she would meet them there. "Upon the dawning" reads as **at the time of the celebration**, which makes the tenth Dawning a deadline for the debt she is owed
