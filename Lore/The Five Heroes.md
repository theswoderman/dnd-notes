# The Five Heroes

The five mortals given [[Queensbane]] during [[The Sundering]], who drove [[Aeralath]] back into [[The Abyss]] and then used the sword to sever the outer planes from the world.

- [[Bjorn Sigurdson]] — human barbarian, the original wielder of [[Queensbane]]. Died in the fight
- [[Lyra Sunstrider]] — tiefling druid (Circle of the Moon). Died in the fight
- [[The Unnamed Cleric]] — dwarf cleric. Died in the fight. Her name has been lost entirely
- [[Dryzmir Aletall]] — drow ranger. Survived
- [[Vhal Nimzur]] — [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] wizard. Survived

Only [[Dryzmir Aletall]] and [[Vhal Nimzur]] survived. Ten thousand years on, [[Dryzmir Aletall|Dryzmir]] could no longer remember the names of the other three. [[The Sons of Thunder]] brought the three fallen heroes back to life at the end of Campaign 1 using a true wish.

The parallel with the [[The Sons of Thunder|Sons of Thunder]] — five mortals, one sword, one Demon Queen — is presumably not accidental.
