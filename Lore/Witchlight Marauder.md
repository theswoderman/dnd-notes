# Witchlight Marauder

An eldritch being capable of eating planets. [[Spirit Warriors]] were built to fight it.

One was released in the [[Astral Sea]] when a task force of elves and orcs killed the researcher who held it, and was defeated by that task force. See [[Defeat of the Witchlight Marauder]].
