# Defeat of the Witchlight Marauder

**When:** Unrecorded, but a year before [[The Sons of Thunder]] arrived in the [[Astral Sea]]
**Where:** A researcher's facility in the [[Astral Sea]]
**Part of:** a one-shot, outside either main campaign
**Sources:** [[one shot elves & orcs]]

## The party

A task force of six: three orcs and three elves, gathered shortly after a war between the two peoples was settled. Tensions between them were still very high. The characters' names are unrecorded.

## What happened

The task force was assembled to capture an elven researcher rumored to be conducting dangerous research. The mission's priority was shutting down his facility. Taking him alive was optional, and no one would have been upset if he died.

The researcher put the party through a series of trials to delay them, and they defeated all of them easily. They chased him deeper into the facility and fought him in his main control room.

Killing him released a [[Witchlight Marauder]], an eldritch being capable of eating planets. The party took up [[Spirit Warriors]], armor built for fighting the marauders that works as a mech suit and enhances all of the wearer's abilities. They defeated the marauder, and each character is now permanently bonded to their own spirit warrior.
