# Torin's Flight into the Sagethorn

**Event:** #6 recorded
**When:** The morning after the feast at [[The Bettermans Guild]]
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]
**Sessions:** [[Session 28]]

## What happened
[[Torin Turen]] left the camp before the party did. He and his pegasus [[Lightfoot]] rode out early, leaving behind a pegasus feather and a note:

> lansteads, i leave the kamp in your charge. the good ones have already left for the sagethorn, illmater bid me in my dream to catch them. trust that we will return soon.

[[Torin Turen|Torin]] wrote it, and it is addressed to [[Ky'ar Lastet|Ky'ar]]. "Lansteads" is a misspelling of Lastet in the raw notes. Torin left the camp in Ky'ar's charge, which is consistent with what he had said the night before about Ky'ar's fitness to lead.

The note is wrong on its own terms. [[The Good Ones]] had not left for the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]. The party thinks the hag lured him out.

## The scrying
[[Falstad Firebeard]] scried on the pegasus. It is a small pegasus, sized for a gnome, confirming it as [[Torin Turen|Torin]]'s mount.

Through it they saw Torin digging up a corpse with a shovel, and beginning to cast speak with dead on it.

The corpse wore armor bearing a black fist, bleeding green lines of energy. A soldier of [[Bane]]. [[Bane]] is one of "the Dead Three", and a major enemy of [[Torm]]. He has apparently taken a foothold in the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]].

Torin then wandered deeper into the forest.

## Where this leaves them
The high priest of [[Illmater]], sleepless, drained, feeling another presence where his god should be, and dreaming every night of a black grail melting over a cracked tomb, is alone in a forest where [[Bane]]'s soldiers are buried, talking to one of them.

He had asked the party to take him to the temple of [[Illmater]] in that same forest. He went without them.

The party's read at the end of the session: they are going to have to fight [[Bane]].
