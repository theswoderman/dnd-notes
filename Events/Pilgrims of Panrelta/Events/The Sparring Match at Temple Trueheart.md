# The Sparring Match at Temple Trueheart

**When:** 10 D.E., the day after [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]]
**Part of:** the stay at [[Temple Trueheart]]
**Sources:** [[Session 37]]

## What happened
That morning [[The Good Ones]] met [[Aurum]], a gold dragon wyrmling training with [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]], who insists on being called Aurum the Mighty.

The order then challenged the party to a friendly duel. [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]] and [[Aeroth]] fought first, with [[Diggory Psalm]] to join if the party could handle itself. The party defeated [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]], and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] joined the fight. Soon after they knocked [[Aeroth]] down and turned on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]].

[[Thol]] took [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s sword, the [[Hand of Truth]], from him. His first attempt failed, and [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] used the [[Golden Hourglass]] to give him another. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], down a powerful weapon, is still a very capable caster, though he is going a bit easy on the party.

The match was still underway when the session ended.
