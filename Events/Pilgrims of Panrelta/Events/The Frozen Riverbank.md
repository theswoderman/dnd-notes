# The Frozen Riverbank

**When:** 10 D.E., the morning after [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]]. Unfinished, and the most recent recorded event
**Part of:** the journey after [[Torin Turen]]
**Sessions:** [[Session 29]]

## What happened
[[Thol]] cast find the path to steer them toward [[The Tree of Pain]], [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] cast treestride, and the party crossed the frozen forest until they reached the icy riverbank the three of them had dreamed about the night before.

The bank was covered in corpses. Soldiers in armor bearing [[Bane]]'s sigil, and a far greater number of beasts of every kind. None of the bodies were fresh, and none of them were old.

Then the corpses began to move, and a gnomish scream came out of them. [[Gribnik Mossfoot]], [[Thol]] and [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] were caught by a magical pull dragging them toward the water, and werewolves came out of the treeline with an enormous panther behind them.

The fight against the werewolves, werebears and the panther ran to the end of the session and is unresolved. [[Thol]] and [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s mount ended up in the river; the mount held its action so [[Thol]] could get onto its back, then teleported up the cliff to where the panther was.

## Open questions
- Whose the gnomish scream was. [[Torin Turen]] is a gnome and went into this forest alone
- What is calling people into the river
- What killed a field of [[Bane]]'s soldiers and that many beasts, and how recently
- Whether the panther is a were-creature, a servant of [[Malar]], or something else
