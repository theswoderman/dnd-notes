# The Centaur Poachers

**When:** The first few sessions, before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]
**Sessions:** [[Early session catch up notes]]

> Reconstructed from the rest of [[The Good Ones]]'s recollections, not from notes taken at the time.

## What happened
Starting out, [[The Good Ones]] came across a field of dead centaurs. Around forty had been scalped and butchered, and all the young ones had been abducted. Poachers were killing them for sport and for their hooves.

The party chased the poachers down and, with the help of a large centaur who joined them, killed them. [[Thol]] almost died in the fight.

They then went to the port the poachers were said to work out of. Asking the local guards directly ("do you know [[The Maiden]], she's a poacher boss") established only that the guards were corrupt, and the party got badly beaten for it.

## Meeting the Maiden
[[The Maiden]] showed up, beat the party, and told them to do what she said if they wanted to live. She led the poachers collecting the centaur hooves.

She said they owed her for killing a great many of her men, and let them live on two conditions:

- Pay her 10,000 gold
- Bring her the heart of an apex griffon

[[Thol]] was beaten badly here too, as he had been against the big centaur.

## Why it matters
- This is the debt that still hangs over [[The Good Ones]], and the reason they expect to meet [[The Maiden]] again
- The apex griffon was killed in [[The Apex Griffon Hunt]], but the heart has not been delivered. The party has not seen [[The Maiden]] since
- Centaur hooves turned up again years later in [[The Collector]]'s cart, which is what set off [[The Collector on the Road]]

## Connections
- [[The Maiden]], poacher boss
- [[Thol]], personally invested; the centaurs came from his homeland
