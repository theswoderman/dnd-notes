# The Pilgrimage

The overall concept of the campaign. The journey [[The Good Ones]] are on, across [[Panrelta]].

## Origin
In the same year [[Aeralath]] was defeated, [[Bathoz Manefell]] returned home to the [[Golden Plains]] and finished his peace talks with the dragonborn clans his people had warred with, working out a lasting truce that let the dragonborn remain near the sacred [[Tulari Hot Springs]] alongside the Leonin tribes.

Having learned from his travels across the world's patchwork of cultures, [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] wanted to offer both peoples the same chance to wander the world and still have a safe, uncontested home to return to. He made an agreement with the newly crowned Grekoshan king, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], allowing them to travel from the [[Golden Plains]] to the lands once known as the [[Blackwaste|Black Waste]], now a verdant haven.

Many walked that path in the first year hoping to find the same wisdom in themselves, and it soon became a recognized rite: a pilgrimage made holy by the desire to better one's self and one's people. It honors the unification celebrated at [[The Dawning]].

It can begin at either [[Tulari Hot Springs]] or [[Ishtir Marsh]]. [[The Good Ones]] started at [[Ishtir Marsh]].

## Tenets
Travelers who embark on this pilgrimage must abide by the following tenets.

- End the journey with a better soul than when you began. The journey is a physical challenge, but it will not bestow any blessings upon you. You undertake this journey because you believe you can be better. You will make yourself better.
- Help those who ask for it. Protect those who cannot protect themselves.
- Travel on the ground by foot or by saddle. The longer the journey the more you stand to learn of yourself.
- If you leave the road you should return to where you left it before resuming your journey. Travel the entire path.

## Why it matters
The second tenet is a standing obligation to help anyone who asks, and it is the reason [[The Good Ones]] takes on most of what it takes on. [[Raid on the Thieves Guild]] began exactly this way.
