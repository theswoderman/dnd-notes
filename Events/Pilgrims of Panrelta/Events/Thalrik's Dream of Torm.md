# Thalrik's Dream of Torm

**When:** After [[The Shade Sent for Beemo]], before [[Exploration of the deep mines]]
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]
**Sessions:** [[Thalrik's Dream]]

## The vision
[[Torm]] granted [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] a dream. He woke not among his friends but in an endless field of grain in the last golden hour of the day, facing a foreboding range of slate ridges and snowy peaks mired in cold shadow. Above them sat dark, boiling thunderheads too tall to see the end of.

The thunderheads collapsed under their own weight and poured out torrential winds, shadow, and an army of embers. The grain caught flame and the soil split beneath him.

Then the ground steadied and began to shrink away. He was rising. The earth he stood on had become an earthen gauntlet the size of a castle, clods falling away to reveal rusted and scarred metal beneath, carrying him above the mountains, the clouds, and out into the cold emptiness of the stars.

The hand closed, then opened onto a colossal golden dragon coiled around a mountain peak of bright celestial light. It rose with a roar he felt in his body, eyes bursting into golden light, wings unfurling across the night like constellations. It released a mote of stardust that pooled beneath his feet.

> "Golden Pilgrim, your devotion has led you many miles, healed many wounds, and brought judgement to many foes. Good has been made of your oath, Thalrik Lastfeat… and [[Torm]] is pleased. Carry now in your hands what you wield in your heart — a brave star of valor. Let this fierce light lead you to [[Temple Trueheart]] and see what awaits those of the golden soul."

## Diggory
The dragon bore its chest, which parted along a seam of white light into a portal onto a snowy mountain peak, and [[Diggory Psalm]], the Hand of Truth, who beckoned him through.

Diggory told him:

- [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] informed him of the threat in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]], something that had gone unseen by both Diggory and [[Torm]]
- [[Torm]] bid him impart a gift to [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] to ensure his safe passage and eventual invitation to the [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet|Knights of the Golden Gauntlet]]
- When Thalrik reached out to [[Torm]] for more than strength on the night of the bulette, Torm granted him his unique abilities at the cost of his soul. Having sensed its purity, Torm could see this is always where his path would have taken him

## What he kept
[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] still had the [[Hand of Valor]] when he woke.

## Why it matters
- The price of the pact in [[Thalrik's Road to Stonehold]] is now known: his soul
- Two of [[The Sons of Thunder]] are directly involved: [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] raised the alarm and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] delivered the gift
- The storm and the army of embers over the shadowed range read as the [[Dragonspine Mountains]] threat, which Diggory names moments later
- Whatever the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] are planning there was hidden from a god

## Open questions
