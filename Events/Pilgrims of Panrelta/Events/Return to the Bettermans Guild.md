# Return to the Bettermans Guild

**Event:** #5 recorded
**When:** Two days out of the deep mines
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]
**Sessions:** [[Session 28]]

## What happened
[[The Good Ones]] buried most of [[The Collector]]'s animal parts and set out for [[The Bettermans Guild]], two days' travel from the mines. The nights of that journey belong to [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]]; the days are below.

## Arrival
Late on the second day, [[Aisha]] met them at the outskirts of the camp. She was disappointed — they had taken far too long, and they had come back **without one of the stonemasons**.

She told them about the [[Mossclaw]] raids the camp had been fending off, including one with a black mohawk who was chased off. The guild is worried he will come back with reinforcements.

[[Torin Turen]] and [[Ky'ar Lastet]] greeted them. Torin looked exhausted; [[Ky'ar Lastet|Ky'ar]] appeared to have been taking up a leadership role. There would be a feast that night, and Torin wanted to speak to the party afterward.

[[Thol]], [[Falstad Firebeard]] and [[Beemo]] entertained the camp's small children with stories of the adventure. [[Gegga]] set up the party's usual resting place, the tower she conjures — recorded as "Gauldur's tower".

## Ky'ar's warning
[[Ky'ar Lastet|Ky'ar]] came to the tower once they were settled. He is worried about [[Torin Turen]], who has **barely slept since the party left** and has been holding the camp against the [[Mossclaw]] incursions. He thinks Torin may die soon.

He also seems to want full leadership of [[The Bettermans Guild]] himself. He suggested [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] and [[Falstad Firebeard]] in particular go and talk to Torin.

## The feast
A simple but bountiful meal. **[[The Bettermans Guild]] is now about 250 people**, and the camp gathered around it has grown from roughly 100 to 300. The survivors of [[Khulask Hollow]] have been welcomed into the community and seem to be enjoying the gentler climate. Spirits were high.

[[Torin Turen]] gave a speech on the purpose of the guild — to create better men, and to offer a life full of strife — and toasted the better men who have died for that cause and those who still will.

## Torin at the tower
He came to them afterward and shared a drink and a story. [[Beemo]] healed him with lesser restoration; Torin refused anything further, saying [[Illmater]] would want him to bear his suffering.

Then he told them what is happening to him.

- **His relationship with [[Illmater]] has worsened.** He can still reach his god when he actively tries — but when he is not actively communing, he feels **another presence**
- [[Gegga]] cast identify on him. No real information came back, but she felt **raw spiritual and divine energy pouring out of him**
- His dreams have gotten worse, and they **drain** him. He dreams of a **black grail above a stone tomb**. When the grail meets the tomb the stone cracks, red blood comes out, and it bursts into green flames that melt the black grail. **Every night the flames burn brighter and longer than the night before**
- Something is eating at him

He asked the party to **take him to the temple of [[Illmater]] in the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn Forest]]**. He told them to **call to [[Illmater]] when they are at their lowest**. And he said he is confident in [[Ky'ar Lastet|Ky'ar]]'s ability to lead the guild.

## "Little shadow"
As he left, [[Torin Turen|Torin]] called [[Beemo]] **"little shadow"** — a name [[Beemo]] has only ever been called by hags.

The party chased him down and confronted him under zone of truth. **[[Torin Turen|Torin]] said, truthfully, that he does not know why he knows the name and has never spoken to a hag.**

Which means the name reached him some other way. He is a high priest of [[Illmater]] whose god has gone quiet and who feels **another presence** in the gap — and that presence appears to know what the hags call [[Beemo]].

## The Sylvan Parcel
The party opened the [[Sylvan Parcel]], the bundle of spell components [[Hiram Oakbrow]], a worshipper of [[Sylvanus]], gave them before session 6, and finally learned what its four modes of consumption do. Each of them also received a spell: treestride for one hour for [[Beemo]] and [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], speak with animals for some, and find the path with a one day duration for [[Thol]].

## Yorrisk
After the second night's dream, [[Falstad Firebeard]] recognized the two heraldries he had seen and remembered what [[House Keroskav]] and [[House Dufrey]] are fighting over: a developing town called **[[Yorrisk]]**.

That closes a thread. [[The Maiden]] had told [[The Collector]] she had acquaintances "on yorrisk upon the dawning", and had told the party earlier that **she would meet them in [[Yorrisk]]**.
