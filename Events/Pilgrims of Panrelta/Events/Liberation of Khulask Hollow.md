# Liberation of Khulask Hollow

**Event:** #2 recorded
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]

## What happened
[[The Good Ones]] defeated [[Crucius Ilyanich]] in a great battle to overthrow his totalitarian rule over [[Khulask Hollow]].

In the aftermath the town was attacked by [[The Drow]], who wanted their magic items and secrets back. [[The Good Ones]] defeated them too, but the town was mostly destroyed, and they led the survivors back to [[The Bettermans Guild]].

## Aftermath
- [[Mirgen Roe]] took over leadership after Crucius's death, and traveled with the survivors
- Mirgen told [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] he is always welcome back, because of how [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] treated him and his son before the overthrow

## Connections
- [[Crucius Ilyanich]], the Bitter Baron
- [[The Drow]], his allies, who burned the town
