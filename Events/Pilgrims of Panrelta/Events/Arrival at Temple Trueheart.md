# Arrival at Temple Trueheart

**When:** 10 D.E., immediately after [[The Destruction of the Abbey]]
**Part of:** the stay at [[Temple Trueheart]]
**Sources:** [[Session 36]]

## What happened
[[Zarisk]] set [[The Good Ones]] down at [[Temple Trueheart]], where a high elf named [[Aeroth]] and [[Diggory Psalm]] greeted them. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] is a satyr of about thirty, and looked older and wearier than that. The party also met [[Gillead Dragonsong]], a golden dragonborn.

### Falstad's arm
[[Falstad Firebeard]]'s cursed arm was cut off, and he went through a healing ritual lasting many hours. During it he had a vision in which he spoke with [[Berrenar]] and reflected on his role as a cleric. She was impressed with how he had grown, but suggested that he draw his power from another god. She named [[Illmater]] and left the choice to him.

### Thalrik and Diggory
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] spoke with [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] about his role as bearer of the [[Hand of Valor]]. Neither [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] nor [[Torm]] had foreseen the coming of [[Bane]]'s champion, and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] stressed the weight of being chosen by [[Torm]].

He then told [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]], as a secret to be kept, that [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] could use his assistance once [[The Pilgrimage]] is over. He handed over a [[Golden Hourglass|golden hourglass]], told [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] to flip it after three seconds, and jumped off a cliff. Flipping the hourglass turned time back and returned [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] to the top of the cliff.

The next day [[Aeroth]], [[Gillead Dragonsong|Gillead]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] are to challenge [[The Good Ones]] to a sparring match. When and whether to use the hourglass in it is [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s choice. He must return it before the party leaves, and is not to mention it to the others.

### The temple's people
Besides [[Aeroth]], [[Gillead Dragonsong|Gillead]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], the temple's members include [[Vallen Halberk]], a human cleric of the knowledge domain, [[Senn Blackstone]], a human sorcerer, and [[Hazel Salen]], an air genasi cleric of the light domain. Another member has left and returned to [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s service in [[Grekosha]].

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] asked the party about the [[Right of Return|rite of return]]. None of them really understands its true nature.

[[Gegga]] explained her mask: the spell she created it with went wrong and left it stuck to her face for good.

### Blackspire
As a death knight, [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] will reform within one to four days of his death. [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] will deal with him. His history as leader of the Blackspire Brotherhood is on [[Oraecheon Blackspire|his own note]].
