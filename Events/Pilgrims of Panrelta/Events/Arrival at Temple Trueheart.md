# Arrival at Temple Trueheart

**When:** 10 D.E., immediately after [[The Destruction of the Abbey]]
**Part of:** the stay at [[Temple Trueheart]]
**Sources:** [[Session 36]], [[Session 37]]

## What happened
[[Zarisk]] set [[The Good Ones]] down at [[Temple Trueheart]], where a high elf named [[Aeroth]] and [[Diggory Psalm]] greeted them. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] is a satyr of about thirty, and looked older and wearier than that. Running the order is apparently taxing work. The party also met [[Gilead Dragonsong]], a golden dragonborn.

[[Torin Turen]] came with them, and the clerics of the temple revived him.

### Falstad's arm
[[Falstad Firebeard]]'s cursed arm was cut off, and he went through a healing ritual lasting many hours. During it he had a vision in which he spoke with [[Berrenar]] and reflected on his role as a cleric. She was impressed with how he had grown, but suggested that he draw his power from another god. She named [[Illmater]] and left the choice to him.

### Thalrik and Diggory
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] spoke with [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] about his role as bearer of the [[Hand of Valor]]. Neither [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] nor [[Torm]] had foreseen the coming of [[Bane]]'s champion, and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] stressed the weight of being chosen by [[Torm]].

He then told [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]], as a secret to be kept, that [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] could use his assistance once [[The Pilgrimage]] is over. The order recently lost a member, [[Vladislav]], and is interested in [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] joining, since [[Torm]] chose him as a new champion by granting him the [[Hand of Valor]]. He handed over a [[Golden Hourglass|golden hourglass]] of his own, told [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] to flip it after three seconds, and jumped off a cliff. Flipping the hourglass turned time back and returned [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] to the top of the cliff.

The next day [[Aeroth]], [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] are to challenge [[The Good Ones]] to a sparring match. When and whether to use the hourglass in it is [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s choice. He must return it before the party leaves, and is not to mention it to the others. See [[The Sparring Match at Temple Trueheart]].

### The temple's people
Besides [[Aeroth]], [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], the temple's members, all of whom belong to [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]], include [[Vallen Halberk]], a human cleric of the knowledge domain, [[Senn Blackstone]], a human sorcerer, and [[Hazel Salen]], an air genasi cleric of the light domain. [[Vladislav]], another member, recently left and returned to [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s service in [[Grekosha]].

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] asked the party about the [[Right of Return|rite of return]]. None of them really understands its true nature.

[[Gegga]] explained her mask: the spell she created it with went wrong and left it stuck to her face for good.

### Blackspire
As a death knight, [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] will reform within one to four days of his death. [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] has members powerful enough to defeat him fully, and will deal with him. [[The Good Ones]] will not be involved. His history as leader of the Blackspire Brotherhood is on [[Oraecheon Blackspire|his own note]].

### Gegga's mask
[[Gegga]]'s mask was removed, and she was flashed back to the dream she had in [[The Garden of Dreams]]. She saw herself before a massive greatwyrm, her hand on its nostril, before the vision ended. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] recommended that the party bring the mask to [[Davynn Brindleknot]] at the end of [[The Pilgrimage]] to learn more about the curse that was on it.

### Zarisk and Tiamat
[[Gribnik Mossfoot]] asked [[Zarisk]] why [[Twitchtail]] had taken on a draconic form. [[Zarisk]] said that in some way [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]] has the heart of a dragon, which is why he has always been so different from other goblins.

[[Zarisk]] sits on a council in [[Bahamut]]'s inner circle. The balance of powers among the gods was thrown off when [[The Sons of Thunder]] defeated [[Aeralath]], and [[Tiamat]] is trying to assert power. [[Zarisk]] has been tasked with finding fifty heroes to hold her back, and thinks [[The Good Ones]] should be among them.

### The order's posture
[[Falstad Firebeard]] argued that heroes need to take a more offensive posture against the great evil powers, and that [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] is holed away instead of out helping the world. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] named some of the things they had done, taking out threats before anyone knew of them. He admitted that he had been unable to detect [[Bane]]'s presence growing: [[Bane]] chose his champion tactically, so that [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] would not be aware of him and would not seek him out.

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] is forming his own council, [[The League of Celestial Champions]], meant to be an organized force against evil in coordination with orders gathered around other gods, such as [[Moradin]] and [[Illmater]].

### The cursed items
[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] looked over all the cursed items the party was carrying: [[Ring of Dread Command]], [[Despot's Reprisal]] and [[Tyrant's Totem Necklace]]. He took them, purged their curses, and transferred their magic into a new item, the [[Ring of Divine Inspiration]]. [[Antagony]], the sword of [[Oraecheon Blackspire]], also came up.

[[The Abyssal Orb]], the ichor deep engine, is likely made of things recorded as "zugot moi" and "shae da claw" (spellings unknown).

### The Sons of Thunder
[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] said that [[The Sons of Thunder]] will wait for the party on a hill near what used to be the [[Blackwaste]], in northwest [[Grekosha]], later in [[The Pilgrimage]], to see them through to the end.
