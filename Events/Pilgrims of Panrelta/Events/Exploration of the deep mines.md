# Exploration of the deep mines

**Event:** #3 recorded
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]
**Sessions:** [[Session 27]]

## What happened
[[The Bettermans Guild]] sent [[The Good Ones]] into the deep mines to retrieve a few miners who had delved too greedily and too deep.

The miners had been taken by [[The Rockspeaker]], a stone giant protecting a location sacred to his people — [[The Garden of Dreams]], where the ancient memories of giants are stored and guarded. He was wiping the miners' memories to make sure they would not return, and would have wiped [[The Good Ones]]'s as well had they not defeated him in battle.

After he surrendered, he asked for their help against the purple worm that had been infesting the mountain.

## The purple worm
[[Beemo]] had Snot fly a frost bomb straight into the worm's mouth, taking off nearly half its health in a single turn, and the party dispatched it with alacrity.

A **necrohulk** then emerged from the worm's corpse — a creature from [[The Abyss]]. An orb came out of the necrohulk in turn; see [[The Abyssal Orb]].

This is a direct link to Campaign 1: [[The Abyss]] is where [[Aeralath]] is from. The party intends to bring the orb to [[The Sons of Thunder]] so they can deal with it.

Everyone gained a level of health. [[Gegga]] gained +1 Intelligence and [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] gained +1 Dexterity.

## The dreams
The party slept holding the broken [[Giant Memory Gemstones]] and each dreamed one of the giants' notable memories.

- **[[Beemo]]** — a large fungal cavern where giants had built a sun into the ceiling out of gemstones. It inspired him as a craftsman, and he slept very well
- **[[Thol]]** — the point of view of a fire giant in a war on a bridge. He was killed by a magma stone
- **[[Gribnik Mossfoot]]** — a giant witnessing dinosaurs made of magma, and a feeling of deep connection to nature
- **[[Falstad Firebeard]]** — standing atop a mountain at a temple, looking out over an expansive desert while stone giants chanted the name of their all-father [[Annum]]. He was turned into a statue as a ritual of some sort — a way to honor a master craftsman
- **[[Thalrik Lastfeast]]** — a cold dark field of colossal rock pillars: the [[Hand of Annum]], in [[Dolghast]]. Dozens of stone giants made an offering toward the night sky as shooting stars flew closer and closer, gathering [[Skystones]] and thanking [[Annum]] for the gift of that rare crafting material
- **[[Gegga]]** — through the eyes of a child in a cave, watching a cloaked figure at the cave mouth look out over a plateau as a massive storm gathered. A flash of lightning illuminated a colossal thunder dragon; the figure raised a staff, recognized as someone's storm staff. The dragon may be [[Thelgadrïm, the Inevitable]], living in [[Dolghast]]

The two with the widest implications are [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s and [[Gegga]]'s, both of which point at [[Dolghast]].

## The crone
Separately, [[Gegga]], [[Beemo]] and [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] had a horrible dream of a crone eating a griffin, who then attacked the dreamer directly. They took damage and woke exhausted — possibly the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]].
