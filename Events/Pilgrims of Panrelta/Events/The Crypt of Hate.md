# The Crypt of Hate

**When:** 10 D.E., immediately after [[The Road to the Abbey]]
**Part of:** the journey through the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]
**Sources:** [[Session 33]]

## What happened
[[The Good Ones]] reached [[The Abbey]], a ruined structure with a broken steeple rising from the forest. The surrounding grounds are desecrated. The abbey is also called the Crypt of Hate, and was once a temple to [[Illmater]].

### The Ilmatari note
Among red cloths bearing [[Illmater]]'s sign, the party found a note from followers of [[Illmater]] who had tried to maintain the temple. Written forty moons after the chapel was raised, it describes an empty darkness in the land that eats faith and tortures the dreams of the waking and the sleeping alike. The Ilmatari could not endure it and abandoned the grounds, writing that "Our Martyred Father does not walk here."

### Oraecheon Blackspire
The party learned that [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] is a [[The Drow|drow]] who tried to establish a settlement above ground. He adopted the icon of a bull when he founded his new House of Blackspire on the surface.

### The assault
A trap at the entrance triggered a fight against a mass of undead hands, followed by an ambush from undead minotaurs. [[The Good Ones]] defeated them along with cultists inside the abbey. Inside, they found pegasus feathers and a cloth stained with a black hand.

A locked trapdoor, opened by the key taken from the Fist of Oppression in [[The Battle at the Bridge]], led to a lower level where more cultists were stationed.

### The cultists' map
In the room below, the party found a map of southern [[Dolghast]] with the towns [[Brashta]] and [[Brinemire]] marked. An X in the Bitter Expanse marks the location where [[Khulask Hollow]] once stood, with a note beside it reading "who beat us to it." The cultists have been abducting people from nearby towns.

### The tunnel
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] detected a secret passage that opened into a tunnel leading deeper underground.
