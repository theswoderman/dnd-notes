# The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire

**When:** 10 D.E., immediately after [[The Tunnels Beneath the Abbey]]
**Part of:** the journey through the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]
**Sources:** [[Session 35]]

## What happened
The fight with [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] beneath [[The Abbey]] resumed with [[Torin Turen]] still on the altar behind him. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] is bound to the altar by magic and cannot be moved by strength or by spell. [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] had already spent his only legendary resistance, [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] had divine favor active, and [[Falstad Firebeard]] was still glowing with daylight, channeling the power [[Torin Turen|Torin]] had granted him.

### Quarrelus
Animated armor stood in the room and fought alongside him. Several times during the fight [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] shouted the name [[Quarrelus]], along with words in Undercommon. A gorgon arrived in answer to the chanting. This was [[Quarrelus]]. Its paralysis magic restrained [[Falstad Firebeard]].

### Torin's blessing
[[Torin Turen|Torin]] whispered "Illmater... blessing..." as he lost consciousness.

### The death knight
Midway through the fight [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] transformed into a death knight and gained new abilities. As he ascended he said "you're too late, the black grail is mine." See [[The Black Grail]].

### Thalrik's death
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] was killed in the fight. In the moment of death he had a vision of [[Diggory Psalm]] rallying him to return to the fight, and he got back up. His right of return, which [[Kalara Mainfell]] granted him at the start of the pilgrimage, was consumed in doing so. He fell again soon after, and [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] killed him. [[Gegga]] also fell unconscious during the fight.

### The end of Blackspire
[[The Good Ones]] defeated [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]]. Before he died he delivered a promise from [[Bane]] to make a martyr of [[Falstad Firebeard]], and cast power word kill on him. The spell killed [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]], whose own right of return was consumed and brought him back to life immediately.

### Aftermath
The session ended almost as soon as [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] fell. [[Torin Turen]] remained bound to the altar. After the fight, [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] was revived, and [[Gegga]] recovered.

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
