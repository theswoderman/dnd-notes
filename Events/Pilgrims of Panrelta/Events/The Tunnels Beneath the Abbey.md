# The Tunnels Beneath the Abbey

**When:** 10 D.E., immediately after [[The Crypt of Hate]]
**Part of:** the journey through the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]
**Sources:** [[Session 34]]

## What happened
The tunnel behind the secret passage [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] detected under [[The Abbey]] opened into a room held by a large mob of cultists of [[Bane]] and the Gauntlets of Bane, who are less powerful than [[The Fists of Bane]] but more formidable warriors than the ordinary cultists. See [[The Cult of Bane]].

### What the cultists kept
The room held the bodies of lycanthropes wrapped in the silver barbed wire used to trap them, along with the corpse of a lycanthrope caught mid-transformation. The cultists' documents record their operations: they have been kidnapping travelers on the roads and taking soldiers from [[Dolghast]] into the cult. The records go back at least five years, and the cult appears to number over fifty.

### The hallway
Beyond lay a hallway littered with signs of torture and a throne made of drow bones. [[Falstad Firebeard]]'s inspection of the throne indicated the place had been a stronghold of [[Lolth]].

### The pool
Deeper down, the party heard [[Torin Turen]] crying out in pain. At a pool [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]] was entranced: he saw the hand of a desiccated corpse rise from the water and offer him the red armband [[Torin Turen|Torin]] had worn. The binding wrapped around his hand and cut his palm, tightening as it went. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] spoke to him in a vision and granted him additional power. [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]] glowed with daylight, channeling divine energy from [[Torin Turen|Torin]], and shared some of the power with [[Thalrik Lastfeast]].

### Oraecheon Blackspire
Past the pool, [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] had [[Torin Turen|Torin]] on an altar, where he had been torturing him. The party attacked, and the fight with [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] is unfinished.

[[Thol]] drank a potion [[Beemo]] brewed from the remains of [[The Fists of Bane]]. Its effect on him is not yet known.

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
