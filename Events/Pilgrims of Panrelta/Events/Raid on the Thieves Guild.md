# Raid on the Thieves Guild

**Event:** #1 recorded
**Part of:** [[The Pilgrimage]]

## What happened
[[Ky'ar Lastet]] stole from the guards and was chased through [[Stonehold]] by [[The Good Ones]]. He then convinced them to help him "retrieve" his belongings from the thieves guild, which they were happy to do, being under contractual obligation to help people out.

After defeating the thieves and recovering his prized possessions, he took [[The Good Ones]] to [[The Bettermans Guild]].

## Why it matters
This is how the party first reached [[The Bettermans Guild]], which has been their base and patron since.
