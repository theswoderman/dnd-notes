# The Slaying of Minthur

After a few days of rest in [[Whitehall]], where they saw [[Darrius|Abrovand]] in his Darrius form, [[The Sons of Thunder]] took a detour before heading to [[Limbo]] for their fourth attunement. Having already slain [[Golzokroth]] and [[Rendibril]], they set their sights on a third hybrid dragon: [[Minthur]] the Mindbreaker, a turquoise (blue-green) hybrid. [[Mathias Ohstal]]'s notes said [[Minthur]] breathed a psychedelic gas that dealt psychic damage, and that he could be found by a large waterfall overlooking the Dreaming Jungle on the border of [[Rorinden]] and Isembard.

[[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] teleported the group to the Dreaming Jungle. Inside, they were ambushed by [[Viridian Sun]], a tabaxi druid who claimed [[Minthur]] was guarding the jungle from being harvested by the Isembard and [[Rorinden]] governments. The party fought and killed him. In his belongings they discovered he was the older brother of Jade Moon, a tabaxi bard they knew. They knew they should tell her what happened. They never did.

Deeper in the caves, they killed a tribe of jungle trolls led by a troll shaman before reaching [[Minthur]]'s lair: a high-ceilinged cave with a magically concealed opening in the roof and a deep, fast-moving river leading to the waterfall. [[Minthur]] attacked from underwater, using hit-and-run tactics, blasting the party with psychic gas before diving again. He whittled them down before being forced to flee.

[[Minthur]] turned invisible and flew for the city of Mushala, vowing to kill as many innocents as he could. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] True Polymorphed into a dragon and the party gave chase. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s keen eyes spotted the trees being disturbed by [[Minthur]]'s invisible wingbeats, and they ran him down. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] was knocked unconscious but they killed [[Minthur]] before he could reach Mushala.

The party recovered over 500,000 gold worth of coins, gems, art, and magic items from the lair.
