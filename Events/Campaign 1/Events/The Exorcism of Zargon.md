# The Exorcism of [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]]

At the [[House of War]] in [[Gildurn]], Warmaster [[Hayden Trask]] proposed an exorcism and binding for [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]], knowing he could not fully banish an elder evil. The plan was for [[Davynn Brindleknot]] to follow the ritual with a ninth-level Imprisonment spell, sealing whatever remained of [[Zargon the Immortal]] inside [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]]'s soul.

Not ideal, but it would stop the corruption spreading. The catch: if [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]] dies before [[Zargon the Immortal]] is destroyed, [[Zargon the Immortal]] consumes his soul.

The ritual worked. The Imprisonment did not. [[Zargon the Immortal]] resisted, possessed [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]] outright and turned him on his allies, on [[Diana Zethros]] and on Trask. The corrupted [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]] summoned a demi-plane linked to the [[Nine Hells of Baator]] around them and called up two ancient Baatorians.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] beat him. [[Bathoz Manefell]] cut off the arm housing the [[Horn of Zargon]]; Trask regrew it with Regeneration.

A celebration followed, honoring [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]] as a new but formidable follower of [[The Red Lady]], and [[Diana Zethros]] confessed her feelings for him.
