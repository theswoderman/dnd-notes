# [[Melvin]]'s Poison and the [[Whisperwood]]

[[The Sons of Thunder]] traveled north to the [[Whisperwood]], where Viera scouts surrounded them unseen before [[Lien]] led them in. The elder [[Zalema]] explained that the young and elderly were falling ill after drinking from the lake.

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] sensed a cave across the water. Inside: [[The Shrike]], [[John Longwater]], [[Callus Halterstock]], [[Dimble Flintbatter]], Prince [[Golimov Brighthorn]], and [[Melvin]], alive.

[[Melvin]] had had [[Dimble Flintbatter|Dimble]] cast Clone so he could step into a new body after "dying" and be hunted by no one. He was testing his poison on the Viera purely to spite [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] for taking their parents' love, and intended to use it on every city that opposed [[Dolghast]] and [[Ornuth]]. This explained the chemical-buyer manifest from the potion factory in [[Xinar]].

The party killed the villains, spared [[Melvin]], and shut the pump down. [[Golimov Brighthorn|Golimov]] surrendered.

**Aftermath:** the Viera lifted [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s banishment. [[Zalema]] revealed his birth name was [[Loryn Malcovitch|Anyil]], and that [[Lien]] was his mother. As they left, [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] saw two more Viera in the branches: a woman his own age, and an older male who looked strikingly like him.
