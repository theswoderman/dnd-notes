# The Hidden Continent

Jabari dropped [[The Sons of Thunder]] on the shore of the uncharted continent and sailed back to [[Whitehall]] to await their return (the party still owed Ghara J'boi a prison break in Cape Durk). The party ventured inland, following the pull of [[Queensbane]] toward the gate to [[Limbo]].

## The Reborn

After four days of travel, they encountered a seemingly bionic man, a wizard made of metal and animated by some unknown magic. He called himself Robin and said he had only been "awake" for a few days, right around the time [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] dispelled the fog wall. Robin brought the party to a hidden alcove where they met a dozen other mechanical beings who called themselves the Reborn, or the Awoken. Their leader, a being called Vanguard, bore a staff marked with the ancient symbol of the [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]].

The party cast Legend Lore on the staff and saw a vision of [[Vhal Nimzur]], the [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] archmage and member of [[The Five Heroes]], speaking with a friend about a plan to save a chosen few of the [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] by transferring their souls into constructed bodies. [[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]] knew the experiment would anger the gods further, but believed it was necessary to preserve his people.

Faced with the revelation of their origin, the Reborn began to remember fragments of their past lives. Several set out to explore the continent, while others asked to come to the main continents to see what had become of the world over the last ten millennia. Vanguard noted that more Reborn continued to wake each day, though there were still no more than a few dozen in total.

## The gate guardian

The party continued southeast into a strange valley where the weather and biome changed every hour. At the far end stood an ancient temple of dark stone, thousands of years old, housing the gate to [[Limbo]]. Inside, they fought the gate's guardian: a faceless being that could manipulate initiative order and everyone's position on the battlefield.
