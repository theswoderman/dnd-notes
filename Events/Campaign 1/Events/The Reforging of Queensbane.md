# The Reforging of [[Queensbane]]

With all five pieces in hand, the sword assembled itself. A ghostly chorus of voices spoke to [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] and named it: **[[Queensbane]]**.

The gem in the hilt then projected a vision of [[Heaven's Altar]], sending [[The Sons of Thunder]] toward the mountain.

It would later turn out the blade was still far from its full strength. See [[Sardior's Revelation]].
