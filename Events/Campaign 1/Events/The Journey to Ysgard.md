# The Journey to [[Ysgard]]

On the eve of departure, [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] used Dream to contact [[Kyron Delinor]] and begged him to return to righteousness. Kyron said it was impossible; what he does is for the greater good.

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] then played his one card again: [[Vedonia]] and [[Bryn]] would not approve. Kyron warned that if anyone mentioned his family again, he would *"butcher every man, woman, and child"* in [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s [[Whitehall]] mansion.

**[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] called the bluff** — insulting him further, calling him a failure as a husband and father. Kyron ended the dream. See [[The Harbinger's Reprisal]].

The party left through the gate beneath the [[Tulari Hot Springs]], where [[Nugasu]] warned them of danger and let them pass.

They arrived on a frigid peak above a valley of chimney smoke, under a sky of nebulae and **moving stars** — constellations slowly acting out battles from tales of old. [[Braegor the Brutal]] escorted them to [[Kadigus]], who told them living trespassers must leave or die — until a voice from the crowd argued otherwise, and [[Soraya Hawthorne]] stepped out.

She vouched for them. Later that night [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] told her exactly why [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] had hunted her down. Without a word she slammed his face into the table and walked out.
