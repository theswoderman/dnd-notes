# The Voyage to the [[Ruby Palace]]

In [[Luxaria]], [[Sal]]'s contact found them a captain — [[Stella Enrulon]], with her first mate [[Bürd]] — and they picked up [[Pentaius Mendivroth]], who had mapped every recent sighting of the [[Ruby Palace]] and calculated where it should be. A two-month journey.

Aboard the [[Blackwater Jewel]] they survived:

- an ambush by two Giff warships and a titanic asteroid spider in the **Shattered Strand**
- the **Great Nothing**, an empty stretch of space, where Stella began behaving strangely — pale and wide-eyed
- the **[[Silvered Nebulas]]**, where a crewman turned into a feral wereraven and Stella transformed into a lunar dragon and attacked. [[Bürd]] talked [[The Sons of Thunder]] into saving her; [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] dispelled her madness curse.
- deep uncharted space, and a **living spellstorm** — a sentient embodiment of pure wild magic — which went supernova as it died.

The party woke in a crater of gray stone. The ship lay in hundreds of pieces. Stella had shielded them with her wings. Everyone survived but [[Pentaius Mendivroth]].

On the horizon: the [[Ruby Palace]].
