# [[Xinar]] and [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] Revealed

[[The Sons of Thunder]] caught [[Princess Kara]]'s kidnapper in [[Xinar]] — a tabaxi woman named [[Umbra]] — who led them to the princess and, for the first time, gave the black-armored figure a name: **[[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]]**.

He killed her for it, fought the party, and fled on the back of a giant necrotic dragon. As he went they glimpsed his face: elven, pale, black-haired, eyes without pupils.

Kara was recovered. The party collected their reward and set out for [[Loradris]] to find out who [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] was.
