# The Ascent of [[Heaven's Altar]]

Stopping in [[Deepforge]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] fought an ice titan and frost wyverns out of a gold-colored rift, then found [[King Fargrim]] held captive by a disguised [[Mohabi]], and this time killed the devil.

With the blessing of the [[Council of Mystics]] they entered the [[Valley of First Life]], where [[Kovir]] showed each of them a vision of their own greatest flaw and questioned their resolve. After days of climbing they beat him in combat at the summit and earned the right to use the astral gate.

It opened onto a portal of pure white energy, and they stepped through.
