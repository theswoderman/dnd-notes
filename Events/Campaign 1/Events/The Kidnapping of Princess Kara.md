# The Kidnapping of [[Princess Kara]]

At [[Princess Kara]]'s birthday celebration (where [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] ran into his father and brother, who insisted he was out of his mind for claiming he'd found his mother chained in a dungeon) the princess was taken.

[[King Berrigan]] offered 50,000 gold. Evidence pointed to [[Xinar]], in [[Isembard]], and [[The Sons of Thunder]] rode.

[[Io]] left for home and the war effort along the way.
