# [[Sardior]]'s Revelation

The single most important conversation of the campaign. Delivered by an **echo** of [[Sardior]] — a shard of his mind left behind because he knew [[The Sons of Thunder]] were coming.

## The origin of everything
Before creation there was the void, and in it [[Aon]], [[Aon|the One]]. He made a universe from thought, grew bored of it, erased it, and began again — an unknowable number of times, each closer to perfection.

He bore two children from his own essence, a daughter and a son, each as powerful as he was, and set them to take turns creating worlds for him to judge.

Then he stopped at a world made by his son [[Aelor]]. Its inhabitants were **not bound to their maker's will** — free, diverse, individual — and over them stood stewards responsible for some domain of life or order. Those stewards are the gods. That world is this one.

[[Aon]] found perfection in a knowingly imperfect thing, and chose to pass the mantle to [[Aelor]].

## The murder
In the instant before the mantle passed, the daughter struck, destroying her father as he looked upon his son. Brother and sister fought in the stars above the world. She dealt [[Aelor]] his mortal wound; with his dying breath he wounded her and cast her into a desolate lower plane.

She broke through it, and through it again, and again — and the layers she left in her wake are [[The Abyss]]. Falling, wounded, she spawned demons out of pure spite.

**That daughter is [[Aeralath]].** Not the first demon. The mother of all demons, the daughter of creation itself, the being who killed god.

## Why the war exists
Everything except demons carries a spark of [[Aelor]]. **The more that die, the stronger she becomes.** That is the whole purpose of the war [[Dolghast]] is waging and of the sacrifices at [[Silnothas]].

## The tasks that follow
- [[Queensbane]] is not at full strength. It must be **attuned to six planes**: [[Ysgard]], [[The Beastlands]], [[Mechanus]], [[Limbo]], the [[Nine Hells of Baator]], and [[The Abyss]].
- [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s patron "[[Zargon the Immortal|Sina'Draiel]]" is [[Zargon the Immortal]]. Not even [[Asmodeus]] could kill him — only lock him in his own horn. To destroy him, the horn must be cast into the flames of the deepest pit at the bottom of the ninth layer of hell.
- [[Sardior]] gave [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] the [[Rose of Asmodeus]] in trade.

## The aftermath
[[Sardior]] had the [[Zodar]] send the party and crew home by Wish and then destroy the palace — the knowledge inside was too dangerous to leave unguarded. They landed in [[Emerald Bay]].

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] then honored his bargain with [[Skrezzit]] by summoning it thousands of feet in the air and letting it fall.
