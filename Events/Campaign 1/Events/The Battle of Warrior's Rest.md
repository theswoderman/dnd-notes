# The Battle of Warrior's Rest

[[Sorith Menyar-Ag]] teleported [[The Sons of Thunder]] across [[Limbo]] to [[Warrior's Rest]], the realm of [[The Red Lady]]. They arrived on a cliff above a wide valley that held dozens of battles between two warring forces, one dressed in red and the other in black. Beside them on the cliff stood a pristine red tower.

## The Red Lady's tower

The tower was hung with tapestries of famous battles from throughout time and lined with priceless weapons and artifacts of war. After climbing many stories the party reached the throne room, where [[The Red Lady]] herself received them.

She knew why they had come to [[Limbo]]: [[Queensbane]] needed a powerful magical source to attune to. She suggested the [[Tower of Fate]] at the southern edge of the valley. The tower sat right on the front line of her eternal war with [[Garagos]], so the party would first have to help push his forces back, far enough that her army could hold the tower while [[The Sons of Thunder]] explored it.

## The charge

The party were given pegasi and led the charge. They plunged into the forces of [[Garagos]] and laid waste to hundreds if not thousands of his soldiers, driving them back from the [[Tower of Fate]] and wiping out many of his war machines and siege weapons.

Among the enemy was [[Krintak Cinderheart]], living out his afterlife in the black army. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] killed him a second time, and [[Krintak Cinderheart|Krintak]] was reborn as a warrior in the service of [[The Red Lady]].

## Lokar'rohg

With many of his forces routed, [[Garagos]] sent out his mightiest soldier: his own son, the empyrean [[Lokar'rohg]], who stood nearly 200 feet tall. He nearly killed [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]], [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] before the party brought him down.

## The gods of war

While the party were still recovering from their wounds, [[Garagos]] himself came forward to avenge his son. With their backs against the wall and on death's door, they readied themselves for what would surely be their doom.

[[Garagos]] charged and brought his axe down on [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]. A red flash of light descended from the heavens and the blow was stopped by a shield: [[The Red Lady]] had come to fight her longtime rival and defend her champion. She healed the party's wounds and sent them on, saying she could handle this fight. As they made for the black [[Tower of Fate]], each strike between the two gods of war let out a roaring clap of thunder behind them.
