# The King's Tournament

On the road to [[Whitehall]] [[The Sons of Thunder]] met other heroes bound for the same tournament, and encountered a second rift, emerald green, along with talking rhinos and a small celestial elephant-like creature. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s hilt sealed this one too.

They won the tournament, earning fame, powerful rewards, and an audience with [[King Berrigan]], who sent them to [[Vallenhein]] to find out why the Alnorian ambassador had been expelled and the border sealed.

[[Krintak Cinderheart]] first fought the party here.
