# The Mines of [[Silverwell]]

To buy information about the kingdom's closure, [[The Sons of Thunder]] investigated the disappearance of gold and silver from the mines beneath [[Silverwell]].

The [[Duergar of Silverwell]] were offering it to [[Mammon]], Lord of Greed, in exchange for devilish power to retake the city. The party freed a dragonborn prisoner, [[Arjan Taneresh]], stopped the duergar, killed a fallen devilish paladin, and closed an orange rift leading to the [[Nine Hells of Baator]].

[[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] found the second fragment of the sword here, and saw [[Blubberpike]] burning.
