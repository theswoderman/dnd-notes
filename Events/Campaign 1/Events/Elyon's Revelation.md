# Elyon's Revelation

At the [[Arcanus Sanctum]], [[Elyon Ivasil]] explained what [[Kyron Delinor]] had become and why, and then, prompted by [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s account of the [[Lost Nezerai City]], told them the whole history of [[The Sundering]].

## The conclusion the party drew
The timing of the colored rifts and Kyron's reappearance cannot be coincidence. The planes are reconverging. The rifts are temporary overlaps, and sometimes creatures are dragged through. If [[The Black Crown]] worships demons, they must have something planned for when the planes fully align.

See [[The Planar Realignment]].

Elyon also granted the party access to teleportation circles worldwide, which changed how they travel for the rest of the campaign.
