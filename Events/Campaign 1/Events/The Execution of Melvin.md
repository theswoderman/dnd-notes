# The Execution of [[Melvin]]

Before he was beheaded in [[Whitehall]] for his crimes as leader of the [[Ashes of Kemnan]], [[Melvin]] gave up the piece that reframed the entire war.

The mass murder was never only about breaking enemy morale, and the people kidnapped and shipped to [[Silnothas]] were not slaves or hostages. They would **"ensure [[The Sons of Thunder]] got started on time,"** as foretold by **"the child of the stars."**

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] worked it out: the prisoners are being sacrificed to [[Aeralath]], to strengthen her for the moment the outer planes align and the demons come.

His body was destroyed afterwards, Clone spell or no.
