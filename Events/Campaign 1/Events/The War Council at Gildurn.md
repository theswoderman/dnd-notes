# The War Council at Gildurn

Returned from [[Limbo]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] regrouped at a war council in [[Gildurn]], the capital of [[Rorinden]]. Representatives and rulers of [[Rorinden]], [[Alnoria]], [[Vallenhein]], [[Loradris]], [[Stonehold]] and [[Ornuth]] were present, and together they formed a plan to end [[The Great War|the war]] with [[Dolghast]] once and for all.

## The plan

The combined armies of the allies would amass at [[Stonehold]] and march north into [[Dolghast]].

While the armies gathered, [[The Sons of Thunder]] would infiltrate the coastal city of Cape Durk, where [[Yosco]] and the rest of Ghara J'boi's friends and crew were being held captive. With luck [[Yosco]] could tell them something of [[Dolghast]]'s plans, which the party could use to aid the marching armies.

From there the party would head further north and enter the [[Nine Hells of Baator|Nine Hells]] through the planar gate within Hellfrost Crater. In Hell they meant to do two things at once: attune [[Queensbane]], and free [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s soul from [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]]. That meant reaching [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]]'s throne on the lowest level of Hell, now held by [[Asmodeus]], speaking with [[Asmodeus]], and finding some way to destroy the [[Horn of Zargon|horn]] and kill [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]] for good. They hoped the [[Rose of Asmodeus]] would buy them an audience.

## The flaw

Entering through the planar gate would leave a journey of weeks, if not months, down to the ninth level, and [[The Planar Realignment|the convergence of the planes]] was roughly two months away. They needed a more direct path to the lower levels.

## Qua Sorii

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] remembered [[Qua Sorii]], a mercane the party had met in the [[Astral Sea]], and called on him using the business card he had left them. Within moments he appeared.

Hearing their situation, [[Qua Sorii]] said he knew of a way he could potentially get them into the seventh layer of Hell, but he needed time to set it up. The party agreed that in the meantime they would break [[Yosco]] out of prison and help get the armies of [[Alnoria]] and its allies well into [[Dolghast]].
