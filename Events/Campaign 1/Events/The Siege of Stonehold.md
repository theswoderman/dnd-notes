# The Siege of [[Stonehold]]

[[The Sons of Thunder]] reached [[Stonehold]] ahead of the Dolghasti army, ran into [[Soraya Hawthorne]] again, and were recruited by [[Magus Trollbeater]] to hold the western wall.

The enemy was led by [[General Vadahm]], apparently human and alive for over four hundred years. Terralite cannons kept the siege towers back until they reached the wall. Then a horn sounded, the Dolghasti pulled away, and [[Kyron Delinor]] stepped forward and obliterated the main gate with Earthquake and Meteor Swarm.

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] crushed a gem taken from an evil dragonborn wizard to borrow a black dragon's power, the act that let [[Sharess the Black]] into him, and the party held the courtyard against war mammoths until sheer numbers forced a retreat under the mountain.

→ [[The Death of Soraya]]

Afterwards, the [[Council of Moradin]] sent them through a hidden mining tunnel to flank the army and kill Vadahm. They did, and [[Queen Nocticula]] puppeted his corpse to tell them the war was far from over.
