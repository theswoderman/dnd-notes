# Nigel's Resurrection

Before leaving for [[Dolghast]] after [[The War Council at Gildurn]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] agreed it would be wise to tell their loved ones that they might be gone a long time, and might not return. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] stayed in [[Whitehall]] to see his parents. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] took [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] and [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] to his home town of [[Blubberpike]] to speak with his own parents, something he had not done since the death of his brother [[Nigel]], before the campaign began.

The reunion was bittersweet. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] asked his parents whether they would want [[Nigel]] returned to life. They were skeptical, but agreed, and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] cast True Resurrection.

[[Nigel]] immediately punched [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] in the nuts. Then he forgave him, and awkwardly but happily greeted his family.

After a brief reunion the four returned to [[Whitehall]] for one final good night's rest before the journey ahead.
