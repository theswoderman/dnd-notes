# The Lair of [[Yskarat]]

[[The Sons of Thunder]] chartered a ship out of [[Zardoba]] and dived for the third fragment of the sword, fighting past a kraken and into the lair of the mad beholder [[Yskarat]]. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] was nearly killed.

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] took the fragment and saw [[Nostra]] overrun by the undead. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] took an [[Orb of Scrying]], which became one of the party's most valuable tools.
