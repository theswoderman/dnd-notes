# The Titan of [[Stonehold]]

At the Fountain of Remembrance in the [[Pools of Memorium]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] fought off the ghosts of drowned heroes, drank, and fell asleep.

They woke in a dark cave surrounded by dwarven miners as a colossal earth titan burst out of the far wall. The equipment bore the mark of [[Stonehold]] — the same city they had defended from [[Dolghast]], and the city [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]] and his leonin warriors saved from a titan nearly four hundred years ago.

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] realized what was happening: they were **reliving Atrios's battle**.

[[Soraya Hawthorne]] fought alongside them, showing off her afterlife-honed skills, and [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]'s Meteor Swarm landed the killing blow. As the titan came apart it released a burst of energy, and [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] held the sword aloft to harness it — finally attuning [[Queensbane]] to [[Ysgard]].

**One plane down. Five to go.**
