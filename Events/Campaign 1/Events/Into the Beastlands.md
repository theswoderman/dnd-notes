# Into the Beastlands

After downtime and resupply, [[The Sons of Thunder]] teleported to [[Raiorma]]'s village on the [[Unclaimed Isle]], a few hours from the planar gate to [[The Beastlands]].

Nearing the gate, nothing larger than insects and frogs lived within two miles of it, and the picked-clean bones of a colossal dinosaur lay nearby. Using [[Queensbane]] as a radar, they went beneath a stone temple and destroyed a hive of mutated wasps that had been feeding on the gate's energy and growing in size and intelligence.

Through the portal: a jungle darker and hotter than the [[Unclaimed Isle]], thick with fog. Intelligent velociraptors ambushed them; a tyrannosaurus couple offered to share a meal. At the edge of a plateau thousands of feet above open grassland they saw a tree tall enough to pierce the clouds, and a lone sign pointing toward the only settlement in the plane.

They floated down and flew on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] in gold dragon form, spotting an immense flying castle and avoiding a roc with a 500-foot wingspan, twice the size of the one in the [[Neziri Desert]].

## Signpost and the Guardians

At [[Signpost]], on the edge of Krigala overlooking Brux, they met [[Myreth]], an agathion. While they discussed why they had come, [[Signpost]] was attacked by large tree-like Guardian creatures. [[Myreth]] sat down and performed a ritual to calm the spirits that had caused them to go rampant, and the party protected [[Myreth]] until it was done. [[Myreth]] explained that the Guardians were rampant because [[The Beastlands]] was being desecrated by something powerful, and directed them to [[Jungle Sovereign]] in Brux.

## The Wild Hunt

In Brux the party found signs of poachers: the [[Wild Hunt]], a fey hunting party led by the Erdking. [[Zusa Manefell]], [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s late mother, appeared as an ally and helped them kill two of the Hunt's lieutenants, Rotgut and Ojiro, before leading them to [[Jungle Sovereign]].

There, [[Bassus]] the Ape King convened a gathering. [[Nobanian]], god of lions and great cats, had been slain. [[Lurue]], goddess of unicorns and intelligent beasts, was being hunted. The [[Wild Hunt]] had made camp in Evergreen Sanctuary, the heart of the Great Tree Yggdrasil, and threatened to burn it and destroy the entire plane if anyone interfered.

A mighty forest spirit named Zaithyron, who took the form of an elk, projected the party into Evergreen Sanctuary (similar to Astral Projection) to meet the Erdking directly. The Erdking noticed [[Razor's Grace]] on [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s belt and [[Darrius]] among them, and proposed a deal: [[The Sons of Thunder]] had 24 hours to evade, hide from, or fight off the Hunt. They could go anywhere in [[The Beastlands]] but could not flee to other planes. If they survived, the Hunt would leave forever. If caught, they had to surrender [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s crystal blade [[Razor's Grace]] and [[Darrius]], and let the Hunt continue. They accepted.

## The 24-hour hunt

The party sought allies. On the way to [[Ursis]], god of werebears, they were ambushed by [[Malivorna]], daughter of Baba Yaga and one of the Hunt's four lieutenants. She summoned two demons and a shadow clone of [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]. They killed her and recovered the [[Demonomicon of Malivorna]].

With [[Ursis]], his werebears, and three Otter River Deities (Lord Vermilion Stoneriver, Lady Azura Shellstream, Lord Viridian Greencreek), they staged a trap in a narrow canyon. Two lieutenants led forces into the ambush: [[Prascilia]], a winged elf archer, and [[Vilerrus]] the Mad Dog with his spectral wolves. The River Deities flooded the canyon and the werebears attacked. [[Vilerrus]] was forced to flee. [[Prascilia]] proved nearly impossible to stop from the air, and it took [[Ursis]] himself to draw her away while the party escaped to Karasuthra, the third layer.

In Karasuthra, a realm of perpetual moonlit night, [[Gaidus]] the Laughing Shadow, a corrupted satyr, attacked from invisibility. The party cornered and killed him, taking his blade [[Hope's Demise]].

## The Erdking

They reached [[Lurue]]'s realm. She healed them to full strength and offered to stand with them. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] used the spell Temple of the Gods to create a fortress. Inside, they waited.

The Erdking came alone, obliterating the door and marching in. Ranged attacks and spells hit him but he was seemingly unstoppable, and his gaze steadily crippled anyone who looked at him. Weakened by solid planning, some luck, and [[Lurue]]'s aid, he was forced to take his true form: [[Cerrunos]], god of hunting and savagery. His presence blew out all lights in the temple, even divine light. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]], [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] were badly wounded, but [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] struck the killing blow with True Telekinesis.

## After the hunt

The surviving members of the [[Wild Hunt]] honored the deal and left [[The Beastlands]] in peace. [[Lurue]] gave a great deal of her divine power to attune [[Queensbane]] to [[The Beastlands]], bringing the sword to two of six attunements.

The Erdking's death broke a curse. [[Darrius]], the black cat who had traveled with the party since near Mudbend, was revealed to be [[Darrius|Abrovand]], Archfey of Trickery and Illusions. He had been a friend of the Erdking, but fifty years ago he stole [[Razor's Grace]] and lured the Erdking to the material plane to keep him from [[The Beastlands]]. He gave the sword to Johan Malcovitch, [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s grandfather, who claimed he won it in a duel. The Erdking caught Abrovand and cursed him into cat form. Freed now, Abrovand swore to always aid [[The Sons of Thunder]] and promised to return to Whitehall regularly as Darrius to check on the orphans.

The party returned to the material plane.
