# Into Mechanus

After [[Into the Beastlands|the Beastlands]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] set out for [[Mechanus]], the Clockwork Nirvana. The gate lay in [[Gudjag Canyon]], home of the red orcs that now served [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]. Near the gate they met a withered priestess of [[Gruumsh]], the sole surviving loyalist among the red orcs, who led them to the ruins where the gate sat, openly hoping her champion would kill them.

The "Eye of Gruumsh" guarding the ruins turned out to be [[Astynom Alpha 009]], a Marut: an ultra-powerful lawful construct loyal only to [[Primus]]. It demanded the party surrender their weapons, including [[Queensbane]], before entering [[Mechanus]]. They refused. In the fight the Marut knocked [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] unconscious and teleported them both away.

## Regulus

The rest of the party stepped through the gate into Regulus, the Modron supercity, home to over 350 million modrons. Rather than fight, they submitted to interplanar customs, a process that took over a full day of paperwork and interviews. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] was being interrogated in a Modron prison but was eventually released.

The three remaining Secundi, the modrons who report directly to [[Primus]], summoned the party. Each of the four Secundi governed an aspect of reality: space, matter, energy, and time. The fourth, [[Chronus]], lord of time, had gone rogue and taken a quarter of the Modron army with him. [[Chronus]] believed he could turn back time to ensure [[Aeralath]]'s defeat, but the Secundi warned that such a thing would tear reality apart. They asked the party to find and slay him.

## The Magnodrone

The party boarded a Magnodrone, a living train-modron that travels along a rail, bound for the Salvatorium, a factory at the edge of Modron-controlled space. Rogue modrons ambushed them on the train and the party fought from car to car, sometimes on the roof, keeping it from derailing.

The Salvatorium was abandoned, as if attacked. In the foreman's office they found a notebook describing Lifeblood: a silvery-white magical fluid that all modrons are born of. Powerful but unstable, exposure to unstable elements could bring objects to life. The foreman hypothesized that similar magic could let organic beings transcend their material bodies and pass into constructed forms without necromancy, but noted this strictly violated something called the "Divine Edict of Forbidden Magics."

They fought a massive rogue forge modron and liquid metal oozes animated by spilled Lifeblood, then pushed through into the Wastes of Gravegear.

## The Wastes of Gravegear

The Wastes sat atop massive, sometimes continent-sized, ever-turning gears: a universal junkyard holding remnants of technologies and civilizations from across time and many planes. The party fought goblinoid junk raiders from [[Acheron]] aboard airships, giant scrap monsters possessed by ghosts, and a junkyard madness that made [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] want to walk around naked.

A storm of tiny metal shards drove them underground, where they found an unlikely group of stranded beings: a Balor who liked to bake, an aberration made of living wood, a fallen angel escaped from Modron prison, a man with the head of a donkey who always wore an unsettling smile, and [[Qadus Ifnir]], an elderly human wizard. [[Qadus Ifnir|Qadus]] turned out to be the previous owner of the party's mansion in [[Whitehall]], who had accidentally disappeared through a portal to [[Mechanus]] during a basement experiment. The stranded villagers would not let the party leave. After a fight, the party moved on.

## The Formians

At a fork in the road the party chose formian territory over myconid. Formians are highly intelligent, psionically gifted ant-beings ranging from three to seven feet tall. The party explained their purpose and were brought before [[Chitinousa|Queen Regent Chitinousa]], the Hive Mother, at the bottom of an inverted tower-like termite mound hanging thousands of feet below a giant gear.

[[Chitinousa]] gave her blessing and ordered her warriors to clear a path through [[Chronus]]'s rogue modrons. She also sent [[Theh Kha]], one of her finest warriors, to fight alongside the party.

## The visions of Chronus

Inside [[Chronus]]'s realm of twisted time, the party saw visions of alternate lives they would have led if they had made different choices:

- [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] never challenged [[Kuzo Clawborn|Kuzo]]. He stayed with the pride, saw Kuzo slain by [[Krintak Cinderheart|Krintak]] during the dragonborn invasion, tried to fight back, and was killed. The leonin were enslaved.
- [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] declined his brother's duel. Consumed by resentment, his rapid arcane rise caught the attention of [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]], who made him an apprentice and led him to aid [[Aeralath]]'s return.
- [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] joined [[Melvin]] in the shipping industry. Desensitized to escalating crimes, he became a full member of the [[Ashes of Kemnan|Ashen Saints]], turned on [[Melvin]] when his conscience forced him to, and was killed by him.
- [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] stayed in the woods of Loradris. On a trip with Prim he stumbled into the [[Eldergleam]], was captured by fiends, corrupted, and turned against his own people.
- [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] did not flee the night he found his mother and the castle burned. He joined [[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]], became a full vampire, helped [[Dolghast]] conquer the world, and knelt to [[Orcus]].
- [[Theh Kha]] waited for the traditional age to begin his warrior's trial. Wounded in a battle with a rival hive before it came, he lived out his days as a mundane attendant to the larvae.

## Chronus

The party found [[Chronus]] in the ruins of a crumbling clock tower. His time manipulation made the fight extremely difficult. [[Theh Kha]] provided the edge they needed, but [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] was nearly killed when [[Chronus]] struck him with a 9th-level Time Ravage, aging him to within thirty days of dying of old age. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] absorbed [[Chronus]]'s power into [[Queensbane]], attuning the blade to [[Mechanus]] and bringing it to three of six attunements.

## After

Back in Regulus, [[Primus]] himself received the party. Eliminating [[Chronus]] had destroyed all rogue modrons who reported to him; [[Primus]] would remake them from the pools of Lifeblood energy he waded through. He enhanced [[Theh Kha]]'s wrist-mounted crossbow to artifact-level power. [[Theh Kha]], tired of hive life, asked to come to the material plane and see the world. [[Primus]] reminded the party that his modrons would fight alongside mortal kind when the demons came, and sent them back through the gate at [[Gudjag Canyon]], where [[Theh Kha]] set off on his own.
