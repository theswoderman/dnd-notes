# The Gnoll Contract

While [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] gathered allies for the coming fight against his father, the rest of [[The Sons of Thunder]] took a job investigating wagon attacks west of [[Whitehall]], and met the half-orc ranger [[Io]].

The attackers were gnolls, who offered to leave the area if the party killed a rogue gnoll that had gone mad and was killing its own. They did — and learned from a survivor that the creature had been exposed to a **bright yellow rift** that opened in thin air, which drove it mad and gave it unnatural strength and magic.

Returning for payment, they were ambushed. [[The Ravager]] killed [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] turned into a giant eagle and flew the body to [[Whitehall]], where [[Kehl Brokenhorn]] revived him.
