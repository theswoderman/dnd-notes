# The Siege of the Ashen Hideout

An assassin attacked [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] in his sleep, wearing a necklace of a fist wreathed in flame, the mark of the [[Ashes of Kemnan]]. A contact of [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s led [[The Sons of Thunder]] into an ambush, and a body there carried a note: *"Three dragon egg omelets - Borly's Fresh-baked Goods"*.

Heading north to [[Rothefell]] to escape the assassins, the party killed the two giants that had eaten [[Io]]'s family a decade earlier. In the city, [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] found Borly's bakery, used the password, and were promptly thrown into a pit with an acidic ooze.

The party regrouped and laid siege to the hideout. They found [[Hariett Hinkapot]] among the Saints, who told them the order had been kidnapping people worldwide and shipping them somewhere. In the subterranean docks they fought [[Callus Halterstock]] and three elite [[Dolghast]] soldiers, and recovered ledgers proving the prisoners were bound for [[Silnothas]].
