# [[The Consumed]] and the [[Vallenhein]] Civil War

Having prevented an invasion, [[The Sons of Thunder]] earned an audience with Prince [[King Fargrim|Fargrim]], who told them his father had gone hunting on the [[Isle of Eismir]] two months earlier, returned alone without his guards, and had been acting strangely ever since.

On Eismir they pieced it together: [[King Oberin Steeleborn]] and his men had killed a hag. Her three sisters killed the guards and summoned [[The Consumed]], an ancient aberration. It ate the king, took his shape, and sat on the throne of [[Silverwell]] — sowing misery and slowly consuming more dwarves and replacing them.

When the party told Fargrim, civil war broke out. They fought to the throne room and killed [[the Consumed]] alongside the prince — but not before it killed [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]].

Fargrim was crowned, and swore that when war came, [[Vallenhein]] would come to [[Alnoria]]'s aid.
