# The Attack on [[Mudbend]]

Where it all began. A group of strangers happened to be in [[Mudbend]] for the annual fall harvest festival — pig wrestling, merriment — when goblins assaulted the village and stole the winter food stores.

The strangers fought them off together, and were asked to follow the raiders into the swamps and recover the food. Without it, the village would not survive the winter. [[Soraya Hawthorne]] guided them through.
