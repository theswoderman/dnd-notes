# Into Limbo

[[The Sons of Thunder]] slew the gate guardian and stepped through the portal into [[Limbo]]. The plane was pure chaos: a maelstrom of constantly changing energy, air exploding into fire, solidifying into diamond, melting into water, erupting into lightning, with no sense of direction in any direction. The only sign of habitation was a stone placard at the edge of their floating island that read, "Breathe the fire; Walk the air; Drink the earth; Warm your hands at the water."

## Journey through chaos

After fighting off praying mantis creatures that could vanish into clouds of sand, the party began moving through the chaos. Each of them had to will themselves forward, with those of higher wisdom or intelligence moving more quickly. They had not gone far before being attacked by a living crescent moon with a malevolent face. That fight was interrupted when the party, the creature, and everything around them were swallowed by a massive worm-like beast flying through the chaos.

## Kra-Thoon

Trapped in the creature's belly, nearly spent, the party fought giant parasites while standing on safe patches amid a sea of stomach acid. They survived but were completely exhausted. While resting inside, they were violently vomited out and rescued by a familiar face: [[Ensira]], the Gith'zerai woman they had freed from a [[Githyanki Empire|Githyanki]] ship on the way to the [[Golden Plains]]. [[Ensira]] explained the rescue was a stroke of luck. Her group happened to be nearby and "tickled" the belly of the worm, which the Gith'zerai called Kra-Thoon, the Wind of Change, forcing it to vomit.

## Lu'Un'Dai

[[Ensira]] brought the party to the Gith'zerai capital city, a floating megacity called Lu'Un'Dai, so densely packed with millions of Gith that the party could barely move. Inside the city, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] was approached by a Gith man named Shath, a general in the Gith army and a secret disciple of [[The Red Lady]]. Shath said [[The Red Lady]] wanted to speak with [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in her tower within Warrior's Rest, a distant pocket realm within [[Limbo]]. The only way to reach it would require the Gith'zerai "God-King" to send them there.
