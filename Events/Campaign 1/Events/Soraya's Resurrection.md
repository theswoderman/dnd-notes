# Soraya's Resurrection

Back at the village where they first entered [[Ysgard]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] convinced [[Soraya Hawthorne]] to let them bring her back.

She agreed on one condition: **kill [[Kyron Delinor]]**, and do not let him claim the lives of any more innocent people.

After heartfelt goodbyes with her late father [[Lucas]], the party planeshifted to [[Mudbend]] — where the whole story began — left Soraya to settle some things, and teleported home to [[Whitehall]].
