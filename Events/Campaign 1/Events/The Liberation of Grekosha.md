# The Liberation of [[Grekosha]]

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s crusade to clear his homeland of vampires, undertaken before [[The Sons of Thunder]] could face [[Golzokroth]].

1. **[[Baristos]]**, the city occupied by undead under [[Onias Kane]], Lord of Blood, with the queen held in her own palace. With the [[Blood Raven]] and the locals, the party broke in, killed Onias, and freed [[Queen Marislova Domeshka]], who named [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Raven Lord]], High General of [[Grekosha]].
2. **The crypts**, the army marched east and stalled when the men could gain no nourishment from food or drink. Haunting singing led to an ancient graveyard from the old war with the stone giants, and [[Lorilai Xalthina]], Mistress of Rot, whose druidic song caused it. Destroyed.
3. **[[Bloodraven Keep]]**, hundreds of Red Orcs under [[Ogzuuk Torah'kor]] held [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s old castle. The party's magic tore through the defenses; Ogzuuk emerged and offered honorable single combat to spare his men. He lost, and [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] took his soldiers as promised.

Allies mustered: [[King Fargrim]] of [[Vallenhein]] and [[Diana Zethros]] of the [[Rorinden Empire]].
