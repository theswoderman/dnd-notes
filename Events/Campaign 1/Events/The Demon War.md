# The Demon War

**When:** 1016–1017 A.P.
**Part of:** Campaign 1
**Outcome:** [[Aeralath]] defeated; the A.P. count closes and the [[Estrana|Dawning Era]] begins

## What happened
[[Aeralath]] set her demons loose on the world to destroy it, so that she could create a new universe. [[The Sons of Thunder]] defeated her, because this is D&D and level 20 is built different.

Her defeat is celebrated every year since as [[The Dawning]].

## The war before the war
The demons arrived at the end of a conflict that had already drawn in nearly every nation of [[Anendal]] and [[Panrelta]] against [[Dolghast]] and [[Ornuth]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] ended that one too, and [[Aeralath]]'s army arrived immediately afterward.

[[Reed]] treats the two as one war. In his words: *"It wasn't called the Great War but yeah basically every nation was at war against [[Dolghast]] + [[Ornuth]]."* The name "the Great War" comes from [[Grant]]'s [[Prelude for Thalrik Lastfeast]], which recorded it incorrectly; the conflict's real name is unrecorded. Because of how closely it ran into the demons' arrival, Reed says most people looking back would consider them the same war, which is why it is folded into this note rather than kept separately.

**Against [[Dolghast]] and [[Ornuth]]:** [[Alnoria]] · [[Rorinden]] · [[Loradris]] · [[Vallenhein]] · [[Nilstraad]] (technically neutral until [[Dolghast]] forces attacked [[Stonehold]]) · [[Grekosha]], eventually (see below).

[[Grekosha]] was never on the "bad side." [[Reed]] has corrected this: *"Grekosha hadn't declared a side. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s dad wanted the queen dead so he could take power and join [[Dolghast]]. Once they took him out, Grekosha joined the good guys."* The nation stayed undeclared; it was [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s father who wanted to throw in with [[Dolghast]], and killing [[Queen Marislova Domeshka]] was how he meant to do it. [[Prelude for Thalrik Lastfeast]] lists Grekosha as an enemy nation, which is wrong.
