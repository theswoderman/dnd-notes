# The Demon War

**When:** 1016–1017 A.P.
**Part of:** Campaign 1
**Outcome:** [[Aeralath]] defeated; the A.P. count closes and the [[Estrana|Dawning Era]] begins

## What happened
[[Aeralath]] unleashed her demons on the world, intending to destroy it and create a new universe in its place. [[The Sons of Thunder]] defeated her. Her defeat is commemorated annually as [[The Dawning]].

The demon invasion came at the tail end of a broader continental war that had drawn nearly every nation of [[Anendal]] and [[Panrelta]] against [[Dolghast]] and [[Ornuth]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] ended that conflict too, and [[Aeralath]]'s army appeared immediately afterward. The two wars ran so close together that most people consider them the same conflict, and this note treats them that way.

## The continental war

**Against [[Dolghast]] and [[Ornuth]]:** [[Alnoria]] · [[Rorinden]] · [[Loradris]] · [[Vallenhein]] · [[Nilstraad]] (technically neutral until [[Dolghast]] forces attacked [[Stonehold]]) · [[Grekosha]], eventually.

[[Grekosha]] stayed undeclared for most of the war. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s father wanted to seize power by killing [[Queen Marislova Domeshka]] and join [[Dolghast]]. Once he was taken out, [[Grekosha]] joined the coalition. [[Reed]] has confirmed this: *"Grekosha hadn't declared a side. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s dad wanted the queen dead so he could take power and join [[Dolghast]]. Once they took him out, Grekosha joined the good guys."*

The continental war has no recorded name. [[Reed]]: *"It wasn't called the Great War but yeah basically every nation was at war against [[Dolghast]] + [[Ornuth]]."* [[Grant]]'s [[Prelude for Thalrik Lastfeast]] calls it "the Great War" and lists [[Grekosha]] as an enemy nation; both details are incorrect.
