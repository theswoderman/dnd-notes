# The Voyage to [[Vallenhein]]

A three-week sea crossing, weresharks fought off along the way, landing at [[Hödlhafen]].

The caravan toward [[Silverwell]] was ambushed by orcs, which is how [[The Sons of Thunder]] met [[Myrn Lim]] and [[Badger Barkbeard]]. On foot afterwards, they had to get past a frost giant and a white dragon to escape the frozen valley.
