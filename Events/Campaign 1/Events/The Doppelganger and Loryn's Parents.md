# The Doppelganger and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s Parents

Word reached [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] that his brother [[Melvin]] had been murdered. In [[Zardoba]] the body showed several circular punctures; a Speak with Dead spell had [[Melvin]] explain that he had met [[The Shrike]] to hire him to kill [[Queen Marislova Domeshka]].

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] suspected his own father's hand. An empty Grekoshan throne would suit him. But why [[the Shrike]] had impaled [[Melvin]] on the antler rack of a deer trophy made no sense.

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] returned home to find his parents bound in their rooms. Opening the door triggered a magical trap that killed them instantly and set the house ablaze.

Revived, they were terrified of him: *[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]* had tied them up and set the trap. A doppelganger was toying with him, and had promised that after their deaths it would "take care of his *real* family."
