# The [[Unclaimed Isle]] and Anathimus

On the [[Unclaimed Isle]], [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] used the [[Orb of Scrying]] to spy on [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] and overheard [[Queen Nocticula]] — learning that [[Dolghast]] would attack [[Stonehold]] in about a week.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] freed [[Hariett Hinkapot]] from lizardfolk, who turned out to serve [[Raiorma]], an ancient amethyst dragon in disguise. She asked them to find her missing mate.

On the way they met [[Mohabi]], who traded information — including that [[Melvin]] and [[Callus Halterstock]] knew each other very well — then caged [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], stole his Ioun Stone and fled to [[The Abyss]].

They infiltrated the [[Yuan-Ti]] city disguised as slavers, fought through the ziggurat, and faced [[Anathimus the Abhorrent]], who was channelling the captive dragon's psychic energy to lift the ziggurat's base into the sky and tear open a pink rift to summon a marilith.

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] grabbed the **fourth fragment** as the floor gave way, and saw [[Zardoba]] in ruins with part of the city fallen into the sea.
