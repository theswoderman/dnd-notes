# [[Jiren's Rock]] and the Duel with Krintak

Traveling to check on [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s family in the [[Golden Plains]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] found leonin tribes wiped out by dragonborn under [[Krintak Cinderheart]], an old tournament rival and ally of [[Dimble Flintbatter]].

They freed [[Kelara]] from [[Jiren's Rock]], learned she was pregnant by [[Kuzo Clawborn]], and with her help reached the [[Tulari Hot Springs]], where the hostages were held. There they found [[Nugasu]] guarding a dormant interplanar gate.

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] duelled Krintak, and instead of killing him, talked him into setting aside his hatred to face the coming demonic invasion. Krintak tentatively agreed. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] was given [[Lundhaidr]] and named Dreadclaw.

*(The truce did not survive [[Bathoz's Mindscape|what came next]].)*
