# [[Av'orel]]'s Tomb

En route to the [[Unclaimed Isle]], [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s ship was magically trapped against a strange island holding a single ancient building — its stone doors guarded by valkyries and the ghosts of Oathsworn knights.

Fighting angels was harrowing and deeply unsettling. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] opened the sealed doors with the strange symbol on his palm. Inside waited [[Av'orel]], a planetar loyal to [[Asmodeus]] — whom it called "the True Light", with no knowledge of him being a devil at all.

## The moon temple
With [[Av'orel]] dead, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] touched the altar and found himself in a temple with a glass ceiling, looking up at [[Estrana]] — he was on the **moon**. He was drawn to a black, bone-like spear and, on grasping it, could not let go.

The voice of [[Zargon the Immortal|Sina'Draiel]] spoke. The spear burned the flesh from his arm to the elbow, then dematerialised and took the appearance of the arm it had destroyed.

That spear is the [[Horn of Zargon]]. The island sank behind them.
