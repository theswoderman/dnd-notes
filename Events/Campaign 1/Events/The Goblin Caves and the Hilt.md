# The Goblin Caves and the Hilt

Beneath the goblin village, [[The Sons of Thunder]] killed the goblin leader and watched a shadow demon rise out of its corpse. They killed that too, pressed deeper, and found the first pink rift to another plane.

Something drew [[Bathoz Manefell]] to a pile of bones, where he found an ancient handle and hilt of dark iron. Touching it, he saw his homeland in flames and heard a vast bestial roar in the distance. When the vision ended, a beam of energy leapt from the hilt and slammed the rift shut.

Nobody understood what had happened for a very long time. See [[Queensbane]] and [[The Planar Realignment]].
