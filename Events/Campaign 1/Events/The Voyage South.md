# The Voyage South

After [[The Slaying of Minthur|slaying Minthur]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] turned to their most daunting task yet: reaching [[Limbo]] for [[Queensbane]]'s fourth attunement. The interplanar gate was thousands of miles away on an uncharted continent far to the south, beyond any known sea route.

The party found a captain willing to make the voyage: Jabari Osidai, a daring sailor whose ship, the Dragon's Demise, was docked in Ciaveses in western [[Ornuth]]. Jabari agreed to the job for a sizeable sum of gold and one condition: the party had to help a sea elf bard named Ghara J'boi, a friend who had once saved his life. Ghara's price was that after the journey the party would break his old crew out of a prison in Cape Durk, a town in eastern [[Dolghast]].

## Scrying on Yosco

Not long into the multi-week voyage, [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] used his scrying orb to scry on [[Yosco]], whom the party deduced was the "child of the stars" that [[Melvin]] had mentioned. The scrying revealed that [[Dolghast]]'s forces planned something drastic against [[Alnoria]] very soon. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] messaged [[King Berrigan]], who responded by ordering [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] to find [[Yosco]] and lead Alnorian forces into [[Dolghast]] to end the war as soon as they returned from [[Limbo]].

## Jakel's memory

During the weeks of downtime, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] began to dwell on the night he fled his father's castle: finding his mother chained in the dungeon, the fire that seemed to guide him below. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] offered to cast a ninth-level Modify Memory, letting [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] relive the night in vivid detail. One thing stood out: the guiding flames had a distinctly pink color, the same shade as the portals to [[The Abyss]] the party had seen. The implication that [[Aeralath]] may have caused the fire, and may have been steering other events in their lives, troubled the entire party.

## The hidden continent

The voyage was not without danger. The party fought off bipedal crustacean and jellyfish creatures that ambushed the ship at night, as well as two ships of cursed undead pirates captained by a pair of legendary twin pirate brothers. Eventually the Dragon's Demise reached a wall of magical fog that could not be sailed through. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] felt compelled to raise [[Queensbane]], and a beam of light shot from the blade and dispelled the wall, revealing an entire continent hidden behind it.
