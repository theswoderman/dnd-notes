# The Sundering

**When:** Roughly 10,000 years ago
**Told by:** [[Elyon Ivasil]], and depicted in [[The Mural and the Gem]]

## What happened
For eons every outer plane was linked to [[Estrana]] by interplanar gates, and beings came and went at will. Then an immense demonic horde poured out of the gateway to [[The Abyss]] in an assault unparalleled to this day.

It was bizarre for two reasons. Demon lords despise each other and would never normally combine their armies. And attacking the world of mortals drew the ire of both the Heavens and the [[Nine Hells of Baator]], since mortal souls sustain gods and devils alike.

Heaven and Hell together drove the demons back — and that was the point. The bloodshed was an offering, a ritual to summon [[Aeralath]] from the deepest level of [[the Abyss]].

Unable to defeat her, the leaders of all the planes drew magical essence from every plane into a single blade: [[Queensbane]]. Five mortal heroes drove [[Aeralath]] and her forces back into [[the Abyss]], then used the sword to **sever the ties binding the outer planes to the world**, setting them adrift in the [[Astral Sea]] and rendering every gate inert. With the planes separated, [[Aeralath|the Demon Queen]] was sealed away.

## The cost
The gods' ability to intervene in the world was drastically weakened — as it remains today. Knowledge of the gates and of the Sundering itself was lost. The blade, too powerful to wield and too powerful to destroy, was shattered into five pieces and scattered across a neutral plane where no power of the outer planes could reach it.

## Why it matters now
The planes are reconverging. See [[The Planar Realignment]].

- Only [[Dryzmir Aletall]] and [[Vhal Nimzur]] survived the fight with [[Aeralath]]
