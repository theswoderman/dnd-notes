# The Slaying of [[Golzokroth]]

**Preparation.** [[Mathias Ohstal]] explained hybrid dragons in [[Whitehall]]. [[Elyon Ivasil]] revealed that [[Golzokroth]] had killed several of his closest friends forty years ago, including [[Mehea Talth]]'s father, lent [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] his Robes of the Archmagi, and pointed them at [[Golzokroth|the Blood Tyrant]]'s sister.

**The bargain.** [[Rendibril]], a purple dragon near [[Dragon's Pass]], would help in exchange for his entire hoard (minus the sword fragment) on condition [[The Sons of Thunder]] butcher the cult that had grown tiresome around her. That cult was led by [[Kru'dahs]].

**The kill.** In the [[Blackwaste]], the party cut through fire salamanders to the heart of a volcano built in the ruins of a fire giant city. [[Mehea Talth]], [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]], [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] were all on death's door before the party maneuvered [[Golzokroth]] into focusing on his sister. Both dragons died.

**The prize.** A hoard worth over a million gold, an artifact-level hat for [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]], and the fifth and final fragment. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] grasped it and saw [[Heaven's Altar]] sundered and falling onto a burning forest.
