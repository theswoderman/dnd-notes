# Knighthood and the Haunted Manor

On their return to [[Whitehall]], [[King Berrigan]] knighted every member of [[The Sons of Thunder]] and granted them property in the city.

The property was said to be haunted. It was, in fact, a few children hiding in an abandoned house, the beginning of the household that [[Harold]] would come to care for, and that [[Kyron Delinor]] would later attack.
