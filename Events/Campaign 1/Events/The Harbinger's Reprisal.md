# [[Kyron Delinor|The Harbinger]]'s Reprisal

While [[The Sons of Thunder]] was in [[Ysgard]], [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] came to their mansion in the night.

He came through the door, cut down [[Harold]] and his sister [[Patrice]], and cast a spell that killed [[Morbach]] instantly and raised his corpse as undead. Then he left, leaving the undead [[Morbach]] to finish off the orphans.

[[Sebastion]] happened to be home that night rather than at the magic university. He set the undead [[Morbach]] ablaze with his own spell, almost certainly saving every child in the house, including [[Morbach]]'s daughter [[Naori]]. None of the others even heard the fighting. He got guards to watch the house until the party returned days later, and was sitting on the front steps when they did.

He blamed [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and the party outright: they may be saving the world, but they leave a wake of destruction behind them.

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], who had been warned twice and pushed anyway (see [[The Journey to Ysgard]]), vowed to be more cautious, resurrected [[Harold]] and [[Patrice]], and paid 25,000 gold in diamonds for the True Resurrection needed to bring back [[Morbach]], who had already been raised as undead. He apologized to all four.

[[Harold]] wanted to leave. He could not bring himself to abandon the children. He did ask for a raise.
