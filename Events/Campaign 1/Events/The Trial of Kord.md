# The Trial of [[Kord]]

Guided by [[Dryzmir Aletall]], [[Gwendolin Thundersong]] and [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]] toward the [[Pools of Memorium]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] first chose to test themselves against [[Kord]] himself.

Atop the lone icy peak at the heart of [[Asgard]] they made a valiant stand against the avatar of the God of Bravery and Storms, and were no match for his strength. They fell one by one — and woke on a riverbank below the mountain without a scratch.
