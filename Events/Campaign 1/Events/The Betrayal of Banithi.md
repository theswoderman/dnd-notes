# The Betrayal of [[Captain Banithi|Banithi]]

Propelling themselves through the [[Astral Sea]] by thought, [[The Sons of Thunder]] were picked up by a spelljammer under [[Captain Banithi]], who offered passage to [[Luxaria]].

They woke surrounded by Githyanki warriors and dozens of warships. [[Captain Banithi|Banithi]] had sold them to the [[Githyanki Empire]] as slaves, and they were taken to [[Tu'Narath]].

Stripped of their gear, they fought five waves of monsters in the combat pits for [[Vlaakith the Eighth]]. The last, a Star Spawn Voidling, used its dying breath to say that *"the progenitors of this world would return to their rightful place over all things"* before [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] cut it in two.

**The escape:** [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] summoned the [[Horn of Zargon|spear]] the guards had failed to
take, used it to call the devil [[Skrezzit]], and broke out [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] and the undead pirate [[Sal]]. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] bargained with [[Skrezzit]] to distract the guards, swearing to summon it to the material plane later or forfeit [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s soul. They killed [[The Commissar]] for their gear and teleported out on a wood splinter [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] had taken from [[Captain Banithi|Banithi]]'s ship, then took the ship itself.
