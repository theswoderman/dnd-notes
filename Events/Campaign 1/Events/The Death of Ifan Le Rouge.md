# The Death of [[Ifan Le Rouge]]

After the feast at [[Bloodraven Keep]], [[Casimir]] appeared in [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s old room to plead their father's case: [[Ifan Le Rouge]] did not want to hurt anyone, only to secure a future where his family would not be hunted for what they are. He warned [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] not to fight a being powerful enough to make a warlock of his own son. [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] would not be moved.

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] projected an image of himself to propose a meeting without violence. [[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]] knew it was a poor decision (he would be out in the open) but accepted anyway, out of love for his son.

They met in the woods outside [[Nostra]]: [[The Sons of Thunder]], [[Diana Zethros]] and [[King Fargrim]] on one side; [[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]], [[Casimir]] and [[Chessa]] on the other.

[[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]] explained the thing [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] had never known. His mother had been kept away for years because she was dying from having carried two vampire children, and the only cure was vampirism, which she refused. He had locked her away to keep her alive until she would accept.

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] attacked anyway, knowing he would have to kill his mother.

[[Diana Zethros]] turned [[Chessa]] to ash. [[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]] flew into a rage, killed [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and very nearly killed [[Diana Zethros]], and was overpowered, impaled through the heart on [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s spear, and burned to ash by the setting sun.

[[Casimir]] was locked, unconscious, in the [[Cube of Carrying]].

**Aftermath:** for the first time in years [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] had no purpose. He walked into the snowy woods and screamed. [[Diana Zethros]] found him. He renounced his oath of vengeance for the Oath of the Red Knight, and became the Scarlet Raven.
