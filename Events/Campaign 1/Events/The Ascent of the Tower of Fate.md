# The Ascent of the Tower of Fate

With [[The Red Lady]] holding off [[Garagos]] outside, [[The Sons of Thunder]] entered the black [[Tower of Fate]] in [[Warrior's Rest]] to find a source of magic strong enough to attune [[Queensbane]] to [[Limbo]]. The tower had once belonged to [[Tyche]], goddess of fate, and many of her old tricks still guarded it. Its current ruler was unknown to the party until they reached the top.

## The wagers

The tower's guardians included golems with reality-manipulating powers. Two of its trials forced the party to wager things of value to sway fate in their favor: the greater and more meaningful the wager, the more fate favored their efforts within the tower. Value was measured by what the thing meant to the one offering it, not by its monetary worth. The wagers were made silently, so no one knew what their allies were gambling.

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] offered what the tower judged the most valuable thing of all: his own kingdom. He was a prince at the time, so the stake was in effect his inheritance. It secured the party's passage further up. He received a magical boon of fate, though he was unsure what it did.

## The laboratory

Higher up was the personal laboratory of the tower's ruler. It held several cursed items, among them the crown of the plague king and the fabled Book of Vile Darkness, along with written correspondence between the ruler and [[Ygorl]], the Slaad Lord of entropy and death.

## The Watcher

The party then met [[The Watcher]], a spider-like woman who oversees the thread of fate. She showed them that their threads in the great loom of the universe were all being pulled together by a single greater pink thread. To the party this could only mean that [[Aeralath]] had been trying to influence their fates from the beginning.

## The surrender

The penultimate floor did not ask for a wager. To reach the top, something of utmost value had to be surrendered outright. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] offered his soul, and the tower accepted. His soul had belonged to [[Torm]]. It now belonged to the tower's owner.

## The Archlich of Fate

On a black throne on the top floor sat [[Vhal Nimzur]], the banished [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] wizard who had fought [[Aeralath]] alongside the rest of [[The Five Heroes]] and lived. He had become a lich, the Archlich who ruled Fate. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] had handed his soul to a lich, and many of his divine powers became powers of undeath.

The fight was brutal. [[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]] could manipulate fate and chance to his own benefit, escaping impossible outcomes and forcing the party to fail against his spellpower. He cast a 10th-level spell that nearly killed all of them, but [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] healed the party and no one died. [[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]] was slain.

## Aftermath

The party attuned [[Queensbane]] to [[Limbo]] within the Vault of Fate, the fourth of six attunements. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] recovered an ancient artifact rifle that [[Primus]] had built as a gift to [[Tyche]] many eons before.

[[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]] was not finished. A lich endures as long as its phylactery does, and so does his claim on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s soul. The party scried on his spirit and found the phylactery worn on a chain around the neck of [[Ygorl]], in his realm at the heart of [[Limbo]]'s chaos. They resolved to return to [[Limbo]] for it once [[Aeralath]] was dealt with.

Back at her tower, [[The Red Lady]] hailed them for their bravery and strength and sent them home to [[Estrana]], where the war with [[Dolghast]] was about to reach its climax.
