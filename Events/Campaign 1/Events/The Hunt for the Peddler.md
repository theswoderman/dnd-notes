# The Hunt for [[the Peddler]]

The strangers decided to stay together and headed for [[Runswick]], chasing [[The Peddler]], a murderer [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] had been tracking across [[Alnoria]].

After a detour into the [[Tomb of Evendor]], they cornered him at the mayor's house. He escaped through a hidden exit and was run down outside town, where they found him talking to a figure in black armor with an antlered helm. That figure killed him with a single spell, summoned two demons, and fled.

On [[the Peddler]]'s back: a manifesto declaring that death and destruction are an offering to "their matron", and a black skull crowned with antlers.

The group took a name after that fight, the [[The Sons of Thunder|Sons of Thunder]], and set out for [[Whitehall]] to collect their reward.
