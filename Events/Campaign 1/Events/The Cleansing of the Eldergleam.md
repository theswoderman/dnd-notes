# The Cleansing of the Eldergleam

The price of passage to the [[Arcanus Sanctum]] was ridding the [[Eldergleam Tree]] of whatever was corrupting it: yugoloths out of [[Hades]], led by a Sibriex.

The demon killed [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], [[Mehea Talth]], Prim, Ray'el and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] — until a necklace that had mysteriously appeared on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] after a dream encounter with a divine being shattered and revived them all in a burst of holy light.

Before leaving, the elves gave up the names of [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]]'s dead family: [[Vedonia]] and [[Bryn]].
