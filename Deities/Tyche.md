# Tyche

**Domain:** Fate

The goddess of fate, who split into two goddesses: Tymora, goddess of good luck, and Beshaba, goddess of bad luck.

The [[Tower of Fate]] in [[Warrior's Rest]] belonged to her before the split, and many of her old tricks still guard it. Many eons ago [[Primus]] built an artifact rifle as a gift to her. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] recovered it after [[The Ascent of the Tower of Fate]].
