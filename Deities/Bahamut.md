# Bahamut

The Platinum Dragon, god of metallic dragons, wisdom and mercy. His [[Platinum Palace]] on [[Mount Celestia]] is built of solid platinum and diamond, guarded by seven metallic greatwyrms.

He, [[Torm]] and [[Tyr]] summoned [[The Sons of Thunder]] to explain that [[Aeralath]] would return within months and that [[Queensbane]] was not yet strong enough to face her.
