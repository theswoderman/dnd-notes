# Sylvanus

A nature god. His worshipper [[Hiram Oakbrow]] gave [[The Good Ones]] the [[Sylvan Parcel]], a bundle of spell components, at some point before session 6. Whether the god intended the gift or [[Hiram Oakbrow|Hiram]] gave it on his own is unrecorded.

## Connections
- [[Hiram Oakbrow]], his worshipper, who handed the parcel over
- [[Sylvan Parcel]], the gift
- [[The Good Ones]], the recipients
