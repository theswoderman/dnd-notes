# Sylvanus

A nature god. His worshipper [[Hiram Oakbrow]] gave [[The Good Ones]] the [[Sylvan Parcel]], a bundle of spell components, at some point before session 6.

When [[Falstad Firebeard]] named Sylvanus during the fight at [[The Frozen Riverbank]], the Beast of [[Malar]] scoffed at the name.

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
