# Torm

God of honor and truth, and the god [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] follows. They met face to face in the [[Platinum Palace]], where Torm gave [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] his own personal sword, the [[Hand of Truth]].

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s soul belonged to Torm until he surrendered it in the [[Tower of Fate]], where it passed to the lich [[Vhal Nimzur]]. See [[The Ascent of the Tower of Fate]].

## Ten years later
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] is sworn to Torm by both oath and pact, and Torm has named him the Golden Pilgrim. See [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]].

[[Bane]] is a major enemy of Torm, and one of "the Dead Three". A buried soldier of [[Bane]] has surfaced in the [[Sage Thorn Forest]], where [[Torin Turen]] has gone alone. See [[Torin's Flight into the Sagethorn]].

Neither Torm nor [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] foresaw the coming of [[Bane]]'s champion, [[Oraecheon Blackspire]]. Since being revived beneath [[The Abbey]], [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] no longer feels Torm's presence, though he can still cast spells. See [[The Destruction of the Abbey]] and [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
