# Kord

God of storms and bravery, whose home is [[Asgard]], along with [[The Einherjar|the einherjar]].

Before leaving [[Ysgard]], [[The Sons of Thunder]] chose to test themselves against his avatar atop the lone icy peak at the heart of [[Asgard]]. They made a valiant stand and were utterly outmatched, falling one by one — and woke on a riverbank below the mountain without a scratch on them.
