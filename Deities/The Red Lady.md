# The Red Lady

The deity of the Rorindenian martial faith, whose greatest temple is the [[House of War]] in [[Gildurn]]. [[Diana Zethros]] and, later, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] both swore her Oath of the Red Knight. [[Hayden Trask]] is the highest-ranking member of her church.

## Warrior's Rest

Her realm is [[Warrior's Rest]], a pocket realm within [[Limbo]] where those who live for battle go when they die. Her red-clad army fights an eternal war there against the forces of her sworn enemy, [[Garagos]]. She is perhaps the single greatest warrior in existence, and has secret disciples as far afield as the Gith'zerai army, such as the general [[Shath]].

She summoned [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] to her tower there, directed [[The Sons of Thunder]] to the [[Tower of Fate]] as a place to attune [[Queensbane]], and gave them pegasi to lead her army's charge. When [[Garagos]] came for the party himself, she descended in a flash of red light, stopped his axe with her shield above [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], healed the party, and fought him while they went on. Afterward she sent them home to [[Estrana]]. See [[The Battle of Warrior's Rest]] and [[The Ascent of the Tower of Fate]].
