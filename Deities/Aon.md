# Aon

**Title:** The One

Before creation, before life and death, before light and dark, before time, there was the void, and within it, Aon.

He created a universe from thought alone, both for company and to test his own creative power, and in that first span of existence experienced what mortals would call godhood. He then grew bored of it, erased existence, and began again. He repeated this an unknowable number of times, each iteration closer to the perfection he was chasing.

Eventually he bore two children from his own essence, each as powerful as he was (a daughter, [[Aeralath]], and a son, [[Aelor]]) and set them to take turns creating worlds for him to judge.

He was killed by his daughter in the instant before he passed the mantle of creation to his son.

Source: [[Sardior's Revelation]]
