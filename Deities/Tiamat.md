# Tiamat

A god who has been trying to assert power since the balance of powers among the gods was thrown off by [[The Sons of Thunder]]' defeat of [[Aeralath]].

[[Zarisk]], who sits on a council in [[Bahamut]]'s inner circle, has been tasked with finding fifty heroes to hold her back. He thinks [[The Good Ones]] should be among them. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
