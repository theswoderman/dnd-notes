# Primus

**Domain:** Law
**Status:** Alive

Supreme God of Law and Lord over all Modrons, ruler of the Clockwork Nirvana of [[Mechanus]]. He presided over the trial of [[Asmodeus]], declared him innocent, and created the [[Rose of Asmodeus]] as a standing warning.

When [[The Sons of Thunder]] killed the rogue Secundi [[Chronus]], [[Primus]] personally received the party. All rogue modrons who had followed [[Chronus]] were destroyed along with him, and [[Primus]] would remake them from the pools of Lifeblood energy he waded through. He enhanced [[Theh Kha]]'s crossbow to artifact-level power, and promised that his modrons would fight alongside mortal kind when the demons came. See [[Into Mechanus]].
