# Lolth

A deity, known in classic lore as the patron of the [[The Drow|drow]].

[[Falstad Firebeard]]'s inspection of a throne made of drow bones in the tunnels beneath [[The Abbey]] indicated that the place had been a stronghold of Lolth. See [[The Tunnels Beneath the Abbey]].
