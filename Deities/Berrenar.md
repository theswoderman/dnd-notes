# Berrenar

The goddess served by [[Falstad Firebeard]], styled Lady Berrenar.

[[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]] lost his divine connection beneath [[The Abbey]]. During the healing ritual at [[Temple Trueheart]] she spoke with him in a vision. She was impressed with how he had grown, but suggested that he draw his power from another god, naming [[Illmater]] and leaving the choice to him. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
