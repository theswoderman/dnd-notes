# Asmodeus

Greatest of the archdevils and High Lord of the [[Nine Hells of Baator]]. No one knows his origin, though some say he was once a powerful celestial who fell.

Two discoveries complicate the standard account:

- [[Av'orel]], a planetar sealed in a tomb for millennia, served Asmodeus as **"the True Light"** and had no knowledge of him being a devil or evil at all.
- Asmodeus did not create hell. He and the devils *took* it from the Baatorians — beings like [[Zargon the Immortal]], whom he could not kill and instead imprisoned inside his own horn on the moon.

At a trial before [[Primus]], the hosts of [[Mount Celestia]] sought to hold him accountable for corrupting mortal souls. He argued that his work was necessary: he offers mortals a choice rather than compelling them, and the souls he converts into devils form the armies that hold the demons of [[The Abyss]] at bay in the [[The Blood War|Blood War]]. [[Primus]] declared him innocent — but gave him the [[Rose of Asmodeus]] as a reminder to stay lawful.

He later traded the Rose to [[Sardior]] for information, disliking the strings attached.
