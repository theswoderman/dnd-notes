# Annam

The all-father of the giants.

His daughter Mordraka bore the empyrean [[Lokar'rohg]] to [[Garagos]], god of bloodlust and war.

Known almost entirely through the memories held in the [[Giant Memory Gemstones]], which [[The Good Ones]] experienced as dreams beneath [[The Garden of Dreams]].

## What the dreams showed
- Stone giants at a mountaintop temple overlooking an expansive desert, chanting his name. A master craftsman was honored by being turned to stone as part of a ritual ([[Falstad Firebeard]]'s dream)
- Dozens of stone giants at the [[Hand of Annam]] making an offering to the night sky as shooting stars fell, gathering [[Skystones]] and thanking Annam for the gift of that rare crafting material ([[Thalrik Lastfeast]]'s dream)
