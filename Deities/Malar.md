# Malar

**Also known as:** the Beastlord

God of hunters, and god of lycanthropes. [[The Collector]] was a self-declared servant of Malar and a member of the [[Black Blood Pack]], a lycanthrope pack in his service.

## The grail
Questioned after death, [[The Collector]] said Malar had sent him to "collect the bestial trophies so we may drink from [[The Black Grail|the black grail]]". The cartload of animal parts was a rite's worth of components, not stock.

Malar's pack and [[Bane]]'s followers both want the grail and are not allies. In [[The Collector]]'s account Malar said of them: "their steel would sharpen our talons, but they seek the grail for themselves. They are not of our pack." Both mean to bring it to [[The Abbey]], near [[The Tree of Pain]]. See [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]].

The werewolves and werebears at [[The Frozen Riverbank]] are his. The enormous panther leading them was a Beast of Malar, a servant from [[The Beastlands]]. It scoffed when [[Falstad Firebeard]] named [[Sylvanus]] during the fight. The party chased and killed it near [[The Tree of Pain]]. Near the tree, [[Bane]]'s undead soldiers routed another pack of Malar's werewolves without much difficulty. See [[The Battle at the Bridge]].

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
