# Garagos

**Also called:** the Reaver
**Domain:** Bloodlust, bloodshed, fury and war

The sworn enemy of [[The Red Lady]]. His black-clad army fights hers without end in the valley of [[Warrior's Rest]], and soldiers who die on her side are reborn in his. He fights with an axe.

His son is the empyrean [[Lokar'rohg]], born to Mordraka, one of the daughters of [[Annam]], god of giants.

When [[The Sons of Thunder]] broke his lines around the [[Tower of Fate]] and defeated [[Lokar'rohg]], [[Garagos]] came for them himself. His blow at [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] was stopped by the shield of [[The Red Lady]], and the two gods of war fought while the party went on into the tower. See [[The Battle of Warrior's Rest]].
