# Illmater

A god. Served by [[Torin Turen]], high priest and leader of [[The Bettermans Guild]].

He is a god of borne suffering. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] refused further healing after lesser restoration on the grounds that Illmater would want him to bear it.

The temple of Illmater in the [[Sage Thorn Forest]] is [[The Abbey]], now also called the Crypt of Hate. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] asked [[The Good Ones]] to take him there, then rode out alone before they could, saying Illmater had bid him in a dream to catch up with a party that had not actually left. See [[Torin's Flight into the Sagethorn]].

A note found at [[The Abbey]] in [[Illmater]]'s red cloths, written by followers who had tried to maintain the temple, describes an empty darkness that eats faith and tortures dreams. The Ilmatari abandoned the grounds, writing that "Our Martyred Father does not walk here." See [[The Crypt of Hate]].

## Torin's failing connection
[[Torin Turen|Torin]] can still reach Illmater when he actively tries. When he is not actively communing, he feels another presence. [[Bane]]'s soldiers turn out to be buried in the same forest that holds Illmater's temple.

## What Torin told the party
Call to Illmater when you are at your lowest.
