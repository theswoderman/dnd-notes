# Orcus

Demon lord. In [[Chronus]]'s vision of an alternate timeline, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] joined his father [[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]], became a full vampire, and helped [[Dolghast]] conquer the world. That vision ended with him kneeling to [[Orcus]]. See [[Into Mechanus]].
