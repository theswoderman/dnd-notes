# Aelor

Son of [[Aon]], and the creator of this universe: the material realm of [[Estrana]] and its outer and elemental planes.

His world was the one [[Aon]] finally judged worthy, and for a specific reason: its inhabitants were not bound to their maker's will. They were free, diverse, individual. Over them he set stewards tied to their existence and fate, each responsible for some domain of life, order or thought, what mortals now call gods.

[[Aon]] found perfection in a knowingly imperfect thing, and chose to hand him the mantle. His sister [[Aeralath]] killed their father before it could happen, then fought and mortally wounded Aelor. With his dying breath he wounded her in return and cast her down.

Every living thing except demons carries a spark of [[Aelor]]. This is why every death strengthens [[Aeralath]], and why [[Queensbane]], forged from the essence of Aelor's first divine breath, can harm her.
