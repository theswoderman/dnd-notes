# The Pools of Memorium

A mountain in the lower reaches of [[Ysgard]] containing the **Fountain of Remembrance**. [[Dryzmir Aletall]], [[Gwendolin Thundersong]] and [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]] recommended it as a magical source strong enough to attune [[Queensbane]].

Ghostly spirits of drowned heroes — those who lost themselves in the pools — attacked [[The Sons of Thunder]] at the fountain. After drinking, each of them fell asleep and woke inside [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]]'s four-hundred-year-old battle with the earth titan beneath [[Stonehold]].
