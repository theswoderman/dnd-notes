# Limbo

A realm of pure chaos and wild magic: a maelstrom of constantly changing energy with no fixed direction, crossed by willing oneself forward. It is the home of [[Ensira]]'s people, the Gith'zerai, whose capital is the floating megacity of [[Lu'Un'Dai]], ruled by [[Sorith Menyar-Ag|God-King Sorith Menyar-Ag]].

One of the six planes [[Queensbane]] must be attuned to, and the fourth to be completed: the sword was attuned in the Vault of Fate at the top of the [[Tower of Fate]].

## Places within
- [[Lu'Un'Dai]], the Gith'zerai capital
- [[Warrior's Rest]], the pocket realm of [[The Red Lady]], which holds the [[Tower of Fate]]
- The realm of [[Ygorl]], Slaad Lord of entropy, at the heart of the chaos

The gate to [[Limbo]] from the material plane is on the hidden southern continent. See [[The Hidden Continent]] and [[Into Limbo]].
