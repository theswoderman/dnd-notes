# The Abyss

A plane of infinite layers — and those layers exist because of a fall.

When [[Aelor]] cast his dying sister down, she struck a desolate lower plane and broke through it, and through it again, and again, deeper and lower, leaving a new layer in her wake each time. That plane is the Abyss, and the falling was [[Aeralath]]'s.

As she fell, wounded but not dead, she spawned the demons out of hatred for her father and her brother's creation.

- [[Mohabi]] hails from the 500th layer
- One of the six planes [[Queensbane]] must be attuned to
- Demon lords like Orcus and Demogorgon never cooperate — which is what makes the united banner on [[The Mural and the Gem]] so alarming
