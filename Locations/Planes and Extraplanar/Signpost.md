# Signpost

The only settlement in [[The Beastlands]]. A dozen wooden buildings with palm-frond roofs, sitting at the edge of Krigala and overlooking Brux.

Where [[The Sons of Thunder]] met [[Myreth]]. Shortly after their arrival, Guardian tree creatures attacked the village. [[Myreth]] calmed them with a ritual while the party held them off. See [[Into the Beastlands]].
