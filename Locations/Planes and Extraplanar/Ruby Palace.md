# The Ruby Palace

[[Sardior]]'s gigantic ruby palace, which shifts between the planes and had been missing for decades. [[Pentaius Mendivroth]] calculated its position from past sightings.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] found it silent and open: the central hall scorched with radiant damage and stained with long-dried gray fluid. Deserted teaching halls, libraries and observatories. The astral elves in the courtyard were mind flayers in illusion.

Midway through, an accidental detour to the Elemental Plane of Air gained the party a djinn ally, [[Mazaïd]], who had visited the palace decades earlier.

**Found here:** the spell True Telekinesis; the *Lexicon Primordia*, which exposed
[[Zargon the Immortal]]; the fact that [[Aeralath]] predates the elder evils; a [[Zodar]]; [[Adomaiah]]; and the echo of [[Sardior]] himself.

Destroyed on [[Sardior]]'s own order because the knowledge inside was too dangerous to leave unguarded. See [[Sardior's Revelation]].
