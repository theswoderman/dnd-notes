# The Silvered Nebulas

A stretch of the [[Astral Sea]] on the route to the [[Ruby Palace]]. One night here a crew member of the [[Blackwater Jewel]] turned into a feral wereraven, and [[Stella Enrulon]]'s curse took hold, transforming her into a lunar dragon.

Beyond the nebulas lay deep uncharted space, and the living spellstorm.
