# The Astral Sea

The space between all planes of existence. Since [[The Sundering]], the outer planes drift here rather than being anchored to [[Estrana]].

[[The Sons of Thunder]] entered it directly through the astral gate on [[Heaven's Altar]], propelling themselves by thought until they were picked up by [[Captain Banithi]]'s spelljammer.

A one-shot set here, a year before [[The Sons of Thunder]] arrived, saw a task force of elves and orcs destroy a researcher's facility and defeat a [[Witchlight Marauder]]. See [[Defeat of the Witchlight Marauder]].
