# Tu'Narath

Capital of the [[Githyanki Empire]], a monstrous city built into the corpse of a six-armed god.

[[Captain Banithi]] sold [[The Sons of Thunder]] here as slaves. Stripped of their gear, they were thrown into the combat pits to entertain [[Vlaakith the Eighth]] and fought five waves of monsters, the last a Star Spawn Voidling which used its dying breath to say that "the progenitors of this world would return to their rightful place over all things."

They escaped via [[Skrezzit]], [[Sal]], and a splinter of wood [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] had taken from [[Captain Banithi|Banithi]]'s ship.
