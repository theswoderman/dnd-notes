# Tower of Fate

**Region:** [[Warrior's Rest]], [[Limbo]]

A black tower at the southern edge of the valley of [[Warrior's Rest]], on the front line between the armies of [[The Red Lady]] and [[Garagos]].

It once belonged to [[Tyche]], goddess of fate, and her old tricks still guard it: golems with reality-manipulating powers, and trials that demand wagers. The more a wager means to the one offering it, the more fate favors the climb. The penultimate floor asks for more than a wager and requires something of utmost value to be surrendered outright.

Its ruler in the time of [[The Sons of Thunder]] was the lich [[Vhal Nimzur]], whose laboratory held cursed items including the crown of the plague king and the Book of Vile Darkness. [[The Watcher]] keeps the loom of fate here. The Vault of Fate within the tower is where [[Queensbane]] was attuned to [[Limbo]].
