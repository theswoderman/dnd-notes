# Warrior's Rest

**Region:** [[Limbo]]

The realm of [[The Red Lady]], a distant pocket realm within [[Limbo]]. It is where those who live for battle go when they die.

Its heart is a wide valley that serves as an eternal battleground between two armies: hers in red, and in black the army of her sworn enemy [[Garagos]]. Soldiers who die on her side are reborn in the opposing army, and the reverse also happens: [[Krintak Cinderheart]] was killed fighting for [[Garagos]] and reborn in the service of [[The Red Lady]].

Her own pristine red tower stands on a cliff above the valley. The black [[Tower of Fate]] stands at the valley's southern edge, right on the front line.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] were sent here by [[Sorith Menyar-Ag]]. See [[The Battle of Warrior's Rest]] and [[The Ascent of the Tower of Fate]].
