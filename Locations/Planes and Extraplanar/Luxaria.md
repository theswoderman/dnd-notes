# Luxaria

The Everlight, a city built around a massive asteroid in the [[Astral Sea]], roughly three times the size of [[Whitehall]] and the largest city [[The Sons of Thunder]] knows of.

Full of eclectic alien life and slightly altered versions of creatures from the material plane. Here they hired [[Stella Enrulon]] and the [[Blackwater Jewel]], and found the guide [[Pentaius Mendivroth]].
