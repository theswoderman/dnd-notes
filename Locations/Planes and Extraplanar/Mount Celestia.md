# Mount Celestia

Heaven, reached through the astral gate atop [[Heaven's Altar]].

[[The Sons of Thunder]] arrived before a city of white marble under an infinite sky of stars, along the shore of an endless sea full of magical life. A trumpet archon met them and carried them by chariot — drawn by unicorn-like celestials — to the [[Platinum Palace]].
