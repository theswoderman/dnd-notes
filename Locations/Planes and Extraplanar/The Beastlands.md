# The Beastlands

A wild, feral plane of oppressive jungle and open grassland, reached through a gate beneath a stone temple on the [[Unclaimed Isle]]. Everything here grows roughly twice the size of its material-plane counterpart.

**Layers:**
- Krigala, perpetually locked at midday. [[Signpost]], the only settlement in the entire plane, sits at its edge
- Brux, a constant sunset under a bright orange-pink sky. Home to [[Jungle Sovereign]], the animal city where [[Bassus]] the Ape King held a gathering against the [[Wild Hunt]]
- Karasuthra, perpetual moonlit night. [[Lurue]], goddess of unicorns, dwells here

**Notable locations:**
- [[Signpost]], a dozen wooden buildings with palm-frond roofs
- [[Jungle Sovereign]], the animal city in Brux
- Balador, the realm of [[Ursis]], god of werebears
- Evergreen Sanctuary, the heart of the Great Tree Yggdrasil, held hostage by the [[Wild Hunt]]

The [[Wild Hunt]], led by [[Cerrunos|the Erdking]], invaded this plane, killed [[Nobanian]] (god of lions and great cats), and hunted [[Lurue]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] defeated the Erdking and freed the plane. See [[Into the Beastlands]].

The second of the six planes [[Queensbane]] was attuned to, powered by [[Lurue]]'s divine energy.
