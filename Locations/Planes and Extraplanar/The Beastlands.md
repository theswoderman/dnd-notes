# The Beastlands

A wild, feral plane of oppressive jungle and open grassland, reached through a gate beneath a stone temple on the [[Unclaimed Isle]]. Everything here grows roughly twice the size of its material-plane counterpart.

**Layers seen so far:**
- **Krigala** — perpetually locked at midday
- **Brux** — a constant sunset under a bright orange-pink sky

Approaching the gate on the material side, nothing larger than insects and frogs lived within two miles of it, and the bones of a colossal dinosaur lay picked clean nearby — mutated wasps feeding on the gate's energy.

**Encountered:** intelligent velociraptors, a hospitable tyrannosaurus couple, a roc with a
500-foot wingspan, an immense flying castle, and a tree tall enough to reach the clouds.

**Settlement:** [[Signpost]] — the only one in the entire plane.

The second of the six planes [[Queensbane]] must be attuned to. *(Current arc.)*
