# Lu'Un'Dai

**Region:** [[Limbo]]

The capital of the Gith'zerai: a floating megacity so densely packed with millions of Gith that a visitor can barely move. It holds the palace of [[Sorith Menyar-Ag|God-King Sorith Menyar-Ag]].

[[Ensira]] brought [[The Sons of Thunder]] here after rescuing them from Kra-Thoon. See [[Into Limbo]].
