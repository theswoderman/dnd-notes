# The Platinum Palace

[[Bahamut]]'s palace on [[Mount Celestia]], built of solid platinum and diamond, guarded by seven metallic greatwyrms and staffed by hundreds of angels.

Here [[The Sons of Thunder]] met [[Bahamut]], [[Torm]] and [[Tyr]], each attended by a solar, and were told that [[Aeralath]] would return within months, that [[Queensbane]] was not yet strong enough, and that only [[Sardior]] knew how to reforge it.

See [[Audience at the Platinum Palace]].
