# The Nine Hells of Baator

Domain of [[Asmodeus]] and the devils, though not originally theirs. Before the devils rose to power, hell belonged to the Baatorians, including [[Zargon the Immortal]].

The orange rift closed beneath [[Silverwell]] led here. [[The Sons of Thunder]] must eventually return: the [[Horn of Zargon]] can only be destroyed by casting it into the flames of the deepest pit at the bottom of the ninth layer.

One of the six planes [[Queensbane]] must be attuned to.

Site of the endless [[The Blood War]] against the demons of [[The Abyss]].
