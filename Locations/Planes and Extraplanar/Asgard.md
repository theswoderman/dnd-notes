# Asgard

Home of [[Kord]] and [[The Einherjar]], within [[Ysgard]], reached across a bridge of rainbow light. Like [[Ysgard]] itself, it admits only the worthy: [[The Sons of Thunder]] had to defeat [[Dryzmir Aletall]], [[Gwendolin Thundersong]] and [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]] to enter.

At its heart stands a lone icy peak, where the party challenged [[Kord]]'s avatar and lost badly, waking unharmed on a riverbank below.
