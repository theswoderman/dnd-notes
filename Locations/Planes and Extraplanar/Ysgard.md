# Ysgard

The realm of heroes — reserved for those who died after an honorable life and were carried there on the wings of valkyries. Reached through the gate beneath the [[Tulari Hot Springs]], guarded by [[Nugasu]].

A frozen, beautiful mountain range broken into floating landmasses, some as large as continents. Travel works by intention: will yourself toward a destination and you reach it. The sky is full of nebulae and stars that **move**, the constellations slowly acting out battles and scenes from old tales.

Living trespassers are meant to be expelled or killed. [[The Sons of Thunder]] stayed because [[Soraya Hawthorne]] vouched for them before [[Kadigus]].
