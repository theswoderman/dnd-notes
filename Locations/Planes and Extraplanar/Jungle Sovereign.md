# Jungle Sovereign

An animal city in Brux, the second layer of [[The Beastlands]]. Ruled by [[Bassus]] the Ape King. Powerful beings gathered here to discuss the threat of the [[Wild Hunt]], including the news that [[Nobanian]] had been slain and [[Lurue]] was being hunted. [[Zusa Manefell]] led [[The Sons of Thunder]] here.
