# Heaven's Altar

A holy mountain crowned by an **astral gate** leading to [[Mount Celestia]]. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] saw it sundered and falling onto a burning forest in the vision granted by the final fragment of [[Queensbane]] — and the reassembled sword then projected an image of the mountain, directing [[The Sons of Thunder]] there.

Ascent requires the blessing of the [[Council of Mystics]] and passage through the [[Valley of First Life]].
