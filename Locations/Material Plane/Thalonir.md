# Thalonir

Capital of [[Loradris]]. Its elves gave [[The Sons of Thunder]] [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]]'s history: three centuries as a beloved hero, then the deaths of [[Vedonia]] and [[Bryn]], then madness, sacrifice, imprisonment, and escape fifty years ago.

They also gave up the names of his wife and daughter, which [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] would use as a weapon twice.
