# Hand of Annam

**Region:** [[Dolghast]]

A cold dark field ringed by colossal rock pillars, the hand of [[Annam]]. Seen by [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] in a vision from the [[Giant Memory Gemstones]].

In the vision, dozens of stone giants gathered to make an offering toward the night sky. Shooting stars flew closer and closer overhead, and the giants gathered [[Skystones]] and thanked [[Annam]] for the gift of that rare crafting material.
