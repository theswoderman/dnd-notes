# Hendrel Dyleeri College of Arcane Studies

The arcane university in [[Rothefell]]. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] studied here and was removed; his old professor offered readmission on two conditions: that he share what he learns on his travels, and that he first gain entry to another arcane university and return as a transfer student.

[[Hariett Hinkapot]] was expelled from here, which set her on the road to the [[Ashes of Kemnan]].
