# Vallenhein

The dwarven kingdom west of [[Alnoria]], ruled from [[Silverwell]], now by [[King Fargrim]], formerly by his father [[King Oberin Steeleborn]].

Vallenhein abruptly expelled the Alnorian ambassador and sealed its borders, which is what [[King Berrigan]] originally sent [[The Sons of Thunder]] to investigate. The cause was [[The Consumed]] wearing the dead king's face.

Fargrim has sworn that when war comes, Vallenhein will come to [[Alnoria]]'s aid.
