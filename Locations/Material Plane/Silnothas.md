# Silnothas

Capital of [[Dolghast]] and the destination of every prisoner the [[Ashes of Kemnan]] kidnapped. Ledgers found aboard a ship beneath [[Rothefell]] proved the route, which is what prompted [[King Berrigan]] to declare war.

[[Melvin]] later confirmed the purpose: the prisoners would "ensure [[The Sons of Thunder]] got started on time," as foretold by "the child of the stars."
