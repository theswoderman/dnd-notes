# The Abbey

**Region:** beneath [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|the Laughing Princes constellation]], near [[The Tree of Pain]]
**Disposition:** Hostile, desecrated. Where [[Bane]] and [[Malar]]'s followers both intend to bring [[The Black Grail]]

## Summary
Also called the Crypt of Hate. A ruined structure with a broken steeple rising from the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]], surrounded by desecrated ground. Once a temple to [[Illmater]], now occupied by cultists of [[Bane]].

Named by [[The Collector]]'s corpse as the destination for [[The Black Grail]]: "they will take the grail to the abbey. You will find the abbey near the tree of pain." See [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]].

A map [[The Good Ones]] picked up early in the campaign shows the abbey and the lost temple as the same place, lying beneath [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|the Laughing Princes constellation]]. The party reached it by ordinary travel through the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]] rather than by reading the stars, and got their first sight of it, about twenty minutes off, at the end of [[The Road to the Abbey]].

## The Ilmatari note
Among red cloths bearing [[Illmater]]'s sign, the party found a note from followers of [[Illmater]] who had tried to maintain the temple. Written forty moons after the chapel was raised, it describes an empty darkness in the land that eats faith and tortures the dreams of the waking and the sleeping alike. The Ilmatari abandoned the grounds, writing that "Our Martyred Father does not walk here." See [[The Crypt of Hate]].

## What is inside
[[The Good Ones]] fought through undead hands, undead minotaurs, and cultists to enter. Inside: pegasus feathers, a cloth stained with a black hand, and a locked trapdoor opened by the key from the Fist of Oppression's body (see [[The Battle at the Bridge]]).

Below the trapdoor, more cultists guarded a room containing a map of southern [[Dolghast]] marking the towns [[Brashta]] and [[Brinemire]], plus an X in the Bitter Expanse where [[Khulask Hollow]] once stood, annotated "who beat us to it." The cultists have been abducting people from nearby towns.

[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] detected a secret passage leading to a tunnel. See [[The Crypt of Hate]].
