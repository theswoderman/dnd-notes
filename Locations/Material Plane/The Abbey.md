# The Abbey

**Region:** beneath [[The Laughing Princess Constellation|the Laughing Princess constellation]], near [[The Tree of Pain]]
**Disposition:** Unknown, and where [[Bane]] and [[Malar]]'s followers both intend to bring [[The Black Grail]]

## Summary
Named by [[The Collector]]'s corpse as the destination for [[The Black Grail]]: "they will take the grail to the abbey. You will find the abbey near the tree of pain." See [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]].

A map [[The Good Ones]] picked up early in the campaign shows the abbey and the lost temple as the same place, lying beneath [[The Laughing Princess Constellation|the Laughing Princess constellation]]. Locating it therefore means waiting for a clear night and reading the stars, which is what the party set out to do in [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]].

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
