# The Whisperwood

A dark and dangerous forest north of [[Zardoba]], home to the [[Viera of the Whisperwood|Viera]] — [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s birth people, who banished him as a child.

[[Melvin]] chose it as the testing ground for his poison, contaminating the village's lake and killing the young and elderly. [[The Sons of Thunder]] found him and a rogues' gallery of old enemies in a cave on the far side of the water.

See [[Melvin's Poison and the Whisperwood]].
