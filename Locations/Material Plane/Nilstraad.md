# Nilstraad

The waters off the west coast of [[Zardoba]], where the gem's map placed the third fragment of [[Queensbane]] — fifty miles offshore, in the lair of the beholder [[Yskarat]].

## As a nation
Nilstraad is also one of the nations of [[The Great War]] — technically neutral until [[Dolghast]] forces attacked [[Stonehold]]. Its plains are [[The Shiftweave]], it contains [[Skyfang Lake]], and [[Saltport]] sits on its western coast.
