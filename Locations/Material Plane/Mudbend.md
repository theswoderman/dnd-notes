# Mudbend

A backwoods border village on the western frontier between [[Alnoria]] and the dwarven kingdom of [[Vallenhein]]. Where the campaign began.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] met here as strangers during the annual fall harvest festival — pig wrestling and merriment — until goblins attacked. Fighting them off led to the swamp expedition, the goblin caves, and the hilt of [[Queensbane]].

[[Soraya Hawthorne]] guided them through the swamp. Years later, she was returned to life here.

See [[The Attack on Mudbend]] and [[The Goblin Caves and the Hilt]].
