# Whitehall

Capital city of [[Alnoria]] and [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s permanent base of operations.

The [[The Sons of Thunder|Sons of Thunder]] won the [[The King's Tournament|King's Tournament]] here, were knighted here, and were granted the mansion here that they now share with a household of orphans and their caretaker [[Harold]]. The house was supposedly haunted; it was actually children hiding in an abandoned building.
