# The Eldergleam Tree

A great tree in [[Loradris]] being corrupted by a force of fiendish yugoloths out of [[Hades]]. Cleansing it was the price of passage to the [[Arcanus Sanctum]].

The Sibriex leading the yugoloths killed [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], [[Mehea Talth]], Prim, Ray'el and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], but a necklace that had mysteriously appeared on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] after a dream encounter with a divine being shattered and revived them all.
