# Xinar

City in [[Isembard]], beyond the [[Neziri Desert]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] tracked [[Umbra]] here and recovered [[Princess Kara]], and had their first face-to-face encounter with [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]], whose name they learned only because [[Umbra]] said it before he killed her.

Xinar also has a potion factory whose chemical-buyer manifests later implicated [[Melvin]].
