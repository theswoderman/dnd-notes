# The Lost Nezerai City

A pristine, utopian city untouched by time, buried beneath the [[Neziri Desert]] at the end of a labyrinth of undead. [[Berithos]] identified it as the lost capital of the [[Nezerai Empire]].

A trial at its center opened a portal to a chamber holding **the mural** — a four-panel history of [[The Sundering]] — and the sixteen-sided gem that fits the hilt of [[Queensbane]]. Both were watched over by the sphinx [[Ahmeses]].

See [[The Mural and the Gem]].
