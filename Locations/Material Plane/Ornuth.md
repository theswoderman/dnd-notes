# Ornuth

Nation to the far south, initially aligned with [[Dolghast]]. Prince [[Golimov Brighthorn]] was spared on condition that he persuade Ornuth's rulers to switch sides. He argued he had only ever wanted to preserve Ornuth's independence, and that [[Melvin]]'s planned mass poisoning went too far.

Ornuth did not stay with [[Dolghast]]. After [[Grekosha]] changed sides and [[Dolghast]] failed to take [[Stonehold]], it opted out of the war, and its representatives sat with the allied nations at [[The War Council at Gildurn]].
