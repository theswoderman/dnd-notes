# Mushala

A city southeast of the [[Dreaming Jungle]]. When [[Minthur]] was forced to flee his lair, he turned invisible and flew for [[Mushala]], vowing to kill as many innocents as he could. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] spotted his wingbeats disturbing the trees and the party ran him down before he could reach it. See [[The Slaying of Minthur]].
