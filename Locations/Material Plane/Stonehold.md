# Stonehold

A dwarven city state governed by the [[Council of Moradin]], built into a mountain behind a great western wall.

Nearly four hundred years ago [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]] and his leonin warriors saved Stonehold from an earth titan below the city, a battle [[The Sons of Thunder]] would later relive in the [[Pools of Memorium]].

Stonehold was the first major target of [[Dolghast]] in the open war. See [[The Siege of Stonehold]] and [[The Death of Soraya]].
