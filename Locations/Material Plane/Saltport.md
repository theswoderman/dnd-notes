# Saltport

**Region:** [[Stonehold]]

Last known location of [[Dwendall Dhormas]], who stayed here to recover after the bulette attack.

In many ways a sister-city to [[Hor Garam]], as if transplanted onto the western coast of [[Nilstraad]]. The main difference is an abundant salt mine.

A guard here named Zeke freed [[The Good Ones]] during their time in the town, before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined them. Zeke was a form of the silver dragon [[Zarisk]].
