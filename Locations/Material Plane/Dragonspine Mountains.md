# Dragonspine Mountains

Where [[Diggory Psalm]] built a holy temple in the five years before the tenth [[The Dawning|Dawning]], and where he now trains a new band of heroes under the banner of [[Torm]].

The [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] are planning something here.

The threat here is serious enough that [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] warned [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] about it, and notably, it had gone unseen by both [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and [[Torm]]. See [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]].

The temple is [[Temple Trueheart]], home of [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]]. [[Torm]] directed [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] toward it, and the silver dragon [[Zarisk]] carried [[The Good Ones]] there. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
