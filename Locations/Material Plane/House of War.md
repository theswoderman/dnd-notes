# House of War

The greatest temple to [[The Red Lady]] in [[Rorinden]], in [[Gildurn]], and a training ground for army recruits. Overseen by Warmaster [[Hayden Trask]].

Site of [[The Exorcism of Zargon]], and of [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s celebration as a follower of [[the Red Lady]].
