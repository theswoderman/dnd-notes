# Silverwell

Capital of [[Vallenhein]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] came here to learn why the kingdom had closed itself off, and instead uncovered two separate crises: the [[Duergar of Silverwell]] selling the mines' gold and silver to [[Mammon]], and [[The Consumed]] impersonating the king.

The city fell into civil war when the truth came out. See [[The Consumed and the Vallenhein Civil War]].
