# The Neziri Desert

The last obstacle between [[Alnoria]] and [[Xinar]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] fought a roc, bandits and scorpion-men crossing it, lost their horses, and woke an ancient blue dragon sleeping in the sands.

Fleeing the dragon, they were driven into wind-exposed ruins whose entrance collapsed behind them. It was the way down into the lost capital of the [[Nezerai Empire]].
