# The Unclaimed Isle

A jungle island so wild and difficult to traverse that no nation bothers to claim it. The fourth fragment of [[Queensbane]] lay at its center.

**Found here:** [[Siren's Rest]] · [[Raiorma]]'s lizardfolk village · [[Mohabi]]'s hut · the [[Yuan-Ti]] city and its ziggurat.

The planar gate to [[The Beastlands]] lies a few hours from [[Raiorma]]'s village.
