# Starfall Clearing

The destination [[The Collector]] named when [[The Good Ones]] met him on the road, and confirmed by his corpse when they questioned it in [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]]. Nothing else is known about it.

Since his errand turns out to have been collecting bestial trophies for [[Malar]]'s rite, whatever is here is probably part of that rather than a market. Nobody has been.
