# Bloodraven Keep

The castle [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and his mercenary company the [[The Blood Ravens]] once called home, held at the time of the crusade by hundreds of Red Orcs under [[Ogzuuk Torah'kor]].

Retaken by [[The Sons of Thunder]] and their allies. After the battle, [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] held a feast here to honor the fallen and welcome his new orc soldiers, and it was here that [[Casimir]] came to plead their father's case.
