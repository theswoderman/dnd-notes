# Panrelta

**Region:** [[Estrana]]

One of the two major continents of [[Estrana]], alongside [[Anendal]], and the setting of [[The Pilgrimage]] walked by [[The Good Ones]].

[Map of Panrelta](https://cdn2.inkarnate.com/1190460-8131bf12-bb55-11f0-aab4-ba97be869f98?disposition=attachment)

## The pilgrimage route
The journey can begin at either of two places, and runs to the lands once known as the [[Blackwaste|Black Waste]], now a verdant haven:

- [[Tulari Hot Springs]], primary home of the Leonin tribes
- [[Ishtir Marsh]], home of the chief dragonborn clan, and where [[The Good Ones]] started

## Waypoints so far
- [[Khulask Hollow]], liberated from [[Crucius Ilyanich]], later destroyed by [[The Drow]]
- [[The Iron Oak Woods]], current home of [[The Bettermans Guild]]
- [[The Garden of Dreams]], at the bottom of the deep mines
- [[Kaz'an Hold]], on the border of [[Dolghast]]
- [[The Shiftweave]] and [[Skyfang Lake]], crossed by [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] on his way to [[Stonehold]]
