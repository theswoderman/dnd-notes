# Gudjag Canyon

Home of the red orcs that once served the vampire lord [[Ogzuuk Torah'kor]] and later [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]. The gate to [[Mechanus]] on the material plane lies here, guarded by [[Astynom Alpha 009]], a Marut construct loyal to [[Primus]].

[[The Sons of Thunder]] entered and exited [[Mechanus]] through this gate. See [[Into Mechanus]].
