# The Blackwaste

A fiery hellscape in northern [[Grekosha]], ruled by [[Golzokroth]] [[Golzokroth|the Blood Tyrant]]. The fifth and final fragment of [[Queensbane]] lay in his volcano lair — itself built in a lost city of fire giants that [[Golzokroth]] destroyed himself.

## Ten years later
Restored. The Blackwaste is now a verdant haven, and the end point of [[The Pilgrimage]]. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] founded an arcane college here.
