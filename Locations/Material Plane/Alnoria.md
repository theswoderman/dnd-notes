# Alnoria

[[The Sons of Thunder]]'s home nation, ruled by [[King Berrigan]] from [[Whitehall]].

A little over a thousand years ago Alnoria fought a world-spanning war against [[Rorinden]], the empire to the south. It ended when the Prince of Alnoria confessed his love for the princess of [[Rorinden]], and a bridge was raised between the two capitals to represent the alliance that followed. That ending is year 0 of the A.P. calendar, *At Peace*. The war's most contested ground was [[The Rosefields]] in Alnoria's southwest, named for how the blood looked from a distance.

Alnoria is now formally at war with [[Dolghast]], declared by Berrigan on evidence the party brought back from the [[Ashes of Kemnan]].

**Settlements:** [[Whitehall]] · [[Mudbend]] · [[Runswick]] · [[Rothefell]]
