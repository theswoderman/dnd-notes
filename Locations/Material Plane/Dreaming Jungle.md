# Dreaming Jungle

A jungle on the border of [[Rorinden]] and [[Isembard]]. [[Minthur]] the Mindbreaker laired in a cave system near a large waterfall overlooking it. The tabaxi druid [[Viridian Sun]] claimed [[Minthur]] was guarding the jungle from being harvested for resources by the [[Isembard]] and [[Rorinden]] governments.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] came here to hunt [[Minthur]]. See [[The Slaying of Minthur]].
