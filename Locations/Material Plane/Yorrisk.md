# Yorrisk

**Region:** Unrecorded, disputed between [[House Keroskav]] of [[Grekosha]] and [[House Dufrey]] of [[The Iron Oak Woods]]
**Disposition:** Unknown

A developing town, and the thing [[House Keroskav]] and [[House Dufrey]] are fighting over.

This is the "yorrisk" [[The Collector]] named on the road. He said [[The Maiden]] told him she had acquaintances "on yorrisk upon the dawning", and the word sat unidentified until [[Falstad Firebeard]] recognized the two heraldries from the shared nightmare and remembered the dispute. [[The Maiden]] had also told the party, before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined, that she would meet them in Yorrisk.

So the pieces line up: a contested town, [[The Maiden]] with people there, and [[The Dawning]], the tenth of which is approaching, as the date.

The spelling is still as first recorded and has not been confirmed in writing.

## Why it matters
- [[The Maiden]]'s outstanding debt is 10,000 gold and an apex griffon heart. She named Yorrisk as the meeting place
- [[The Dawning]] reads as the deadline
- The town is already the subject of a war between a Grekoshan house and an Iron Oak house, and [[The Bettermans Guild]] sits in the Iron Oak
