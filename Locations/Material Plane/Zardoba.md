# Zardoba

City where [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] was raised by his adoptive family, and the staging point for the undersea expedition to [[Yskarat]]'s lair — the third fragment of [[Queensbane]] lay fifty miles off the coast.

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] saw Zardoba in ruins, part of the city fallen into the sea, in the vision granted by the fourth fragment. His parents were murdered here by a doppelganger wearing his face, and revived.

To the north lies the [[Whisperwood]].
