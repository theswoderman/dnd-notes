# The Rosefields

**Region:** southwestern [[Alnoria]], on the [[Rorinden]] border
**Disposition:** peaceful

## Summary
Two adjoining stretches of open country, marked on `Map of Estrana.webp` as the **Upper** and **Lower Rosefields**, lying in the southwest of [[Alnoria]] against the [[Rorinden]] frontier.

They take their name from the war between [[Alnoria]] and [[Rorinden]] that ended roughly a thousand years ago — the ending that begins the A.P. calendar, *At Peace*. This was the ground the two nations fought over most often, and the battleground they returned to again and again. Seen from a distance the whole country appeared to be carpeted in roses.

It was blood.

## Connections
- [[Alnoria]] — the fields lie in its southwest
- [[Rorinden]] — the war's other side; the border runs here
- [[Estrana]] — the A.P. calendar dates from the end of that war
