# The Iron Oak Woods

**Region:** [[Stonehold]]
**Disposition:** Positive

Current location of [[The Bettermans Guild]], and the closest thing [[The Good Ones]] have to a home base.

## House Dufrey
The woods are the seat of Iron Oak [[House Dufrey]], whose heraldry is green fields with a large black oak, red fringed. The red fringe symbolizes [[Grekosha]]n encroachment on the Iron Oak forest. [[House Dufrey]] and the Grekoshan [[House Keroskav]] are at war over a developing town called [[Yorrisk]].

That puts [[The Bettermans Guild]]'s camp, and therefore the party's home base, inside contested ground.

See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]] and [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
