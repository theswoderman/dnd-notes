# Blubberpike

[[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]'s home town. He saw it in flames in the vision granted by the second fragment of [[Queensbane]], found in the mines beneath [[Silverwell]].

[[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] stayed away after the death of his brother [[Nigel]], and returned only on the eve of the march into [[Dolghast]], when [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] raised [[Nigel]] with True Resurrection in front of their parents. See [[Nigel's Resurrection]].
