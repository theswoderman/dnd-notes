# Temple Trueheart

A place [[Torm]] directed [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] toward in [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]]: *"Let this fierce light lead you to Temple Trueheart and see what awaits those of the golden soul."*

The portal in the dream opened onto a snowy mountain peak where [[Diggory Psalm]] waited — likely the [[Dragonspine Mountains]], where Diggory built his temple to [[Torm]], though the dream does not say so outright.
