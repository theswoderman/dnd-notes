# Rorinden

The empire nation to the south, and [[Alnoria]]'s ancient enemy turned permanent ally — a bridge between the two capitals commemorates the marriage that ended their war a thousand years ago.

Its Grand General is [[Diana Zethros]]. Its capital is [[Gildurn]], site of the [[House of War]]. Red Orcs out of Rorinden garrisoned [[Bloodraven Keep]] under [[Ogzuuk Torah'kor]].
