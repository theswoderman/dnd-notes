# Deepforge

Dwarven city state, home of the [[Council of Mystics]]. [[King Fargrim]] was here forging armor from [[Golzokroth]]'s hide for [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] when [[Mohabi]] took him captive.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] fought an ice titan and frost wyverns that came through a gold-colored rift on their way in, rescued Fargrim and killed [[Mohabi]], and received the Council's blessing to ascend [[Heaven's Altar]].
