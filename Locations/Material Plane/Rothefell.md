# Rothefell

City north of [[Whitehall]], home to the [[Hendrel Dyleeri College of Arcane Studies]] where [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] studied.

Beneath a bakery called Borly's Fresh-baked Goods lay a hidden chapter house of the [[Ashes of Kemnan]], and beneath that, subterranean docks where kidnapped prisoners were being loaded onto ships bound for [[Silnothas]].

See [[The Siege of the Ashen Hideout]].
