# The Golden Plains

Grasslands home to [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s leonin people. [[The Sons of Thunder]] arrived to find tribes wiped out by dragonborn under [[Krintak Cinderheart]], with prisoners being taken north to [[Jiren's Rock]].

[[Thol]] of [[The Good Ones]] was raised here. The centaurs butchered in [[The Centaur Poachers]] came from this region, which is why he takes the poaching personally.
