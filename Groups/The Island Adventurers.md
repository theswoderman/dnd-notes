# The Island Adventurers

The five adventurers who sailed out to find a wandering island and accidentally woke the turtle-shaped elemental underneath it. See [[Awakening of the turtle]].

They are not part of either main campaign.

## Members
- [[Grug]], goliath monk
- [[Alyris]], half-elf bard
- [[Orist]], high elf paladin
- [[Kael'zar]], tiefling sorceress
- [[Haakon]], aarakocra warlock
