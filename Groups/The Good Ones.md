# The Good Ones

The party of pilgrims making the journey across [[Panrelta]] (see [[The Pilgrimage]]).

Bound by the tenets of the pilgrimage, including a standing obligation to help anyone who asks for it, which is how most of their trouble starts.

**Disposition:** Positive

## Members
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], dwarf, oath and pact of [[Torm]]
- [[Beemo]], voidborne artificer, created by [[Festra]]
- [[Gribnik Mossfoot]], goblin ranger, "[[Gribnik Mossfoot|Leaf-ear]]"
- [[Falstad Firebeard]], dwarf cleric of [[Berrenar]]
- [[Gegga]], human origami wizard, masked
- [[Thol]], half-orc fighter, adopted son of [[Bathoz Manefell]]

## Current position
At [[Temple Trueheart]], as guests of [[Diggory Psalm]]. The silver dragon [[Zarisk]] carried them there from the ruins of [[The Abbey]], which he destroyed along with the lycanthropes around it while the party rested in the caves beneath. [[Torin Turen]] came with them and was revived by the temple's clerics. See [[The Destruction of the Abbey]].

Beneath the abbey they killed [[Oraecheon Blackspire]], [[Bane]]'s champion, interrupting the ritual of [[The Black Grail]] he was performing on [[Torin Turen]]. [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] and [[Falstad Firebeard]] both spent their [[Right of Return|right of return]] in the fight, and both lost their divine connection afterward. [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]]'s cursed arm was cut off at the temple.

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] purged their cursed items into a [[Ring of Divine Inspiration]]. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]]. They are now in the middle of a sparring match against [[Aeroth]], [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]], and have taken [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s sword, the [[Hand of Truth]]. See [[The Sparring Match at Temple Trueheart]].

## What they are carrying
- [[The Abyssal Orb]], bound for [[The Sons of Thunder]]. It has started speaking: it told [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] "free me". [[The Collector]]'s corpse no longer shares the bag, having been thrown off a cliff
- 8 adult black dragon scales
- [[Ring of Divine Inspiration]], made by [[Diggory Psalm]] from the magic of [[Ring of Dread Command]], [[Despot's Reprisal]] and [[Tyrant's Totem Necklace]] once their curses were purged
- [[Golden Hourglass]], lent to [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] in secret and due back before they leave the temple
- An unidentified key, found on one of [[The Fists of Bane]]
- The opened [[Sylvan Parcel]] and its four effects
- Hags in their dreams. [[Mirna Gribwood]] and now [[Sila Frostblood]], with the whole [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil|coven]] circle casting nightly (see [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]])
