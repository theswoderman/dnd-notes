# The Good Ones

The party of pilgrims making the journey across [[Panrelta]] (see [[The Pilgrimage]]).

Bound by the tenets of the pilgrimage, including a standing obligation to help anyone who asks for it, which is how most of their trouble starts.

**Disposition:** Positive

## Members
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], dwarf, oath and pact of [[Torm]]
- [[Beemo]], voidborne artificer, created by [[Festra]]
- [[Gribnik Mossfoot]], goblin ranger, "[[Gribnik Mossfoot|Leaf-ear]]"
- [[Falstad Firebeard]], dwarf cleric of [[Berrenar]]
- [[Gegga]], human origami wizard, masked
- [[Thol]], half-orc fighter, adopted son of [[Bathoz Manefell]]

## Current position
In the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]], within sight of [[The Abbey]], about twenty minutes off. They destroyed [[Bane]]'s undead garrison on a bridge along with [[The Fists of Bane|its three drow captains]] and the Specter of Bane they reformed into, survived a were-rat attack on [[Gegga]]'s conjured tower on the road afterward, and left their horses at the refugee camp around [[The Bettermans Guild]] with two weeks of supplies.

They are closing in on [[The Abbey]], where both [[Malar]] and [[Bane]]'s followers mean to take [[The Black Grail]]. [[Torin Turen]] went into this forest alone ahead of them, and by [[The Collector]]'s account the grail is inside him.

## What they are carrying
- [[The Abyssal Orb]], bound for [[The Sons of Thunder]]. It has started speaking: it told [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] "free me". [[The Collector]]'s corpse no longer shares the bag, having been thrown off a cliff
- 8 adult black dragon scales
- [[Ring of Dread Command]], on [[Thol]]'s hand and not coming off
- [[Despot's Reprisal]], the cursed longsword [[Thol]] has attuned to, and [[Tyrant's Totem Necklace]], also cursed
- An unidentified key, found on one of [[The Fists of Bane]]
- The opened [[Sylvan Parcel]] and its four effects
- Hags in their dreams. [[Mirna Gribwood]] and now [[Sila Frostblood]], with the whole [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil|coven]] circle casting nightly (see [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]])
