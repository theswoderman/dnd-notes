# The Bettermans Guild

**Disposition:** Positive
**Led by:** [[Torin Turen]], high priest of [[Illmater]]
**Base:** [[The Iron Oak Woods]]
**Size:** About 250 in the guild; about 300 in the camp around it

A group taking care of refugees and generally trying to look after good people. They have become [[The Good Ones]]' effective home base and patron: they sent [[The Good Ones]] into the deep mines, and the survivors of [[Khulask Hollow]] were led back here after the town was destroyed.

## The camp
The camp has grown from roughly 100 people to 300, gathered around a guild of about 250. The [[Khulask Hollow]] settlers have been welcomed into the community and seem to be enjoying the gentler climate. At the feast for [[The Good Ones]]' return, spirits were high.

[[Torin Turen]]'s speech there set out the purpose of the guild: to create better men, and to offer a life full of strife. He toasted the better men who have died for that cause and those who still will.

## Under pressure
- [[Mossclaw]] incursions. The camp has been fending off raids the whole time [[The Good Ones]] were in the mines. One with a black mohawk was chased off and is expected back with reinforcements
- [[Torin Turen]] is failing. He has barely slept, his connection to [[Illmater]] is degrading, and he has now ridden alone into the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]] (see [[Torin's Flight into the Sagethorn]])
- [[Ky'ar Lastet]] is stepping up, and wants full leadership
- One of the guild's stonemasons did not come back from the deep mines
