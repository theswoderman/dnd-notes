# Black Blood Pack

**Disposition:** Hostile
**Base:** The [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]

A lycanthrope pack in the service of [[Malar]], god of hunters and lycanthropes. [[The Collector]] presumably belonged to it, and warned that the pack would come for [[The Good Ones]] in the moments before his death at [[Gegga]]'s hand.

The lycanthropes around [[The Abbey]] in the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]] were the pack. The silver dragon [[Zarisk]] destroyed them on the night of the full moon. More of the pack likely remain. See [[The Destruction of the Abbey]].

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
