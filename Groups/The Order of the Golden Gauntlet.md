# The Order of the Golden Gauntlet

**Disposition:** Positive

A group of followers of [[Torm]] who are not exactly paladins or clerics, but carry out his will.

[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] has been told to go and meet up with them when possible.

[[Torm]] has since confirmed that an invitation is coming: the gift delivered in [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]] was meant to ensure his safe passage and eventual invitation to the order. The dream names them the Knights of the Golden Gauntlet.

At [[Temple Trueheart]], [[Diggory Psalm]] told [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] in confidence that the order could use his assistance once [[The Pilgrimage]] is over. The order is also to deal with [[Oraecheon Blackspire]], who as a death knight will reform within days of his death. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].

The order's members live at [[Temple Trueheart]].
