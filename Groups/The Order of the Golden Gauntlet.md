# The Order of the Golden Gauntlet

**Disposition:** Positive

A group of followers of [[Torm]] who are not exactly paladins or clerics, but carry out his will. [[Diggory Psalm]] runs the order from [[Temple Trueheart]], the temple he built in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]].

[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] has been told to go and meet up with them when possible.

[[Torm]] has since confirmed that an invitation is coming: the gift delivered in [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]] was meant to ensure his safe passage and eventual invitation to the order. The dream names them the Knights of the Golden Gauntlet.

At [[Temple Trueheart]], [[Diggory Psalm]] told [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] in confidence that the order could use his assistance once [[The Pilgrimage]] is over. The order recently lost a member, [[Vladislav]], who returned to serve [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in [[Grekosha]]. It is interested in [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] joining, since [[Torm]] chose him as a new champion by granting him the [[Hand of Valor]]. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].

The order has members powerful enough to defeat [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] fully, and will deal with him when he reforms as a death knight. [[The Good Ones]] will not be involved.

[[The Good Ones]] are asking the order to examine [[The Abyssal Orb]].
