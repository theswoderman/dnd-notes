# The Cult of Bane

**Affiliation:** [[Bane]]
**Disposition:** Hostile
**Base:** [[The Abbey]] and the tunnels beneath it

## Summary
The cultists who occupy [[The Abbey]], the former temple of [[Illmater]]. The cult appears to number over fifty. Its documents go back at least five years.

It has been kidnapping travelers on the roads and abducting people from towns in southern [[Dolghast]], including [[Brashta]] and [[Brinemire]], and it welcomes soldiers from [[Dolghast]] into its ranks. A cultists' map marks [[Khulask Hollow]] with "who beat us to it."

The Gauntlets of Bane are its elite warriors: less powerful than [[The Fists of Bane]] but more formidable than the ordinary cultists.

One of their rooms in the tunnels held the bodies of lycanthropes wrapped in the silver barbed wire used to trap them, and the corpse of a lycanthrope caught mid-transformation.

[[Oraecheon Blackspire]] was found holding [[Torin Turen]] prisoner in the tunnels beneath the abbey. See [[The Tunnels Beneath the Abbey]].
