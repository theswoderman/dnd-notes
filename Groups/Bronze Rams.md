# Bronze Rams

A dwarven brotherhood of warriors. Members can be granted a **semicentury release** from service — [[Brom Stonefist]] received one after being disfigured at the [[The Siege of Stonehold|Battle of Stonehold]].

## Known members
- [[Brom Stonefist]] — former member, retired to [[Hor Garam]]
- [[Magus Trollbeater|Maygus Trollbeater]] — [[Brom Stonefist|Brom]]'s old mentor, in [[Stonehold]]
