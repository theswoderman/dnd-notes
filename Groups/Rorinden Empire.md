# The [[Rorinden]] Empire

The southern empire, [[Alnoria]]'s ancient enemy and permanent ally. Its Grand General is [[Diana Zethros]]; its martial faith centers on [[The Red Lady]] and the [[House of War]] in [[Gildurn]].

Red Orcs out of [[Rorinden]] garrisoned [[Bloodraven Keep]] under [[Ogzuuk Torah'kor]].
