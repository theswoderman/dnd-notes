# The Viera of the [[Whisperwood]]

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s birth people, living in a hidden village in the [[Whisperwood]] north of [[Zardoba]]. Skilled scouts who move unseen through the trees, and possessed of no magic at all, which is why [[Melvin]]'s poisoned lake was killing their young and elderly with no recourse.

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] was banished as a child. The banishment was lifted after he saved them.

- [[Zalema]], village elder
- [[Lien]], scout and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s mother
