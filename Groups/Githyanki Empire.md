# The Githyanki Empire

Ruled by [[Vlaakith the Eighth]], [[Vlaakith the Eighth|the Lich Queen]], from [[Tu'Narath]], a city built into the body of a dead six-armed god in the [[Astral Sea]].

[[Captain Banithi]] sold [[The Sons of Thunder]] to them as slaves. They fought in the combat pits and escaped. See [[The Betrayal of Banithi]].
