# The Nezerai Empire

A civilisation of desert elves, extinct for nine thousand years, rumored to have been the very first civilisation in all of [[Estrana]].

Their lost capital lies untouched beneath the [[Neziri Desert]], preserving [[The Mural and the Gem|the mural and the sixteen-sided gem]] under the guard of [[Ahmeses]].

[[Vhal Nimzur]], one of [[the five heroes]] who fought [[Aeralath]], was a Nezerai elf.
