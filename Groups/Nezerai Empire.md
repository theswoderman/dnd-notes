# The Nezerai Empire

A civilisation of desert elves, extinct for nine thousand years, rumored to have been the very first civilisation in all of [[Estrana]].

Their lost capital lies untouched beneath the [[Neziri Desert]], preserving [[The Mural and the Gem|the mural and the sixteen-sided gem]] under the guard of [[Ahmeses]].

[[Vhal Nimzur]], the highest-ranking member of Nezerai mageocracy and one of [[The Five Heroes]] who fought [[Aeralath]], was a Nezerai elf.

## The Reborn

Before the civilization's destruction, [[Vhal Nimzur]] devised an experiment to transfer the souls of a chosen few into constructed, mechanical bodies. These beings, called the Reborn or the Awoken, lay dormant on an uncharted continent hidden behind a wall of magical fog for nearly ten thousand years. They began to wake when [[Queensbane]]'s power dispelled the fog. Their leader, a being called Vanguard, carried a staff bearing the ancient Nezerai symbol. See [[The Hidden Continent]].
