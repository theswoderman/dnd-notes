# The Vampire Lords

Four vampires who ruled different portions of the world and ensured the survival of their kind. All four are now destroyed.

| Lord | Title | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| [[Onias Kane]] | Lord of Blood | Killed in [[Baristos]] |
| [[Lorilai Xalthina]] | Mistress of Rot | Destroyed in the crypts |
| [[Ogzuuk Torah'kor]] | Lord of War | Fell in honorable single combat at [[Bloodraven Keep]] |
| [[Ifan Le Rouge]] | Lord of Death | Killed by his son outside [[Nostra]] |

See [[The Liberation of Grekosha]] and [[The Death of Ifan Le Rouge]].
