# The Ashes of Kemnan

**Also called:** the Gray Saints, the Ashen Saints
**Symbol:** A fist wreathed in flame
**Leader:** [[Melvin]] (executed)

An order of criminals that once robbed the powerful to help those in need. For the last several decades it has served only itself.

Its real business was kidnapping people from all over the world and shipping them to [[Silnothas]] — where, as [[Melvin]] admitted before his execution, they were to be sacrificed to [[Aeralath]].

**Known members:** [[Melvin]] · [[Callus Halterstock]] · [[Hariett Hinkapot]] (defected)

**Hideout:** beneath Borly's Fresh-baked Goods in [[Rothefell]], with subterranean docks.
See [[The Siege of the Ashen Hideout]].
