# The Black Crown

**Symbol:** A black skull crowned with antlers
**Worship:** Demons — and specifically "their matron", [[Aeralath]]

A cult about which very little is known. Its mark was first seen tattooed across the back of [[The Peddler]], alongside a manifesto declaring death and destruction an offering.

The same symbol appears on the banner carried by the invading demons in [[The Mural and the Gem]] — a ten-thousand-year-old depiction of [[The Sundering]]. That is what makes the cult more than a local menace: it connects the present war to the last time [[Aeralath]] tried to unmake the world.

[[Kyron Delinor]] is its most visible servant.
