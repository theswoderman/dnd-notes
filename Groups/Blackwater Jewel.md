# The Blackwater Jewel

The spelljammer hired in [[Luxaria]] to carry [[The Sons of Thunder]] to the [[Ruby Palace]], captained by [[Stella Enrulon]] with [[Bürd]] as first mate.

Destroyed by the living spellstorm's supernova at the very edge of the palace's asteroid; the crew survived because they were inside when it happened. Stella shielded the party with her wings.

She and her crew now live on the material plane, learning to sail water instead of the [[Astral Sea]].
