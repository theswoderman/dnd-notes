# The League of Celestial Champions

**Disposition:** Positive

A council [[Diggory Psalm]] is forming, meant to be an organized force against evil. It is to coordinate [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] with orders gathered around other gods, such as [[Moradin]] and [[Illmater]].

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] described it at [[Temple Trueheart]] after [[Falstad Firebeard]] argued that heroes need to take a more offensive posture against the great evil powers. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
