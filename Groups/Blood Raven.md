# The Blood Raven

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s old mercenary company, based out of [[Bloodraven Keep]] in [[Grekosha]]. They helped [[The Sons of Thunder]] break into the queen's palace in [[Baristos]] and kill [[Onias Kane]].

## Ten years later
With [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] on the throne, the Blood Ravens are described as **[[Grekosha]]'s elite unit** — "basically seal team six", in the party's words.

**They killed a black dragon.** [[The Good Ones]] found boxes of black dragon scales in [[The Collector]]'s cart, apparently being smuggled, and the Blood Ravens' kill is the only black dragon death anyone knows of. It seems unlikely a second dragon died — which would mean the scales came from that one, and someone has been selling them.
