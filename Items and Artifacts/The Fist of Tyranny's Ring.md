# The Fist of Tyranny's Ring

A ring worn by the Fist of Tyranny, one of [[The Fists of Bane]]. It came off his hand when he died at [[The Battle at the Bridge]].

[[Thol]] put it on in the minutes while the three captains' essences were still gathering, and cannot take it back off. [[Gegga]] identified it as certainly an evil magic ring. What it actually does has not been established.
