# Ring of Dread Command

A ring worn by the Fist of Oppression, the purple-armored knight of [[The Fists of Bane]]. It came off his hand when he died at [[The Battle at the Bridge]].

[[Thol]] put it on in the minutes while the three captains' essences were still gathering, and cannot take it back off. [[Gegga]] read it as certainly an evil magic ring at the time, and it was later identified as a Ring of Dread Command, though what it does for [[Thol]] in practice is still unknown.

It was first recorded as the Fist of Tyranny's ring and then the Fist of Oppression's ring, before it was identified. Both old names are kept as aliases.
