# Ring of Dread Command

A ring worn by the Fist of Oppression, the purple-armored knight of [[The Fists of Bane]]. It came off his hand when he died at [[The Battle at the Bridge]].

[[Thol]] put it on in the minutes while the three captains' essences were still gathering, and could not take it back off. [[Gegga]] read it as certainly an evil magic ring at the time, and it was later identified as a Ring of Dread Command.

Its curse made [[Thol]] unwilling to follow authorities.

[[Diggory Psalm]] purged its curse at [[Temple Trueheart]] along with the party's other cursed items, and transferred their magic into the [[Ring of Divine Inspiration]]. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].

It was first recorded as the Fist of Tyranny's ring and then the Fist of Oppression's ring, before it was identified. Both old names are kept as aliases.
