# Skystones

A rare crafting material gathered by stone giants at the [[Hand of Annum]], where shooting stars fall. The giants treat them as a gift from [[Annum]] and thank him for them.

They are known only from [[Thalrik Lastfeast]]'s vision in the [[Giant Memory Gemstones]], and none have been recovered yet.
