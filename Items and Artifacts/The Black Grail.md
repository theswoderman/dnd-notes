# The Black Grail

A ritual that turns the one performing it into a death knight by consuming part of another person's soul. [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] performed it on [[Torin Turen]] beneath [[The Abbey]] and became a death knight, at the cost of part of [[Torin Turen|Torin]]'s soul. [[The Good Ones]] interrupted it. See [[The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire]].

The followers of [[Malar]] and [[Bane]] both sought it, and spoke of it as something to drink from and something to be carried.

## What is known
[[Torin Turen]] has dreamed of it every night for weeks: a black grail hanging above a stone tomb. When the two meet the stone cracks, red blood runs out, and it bursts into green flames that melt the grail. Every night the flames burn brighter and longer than the night before, and the dreams drain him. See [[Return to the Bettermans Guild]].

Interrogated after death, [[The Collector]] said [[Malar]] had sent him to "collect the bestial trophies so we may drink from the black grail". Asked whether the grail was in the temple of [[Illmater]], he answered "it is within the small gnome." [[Torin Turen|Torin]] is a gnome. Asked what would happen next, he said the grail would be taken to [[The Abbey]], near [[The Tree of Pain]]. See [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]].

[[Bane]]'s followers want it too, and are not allies of [[Malar]]'s. [[Malar]] told his pack "their steel would sharpen our talons, but they seek the grail for themselves."

[[Oraecheon Blackspire]] claimed it beneath [[The Abbey]] as the ritual transformed him into a death knight: "you're too late, the black grail is mine." [[Torin Turen]] lay bound to the altar behind him. See [[The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire]].
