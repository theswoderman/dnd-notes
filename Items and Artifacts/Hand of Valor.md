# Hand of Valor edit

**Bearer:** [[Thalrik Lastfeast]]
**Given by:** [[Torm]], delivered by [[Diggory Psalm]]

A weapon given to [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] in [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]]. [[Torm]] described it in the vision as *"what you wield in your heart — a brave star of valor"*, but the weapon itself is called the **Hand of Valor**.

**He still had it when he woke.**

Its purpose, as given: to light the way to [[Temple Trueheart]], and to ensure safe passage toward an eventual invitation to the [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet|Knights of the Golden Gauntlet]].

## Connections
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] — its bearer
- [[Torm]] — its source
- [[Diggory Psalm]] — delivered it, and himself bears the [[Hand of Truth]]
