# The Abyssal Orb

An orb that emerged from the corpse of a necrohulk, which itself emerged from the body of the purple worm beneath [[The Garden of Dreams]] after [[The Good Ones]] killed it. The necrohulk was a creature from [[The Abyss]].

See [[Exploration of the deep mines]].

## Cross-campaign significance
[[The Abyss]] is where [[Aeralath]] is from. An abyssal creature surfacing under a [[Panrelta]] mountain a decade after she fell is the most concerning thing the pilgrims have turned up.

[[The Good Ones]] plan to bring the orb to [[The Sons of Thunder]], who are still alive and very much a power in the world, so they can take care of it.

## It spoke
[[Thalrik Lastfeast]] climbed into the bag of holding. After about a minute he felt something in there with him but could see nothing, and then heard a voice in his mind: "Free me." He turned around and found the orb staring at him. He left in a hurry and told the party. See [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]].

The session notes call it the ichor deep engine at this point, which is the first time that phrase has attached to the orb itself rather than to the half-heard business about "shae de claw" below.

## What was in the bag with it
[[The Collector]]'s corpse used to share the bag. After [[Gegga]] killed him the party put the body in with the orb, their own assessment at the time being that this was probably a horrible idea. See [[The Collector on the Road]]. They used it once more for [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]], then [[Beemo]] threw it off a cliff, so the orb is alone in there now.
