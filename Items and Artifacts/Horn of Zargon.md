# The Horn of [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]]

**Also called:** the Spear of [[Zargon the Immortal|Sina'Draiel]]

A black, bone-like spear found in a temple on the **moon**, reached from the altar in [[Av'orel's Tomb]]. When [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] grasped it he could not let go; it burned the flesh from his arm to the elbow, then dematerialised and took the appearance of the arm it had destroyed.

It is not a divine gift. It is the horn of [[Zargon the Immortal]] — the prison [[Asmodeus]] made for a Baatorian he could not kill.

**To destroy it:** carry it to the bottom of the ninth layer of the
[[Nine Hells of Baator]] and cast it into the flames of the deepest pit.

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] gave up using it in favour of the [[Rose of Asmodeus]]. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] cut the horn-bearing arm off during [[The Exorcism of Zargon]]; [[Hayden Trask]] regrew it.
