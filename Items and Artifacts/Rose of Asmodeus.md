# The Rose of [[Asmodeus]]

A spear created by [[Primus]], god of law and the modrons, in [[Mechanus]].

It was given to [[Asmodeus]] after his trial before the hosts of [[Mount Celestia]], not as a punishment but as a reminder to keep to his lawful (if still evil) ways, or [[Primus]] would be forced to act. [[Asmodeus]] disliked the strings and traded it to [[Sardior]] millennia later as payment for information.

It had gathered dust ever since, so [[Sardior]] let [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] carry it against [[Aeralath]], a piece of irony he seemed to enjoy.
