# The Fist of Oppression's Ring

A ring worn by the Fist of Oppression, the purple-armored knight of [[The Fists of Bane]]. It came off his hand when he died at [[The Battle at the Bridge]].

[[Thol]] put it on in the minutes while the three captains' essences were still gathering, and cannot take it back off. [[Gegga]] read it as certainly an evil magic ring, but it has not been properly identified and what it does is unknown.

It was first recorded as the Fist of Tyranny's ring, which is why the old name is kept as an alias.
