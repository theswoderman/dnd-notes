# Sylvan Parcel

A bundle of spell components given to [[The Good Ones]] by [[Hiram Oakbrow]], a worshipper of [[Sylvanus]], back before session 6. That is before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined and before the notes begin. The party knew there were several ways to consume it and that each did something different, but not what the ways or the effects were.

**They opened it at [[The Bettermans Guild]]'s camp** and found out. See [[Return to the Bettermans Guild]].

[D&D Beyond entry](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/10940881-sylvan-parcel)

## Effects
| Consumption | Effect |
|---|---|
| Inhale smoke | +10 walking speed |
| Cook with flame | Maximum HP increases by 2dX hit dice + level |
| Mark with ash | Add your proficiency bonus to Stealth checks and DEX saving throws — stacks with expertise |
| Suffer flames | Resistance to fire damage, and 1d4 fire damage on one attack per turn |

## Spells granted
Opening it also gave each of the party a spell to cast.

- [[Beemo]] and [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] — treestride, for one hour
- Others — speak with animals
- [[Thol]] — find the path, with a one day duration
