# Golden Hourglass

**Owner:** [[Diggory Psalm]]
**Bearer:** [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], on loan

A golden hourglass that turns time back when it is flipped. It belongs to [[Diggory Psalm]], who has had it since his days with [[The Sons of Thunder]]. [[Diggory Psalm]] demonstrated it at [[Temple Trueheart]]: he handed it to [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], told him to flip it after three seconds, and jumped off a cliff. Flipping it returned [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] to the top of the cliff.

[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] holds it in secret, and is the only one of [[The Good Ones]] who knows of it. When and whether to use it in the sparring match against [[Aeroth]], [[Gillead Dragonsong]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] is his choice. He must return it before [[The Good Ones]] leave the temple, and is not to mention it to the rest of the party. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
