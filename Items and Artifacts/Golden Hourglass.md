# Golden Hourglass

**Bearer:** [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], on loan
**Given by:** [[Diggory Psalm]]

A golden hourglass that turns time back when it is flipped. [[Diggory Psalm]] demonstrated it at [[Temple Trueheart]]: he handed it to [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], told him to flip it after three seconds, and jumped off a cliff. Flipping it returned [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] to the top of the cliff.

[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] holds it in secret. When and whether to use it in the sparring match against [[Aeroth]], [[Gillead Dragonsong]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] is his choice. He must return it before [[The Good Ones]] leave the temple, and is not to mention it to the rest of the party. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
