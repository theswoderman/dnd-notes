# The Hand of Truth

[[Torm]]'s own sword, given to [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] when they met face to face in the [[Platinum Palace]].

## Ten years later
[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] still carries it. In [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]]'s sparring match at [[Temple Trueheart]], [[Thol]] took it from him on a second attempt, bought by [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] with the [[Golden Hourglass]]. See [[The Sparring Match at Temple Trueheart]].
