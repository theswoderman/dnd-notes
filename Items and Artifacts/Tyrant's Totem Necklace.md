# Tyrant's Totem Necklace

A totem necklace taken from the Fist of Tyranny, one of [[The Fists of Bane]], after [[The Battle at the Bridge]]. Identified as the Tyrant's Totem Necklace, cursed: it has a chance of making its wearer treat enemies as allies and allies as enemies.
