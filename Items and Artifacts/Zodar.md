# The Zodar

An otherworldly metallic being found dormant in the [[Ruby Palace]], a servant of [[Sardior]].

On its master's instruction it used a Wish spell to send [[The Sons of Thunder]] and the crew of the [[Blackwater Jewel]] back to the material plane, which caused it to self-destruct and destroy the palace in the process.
