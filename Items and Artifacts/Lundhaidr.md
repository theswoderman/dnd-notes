# Lundhaidr

A legendary leonin great hammer, originally wielded by [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]], whose warriors liberated [[Stonehold]] from an earth titan nearly four hundred years ago.

Given to [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] at the [[Tulari Hot Springs]] along with the rank of Dreadclaw.
