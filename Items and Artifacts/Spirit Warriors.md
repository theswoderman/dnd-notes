# Spirit Warriors

Armor built for fighting [[Witchlight Marauder|Witchlight Marauders]]. Each works essentially as a mech suit and enhances all of the wearer's abilities. A wearer becomes permanently bonded to their spirit warrior.

A task force of three orcs and three elves used six of them to defeat a marauder in the [[Astral Sea]]. See [[Defeat of the Witchlight Marauder]].
