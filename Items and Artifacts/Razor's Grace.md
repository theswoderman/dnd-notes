# Razor's Grace

A crystal blade, originally stolen from the Erdking by [[Darrius|Abrovand]] to lure him away from [[The Beastlands]]. Abrovand gave it to Johan Malcovitch, [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s grandfather, who said he won it in a duel. It passed through the family and was eventually recovered from [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s brother [[Melvin]]. The Erdking recognized it on sight and wanted it back, which was part of his stakes in the 24-hour hunt.
