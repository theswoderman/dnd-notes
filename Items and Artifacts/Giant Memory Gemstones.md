# Giant Memory Gemstones

Broken gemstones holding the memories of giants, recovered at [[The Garden of Dreams]] — the sacred site deep beneath the mountain where those memories are stored and guarded by [[The Rockspeaker]].

[[The Good Ones]] slept holding them and each dreamed one of the memories inside.

## The dreams
| Who | What they saw |
|---|---|
| [[Beemo]] | A large fungal cavern where giants had built a sun into the ceiling out of gemstones. Inspiring as a craftsman; he slept very well |
| [[Thol]] | The point of view of a fire giant in a war on a bridge. He was killed by a magma stone |
| [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] | A giant witnessing dinosaurs made of magma; felt connected to nature |
| [[Falstad Firebeard]] | A mountaintop temple over an expansive desert, stone giants chanting [[Annum]]'s name. He was turned into a statue as a ritual honoring a master craftsman |
| [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] | The [[Hand of Annum]] in [[Dolghast]] — stone giants offering to the night sky, gathering [[Skystones]] as shooting stars fell |
| [[Gegga]] | A child in a cave; a cloaked figure with a storm staff facing a colossal thunder dragon, possibly [[Felgedrine the Inevitable]], over a plateau in [[Dolghast]] |

[[Beemo]], [[Gegga]] and [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] additionally suffered a dream of a crone eating a griffin who then attacked the dreamer directly. They took damage and woke exhausted — possibly the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]].
