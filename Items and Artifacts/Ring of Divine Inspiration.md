# Ring of Divine Inspiration

**Made by:** [[Diggory Psalm]]
**Owner:** [[The Good Ones]]

A ring [[Diggory Psalm]] made for [[The Good Ones]] at [[Temple Trueheart]]. He took every cursed item the party was carrying, among them [[Ring of Dread Command]] and [[Despot's Reprisal]], purged their curses, and transferred their magic into this new item. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
