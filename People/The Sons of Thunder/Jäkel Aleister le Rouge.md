# Jäkel

**Class:** Warlock, later paladin of the Oath of the Red Knight
**Affiliation:** [[The Sons of Thunder]], [[Blood Raven]], armies of [[Grekosha]]
**Status:** Alive
**Titles:** Raven Lord, High General of [[Grekosha]]; the Scarlet Raven

## Summary
Son of the vampire lord [[Ifan Le Rouge]]. Jakel's arc is a long war against his own family, followed by the discovery that his patron was never a god at all.

His patron "[[Zargon the Immortal|Sina'Draiel]]" turned out to be [[Zargon the Immortal]], First Lord of Hell — a Baatorian elder evil sealed inside its own horn on the moon. Jakel grasped that horn in a lunar temple; it burned away his arm to the elbow and took its place as the  [[Horn of Zargon|Spear of Sina'Drael]].

## Key moments
- Opened the sealed doors of [[Av'orel's Tomb]] with the symbol on his palm, then claimed the spear on the moon
- Found the fourth sword fragment beneath the sea and saw [[Nostra]] overrun by undead
- Named Raven Lord by [[Queen Marislova Domeshka]] after the death of [[Onias Kane]]
- Killed his mother [[Chessa]] and his father [[Ifan Le Rouge]] outside [[Nostra]] — see [[The Death of Ifan Le Rouge]]
- Renounced his oath of vengeance for the Oath of the Red Knight, becoming the Scarlet Raven
- Possessed by [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]] during [[The Exorcism of Zargon]]; the arm holding the horn was cut off by [[Bathoz Manefell]] and regrown
- Traded the horn for the [[Rose of Asmodeus]], gifted by [[Sardior]]

## Connections

## Ten years later
Appears in the [[00 - Panrelta Index|Pilgrims of Panrelta]] campaign, set a decade after Campaign 1: [[The Good Ones]] met him at a dinner in [[Stonehold]].

Half-elf paladin (originally dhampir) of the Oath of the Red Knight, and **current mortal champion of [[The Red Lady]]**.

Following the defeat of [[Aeralath]] he became **King of [[Grekosha]]** and wed Queen/General [[Diana Zethros]] of [[Rorinden]], herself a paladin of the Red Knight. He fathered three children, two of them aasimar/tiefling twin sons.

As king he made the agreement with [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] that opened the road for [[The Pilgrimage]].

## In the Panrelta campaign
Besides the dinner in [[Stonehold]], [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] is the one who **warned [[Diggory Psalm]] of the threat in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]]** — a threat that had gone unseen by both Diggory and [[Torm]]. See [[Thalrik's Dream of Torm]].

**He is also the one who tried to resurrect [[Arthur Van Hark]]** — corrected in session 28, having previously been recorded against [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]. The attempt failed because the requirements of the revive spell were not met.

[[Thol]] dreamed of him freezing alongside [[Bathoz Manefell]] — his adoptive father and his adopted uncle — in one of [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]].
