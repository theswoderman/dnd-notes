# Davynn

**Player:** [[Ian]]
**Race/Class:** Wizard
**Affiliation:** [[The Sons of Thunder]]
**Status:** Alive (killed once by [[Sharess the Black]], revived by [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]])
**Home:** [[Blubberpike]]

## Summary
[[The Sons of Thunder]]'s arcanist and primary means of travel: most of the group's teleports, and a great deal of its firepower (Meteor Swarm, True Telekinesis, Imprisonment), come from him. Formerly a student at the [[Hendrel Dyleeri College of Arcane Studies]] in [[Rothefell]], from which he was removed; readmission was offered on condition that he feed the college information from his travels.

## Key moments
- Found the second fragment of the sword in the mines beneath [[Silverwell]], and saw [[Blubberpike]] burning
- Given radiant magic by the sphinx [[Ahmeses]]
- Borrowed the Robes of the Archmagi from [[Elyon Ivasil]] for the hunt for [[Golzokroth]]
- Landed the killing blow on the earth titan in the [[Pools of Memorium]] with Meteor Swarm
- Cast Imprisonment during [[The Exorcism of Zargon]], but the spell was resisted
- Struck the killing blow on [[Cerrunos|the Erdking]], god of hunting, with True Telekinesis in [[The Beastlands]]
- Nearly killed by [[Chronus]]'s 9th-level Time Ravage in [[Mechanus]], aging him to within thirty days of death
- Reportedly involved in the death of his own brother, a guilt [[Kovir]] judged him for; the brother was later revived

## Ten years later
He is a gnome evocation wizard, among the most preeminent of his time, and may have no true rival on the material plane.

After the war he founded an arcane college in the restored and verdant lands of the [[Blackwaste]]. *College name not yet given.*
