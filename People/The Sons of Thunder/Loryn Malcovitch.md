# Loryn

**Race:** Viera — see [[Viera of the Whisperwood]]
**Affiliation:** [[The Sons of Thunder]]
**Status:** Alive (killed once by [[The Ravager]], revived by [[Kehl Brokenhorn]])
**Origin:** Born in the [[Whisperwood]]; raised in [[Zardoba]]

## Summary
[[The Sons of Thunder]]'s hunter of criminals. His pursuit of [[The Peddler]] is what pulled the group east out of [[Mudbend]] and set the whole campaign in motion. Banished from his Viera people as a child, he was raised by an adoptive family in [[Zardoba]] — a family whose favor he unknowingly took from his brother [[Melvin]], which became [[Melvin]]'s justification for everything that followed.

## Key moments
- Killed by [[The Ravager]] during [[The Gnoll Contract]] and flown to [[Whitehall]] for resurrection
- Grabbed the fourth sword fragment as the ziggurat fell, and saw [[Zardoba]] in ruins — see [[The Unclaimed Isle and Anathimus]]
- His adoptive parents were murdered by a doppelganger wearing his face; the party revived them
- Cleared his name in the [[Whisperwood]] and had his banishment lifted — see [[Melvin's Poison and the Whisperwood]]
- Learned his birth name was Anyil and that the scout [[Lien]] is his mother
- Killed in the [[Ruby Palace]] by [[Adomaiah]]; restored by [[Diggory Psalm]], but weakened

## Connections
- [[Melvin]] — brother and nemesis, leader of the [[Ashes of Kemnan]]
- [[Lien]] — birth mother
- [[Zalema]] — village elder of the [[Whisperwood]] Viera
- Judged by [[Kovir]] for overindulgence in physical desire

## Ten years later
Made a career for himself as a detective in [[Whitehall]] and now serves under [[King Berrigan]] as chief inspector. After the first [[The Dawning|Dawning]] he founded a restaurant. He still serves the Alnorian crown.
