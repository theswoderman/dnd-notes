# Bathoz Manefell

**Race/Class:** Leonin barbarian
**Affiliation:** [[The Sons of Thunder]]
**Status:** Alive (slain once by [[The Consumed]], later restored)

## Summary
The first bearer of the broken blade that would become [[Queensbane]]. Bathoz found the original hilt in the caves beneath the goblin village near [[Mudbend]], and the weapon has been bound to him ever since — it seals rifts, acts as a planar compass, and shows visions to whoever touches a new fragment.

Exiled from his leonin tribe in the [[Golden Plains]] after losing a duel to [[Kuzo Clawborn]].

## Key moments
- Touched the hilt beneath the goblin village and saw his homeland in flames — see [[The Goblin Caves and the Hilt]]
- Killed by [[The Consumed]] in the throne room of [[Silverwell]] — see [[The Consumed and the Vallenhein Civil War]]
- Took the powers of a black dragon during the [[The Siege of Stonehold|Siege of Stonehold]], which let the spirit of [[Sharess the Black]] take root in him
- Tore [[Krintak Cinderheart]] in two, then was cleansed of Sharess by [[Cauladra Thiltiss]] — see [[Bathoz's Mindscape]]
- Awarded the great hammer [[Lundhaidr]] and the rank of Dreadclaw at the [[Tulari Hot Springs]]
- Relived [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]]'s battle with the earth titan in the [[Pools of Memorium]]
- Cut the [[Horn of Zargon]] out of [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]]'s arm
- Absorbed [[Chronus]]'s power into [[Queensbane]], attuning it to [[Mechanus]] (the third of six attunements)

## Connections
- [[Kelara]] — sister, freed from [[Jiren's Rock]]
- [[Kuzo Clawborn]] — rival, and the father of [[Kelara]]'s child
- [[Zusa Manefell]] — late mother, encountered in [[The Beastlands]]
- [[Atrios Wyrmslayer]] — previous wielder of [[Lundhaidr]], met in [[Asgard]]
- Judged by [[Kovir]] for over-reliance on his own strength

## Ten years later
Leonin totem barbarian, Dreadclaw; the moral heart of [[The Sons of Thunder]] and the one who wielded [[Queensbane]] against [[Aeralath]].

After the war he returned to his tribe, finished the peace talks with the dragonborn clans, and secured a lasting truce letting them remain near the sacred [[Tulari Hot Springs]] alongside the Leonin. Whether he meant to or not, this inspired [[The Pilgrimage]].

He adopted a son — [[Thol]], a half-orc orphan from [[Whitehall]] whom [[The Sons of Thunder]] met early in their travels, now a member of [[The Good Ones]].
