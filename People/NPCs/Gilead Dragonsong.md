# Gilead Dragonsong

**Race:** Golden dragonborn
**Affiliation:** [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]], [[Temple Trueheart]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
A golden dragonborn of [[Temple Trueheart]] and a member of [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]]. Formerly recorded as Gillead, kept as an alias.

He and [[Aeroth]] fought [[The Good Ones]] first in the order's sparring match, with [[Diggory Psalm]] to join if the party held its own. Gilead was the first of the three to be defeated. See [[The Sparring Match at Temple Trueheart]].
