# Soraya Hawthorne

**Class:** Fighter
**Status:** Alive again, slain by [[Kyron Delinor]], found in [[Ysgard]], resurrected

## Summary
The young guide from [[Mudbend]] who led [[The Sons of Thunder]] through the swamps in the very first adventure. She grew into a capable fighter and reappeared years later at [[Stonehold]], where [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] murdered her with a Disintegrate spell in full view of the party, an act of pure spite aimed at [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]].

The party found her again in [[Ysgard]], where she had spent the intervening months becoming a battle-scarred hero of the afterlife. She vouched for them before [[Kadigus]], which is the only reason they were permitted to remain in the plane.

She agreed to be resurrected on one condition: that the party kill Kyron, and stop him taking any more innocent lives.
