# Hariett Hinkapot

Old friend and love interest of [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] from the [[Hendrel Dyleeri College of Arcane Studies]]. Expelled from the university, she drifted into petty crime and joined the [[Ashes of Kemnan]]. She told [[The Sons of Thunder]] the Saints had been kidnapping people worldwide and shipping them somewhere, the thread that eventually led to [[Silnothas]] and the sacrifices to [[Aeralath]].

Later found captive among the lizardfolk on the [[Unclaimed Isle]], freed, and instrumental in the defeat of [[Anathimus the Abhorrent]].
