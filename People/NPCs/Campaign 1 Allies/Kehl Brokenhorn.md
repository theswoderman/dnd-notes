# Kehl Brokenhorn

Blue dragonborn cleric based in [[Whitehall]]. He revived [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] after [[The Gnoll Contract]], and later diagnosed [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] as being slowly possessed by an evil spirit, pointing [[The Sons of Thunder]] toward a powerful druid, which led them to [[Cauladra Thiltiss]].
