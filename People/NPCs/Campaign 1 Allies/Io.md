# Io

**Race/Class:** Half-orc ranger

Met while investigating wagon attacks west of [[Whitehall]]. Traveled with [[The Sons of Thunder]] through the [[Ashes of Kemnan]] arc and struck the killing blow on one of the two giants who had eaten his family a decade earlier. He left the group on the road to [[Neziri Desert]] to return home and join the war effort against [[Dolghast]].
