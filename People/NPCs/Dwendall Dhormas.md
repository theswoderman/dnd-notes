# Dwendall Dhormas

**Race:** Dwarf, male, ~120
**Class:** Champion fighter
**Status:** Alive, in [[Saltport]]

## Summary
Childhood friend of [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], who originally set out on [[The Pilgrimage]] with him. He survived the bulette attack that killed [[Sven]], [[Clara]] and [[Hansel]], but was badly shaken and stayed in [[Saltport]] after recovering.

Saving Dwendall is the reason [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] made his pact with [[Torm]].

## Connections
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] — childhood friend

He stayed to save enough gold for passage by sea back to [[Hor Garam]], and would neither finish the pilgrimage nor walk the road home. See [[Thalrik's Road to Stonehold]].
