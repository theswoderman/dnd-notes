# Oraecheon Blackspire

**Race:** Drow
**Status:** Alive, in combat with [[The Good Ones]]

## Summary
A [[The Drow|drow]] who attempted to establish a settlement above ground, somewhere in [[Dolghast]] (exact location unrecorded). When he founded his new House of Blackspire on the surface, he adopted the icon of a bull. His twin sons eventually led a mutiny against him. [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|The Laughing Princes constellation]] is named after them.

His name first surfaced in a dream. [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] dreamed of [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|the Laughing Princes constellation]] and came out of it with the name and some connection to [[The Drow]]. His identity was revealed at [[The Abbey]]. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]] and [[The Crypt of Hate]].

He is in the tunnels beneath the abbey, where he had [[Torin Turen]] on an altar and was torturing him. [[The Good Ones]] engaged him there. See [[The Tunnels Beneath the Abbey]].

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
