# Oraecheon Blackspire

**Race:** Drow, later a death knight
**Affiliation:** [[Bane]], as his champion
**Status:** Killed by [[The Good Ones]] beneath [[The Abbey]]. As a death knight he will reform within days

## Summary
A [[The Drow|drow]] prince, the youngest son of one of the great houses (recorded as Baenra), and the champion of [[Bane]]. Many centuries ago, perhaps a millennium, he led a small splinter of [[The Drow|drow]] out of the Underdark city recorded as Menzoveldrin in rebellion against [[Lolth]]. They were no allies of the surface lands, and meant to build a new empire free of [[Lolth]]'s oppression of their sex. His followers, other princes and lesser noblemen of the Underdark, were the Blackspire Brotherhood. In less than a century they mutinied against his hateful and selfish leadership, and apparently sealed him to die alone in a crypt he had built to resemble his home.

> It many centuries ago, perhaps a millennia, that a small splinter of the Drow of fled the depths of Menzoveldrin to rebel against Lolth. They were no allies of the surface lands and sought to craft a new empire free of Lolth’s oppression of their sex. They were lead by the youngest son of one of the great houses (Baenra), Oraecheon Blackspire. I suppose he was not as fit to lead as he believed--in less than a century the Blackspire Brotherhood--his followers, other Princes and lesser noblemen of the Underdark--had mutinied against his hateful and selfish leadership and apparently sealed him to die alone in a crypt he built to resemble his home.

The settlement was above ground, somewhere in [[Dolghast]] (exact location unrecorded). When he founded his new House of Blackspire on the surface, he adopted the icon of a bull. His twin sons eventually led a mutiny against him. [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|The Laughing Princes constellation]] is named after them.

His name first surfaced in a dream. [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] dreamed of [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|the Laughing Princes constellation]] and came out of it with the name and some connection to [[The Drow]]. His identity was revealed at [[The Abbey]]. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]] and [[The Crypt of Hate]].

He held [[Torin Turen]] on an altar in the tunnels beneath the abbey and tortured him there, performing the ritual known as [[The Black Grail]], which consumed part of [[Torin Turen|Torin]]'s soul to turn him into a death knight. [[The Good Ones]] interrupted it. In the fight with [[The Good Ones]] he called a gorgon, [[Quarrelus]], by chanting its name in Undercommon, and transformed into a death knight mid-fight, gaining new abilities, and said the [[The Black Grail|black grail]] was his as he did. He killed [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] before the party defeated him. In his last moments he delivered a promise from [[Bane]] to make a martyr of [[Falstad Firebeard]] and cast power word kill on him, which killed [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]]. [[Quarrelus]] died the moment he did. See [[The Tunnels Beneath the Abbey]] and [[The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire]].

[[Bane]] himself was not at the abbey, only his champion. Death knights reform within one to four days of their death, and [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]] is to deal with him when he does. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
