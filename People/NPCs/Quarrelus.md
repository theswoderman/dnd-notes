# Quarrelus

**Race:** Gorgon
**Affiliation:** Summoned by [[Oraecheon Blackspire]]
**Status:** Dead

## Summary
A gorgon that [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] called with a chant in Undercommon, shouting its name several times during the fight beneath [[The Abbey]]. It arrived in answer and restrained [[Falstad Firebeard]] with its paralysis magic. It died the moment [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] did. See [[The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire]].
