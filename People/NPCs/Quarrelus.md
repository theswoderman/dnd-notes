# Quarrelus

**Race:** Gorgon
**Affiliation:** Summoned by [[Oraecheon Blackspire]]
**Status:** Unknown

## Summary
A gorgon that [[Oraecheon Blackspire]] called with a chant in Undercommon, shouting its name several times during the fight beneath [[The Abbey]]. It arrived in answer and restrained [[Falstad Firebeard]] with its paralysis magic. See [[The Death of Oraecheon Blackspire]].
