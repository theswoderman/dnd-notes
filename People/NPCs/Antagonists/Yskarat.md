# Yskarat

A mad beholder whose lair lay at the bottom of the sea off the coast of [[Zardoba]], guarding the third fragment of the sword. [[The Sons of Thunder]] fought past a kraken to reach him. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] was nearly killed. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] took an [[Orb of Scrying]] from the lair, which became one of the party's most useful tools.
