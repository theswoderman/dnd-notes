# Kyron Delinor

**Also known as:** The Harbinger
**Race:** Elf, born aasimar
**Affiliation:** [[The Black Crown]]
**Status:** Alive

## Appearance
Black armor, helmet adorned with antlers. Beneath it: an elf, pale, black-haired, with pupilless eyes. Rides a great necrotic dragon.

## Summary
For three centuries Kyron was a hero of [[Loradris]] — a sorcerer of enormous power from childhood who took up the paladin's mantle because his people loved him for it. Then his wife [[Vedonia]] and daughter [[Bryn]] fell to an illness even he could not cure, and died. He turned to dark magic to bring them back, was imprisoned for sacrificing innocents in the attempt, and broke out roughly fifty years ago.

He is [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s most persistent enemy and the public face of the [[The Black Crown|Black Crown]].

## Key moments
- Killed [[The Peddler]] with a single spell to keep him silent, then summoned demons to cover his escape
- Confronted the party in [[Xinar]] over [[Princess Kara]], killing [[Umbra]] in front of them
- Shattered the gate of [[Stonehold]] with Earthquake and Meteor Swarm, then murdered [[Soraya Hawthorne]]
- Warned [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] never to mention his family again — and, when goaded a second time, carried out [[The Harbinger's Reprisal]]

## Connections
