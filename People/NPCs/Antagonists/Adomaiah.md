# Adomaiah

**Title:** The Hand of the Ruby

[[Sardior]]'s solar angel, a being of near-godly might, guarding the door to [[Sardior]]'s tower in the [[Ruby Palace]]. When [[The Sons of Thunder]] reached him he was being puppeted by the elder brain ruling the mind flayer colony that had overrun the palace.

Through him the elder brain spoke of "two siblings playing god" — and knew [[Aeralath]] by name, which unsettled the party more than the fight did. Both [[Adomaiah]]and the elder brain were killed; [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] died in the attempt.
