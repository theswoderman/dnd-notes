# Mirna Gribwood

**Race:** Dusk hag, working in the realm of dreams
**Affiliation:** [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]], presumably
**Relation:** [[Beemo]]'s aunt
**Status:** Alive
**Spelling:** "Girbwood" is unconfirmed, recorded uncertainly during the session

## Summary
The haglike woman who has been appearing in [[The Good Ones]]' dreams. [[Beemo]] recognized her as his aunt, a dusk hag who works in the realm of dreams. Since [[Festra]] created and raised [[Beemo]], [[Mirna Gribwood|Mirna]] is her sister in some sense, making the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] a family.

The hags are circle casting the dream spell together, all of them, on the party at once.

She has said two things directly to the party, one on each night:

> "I like this mind of yours, I'll stay a while."

> "See what is born of your bravery, and rejoice your heroes."

In every dream she has appeared in, all six of [[The Good Ones]] are present, except [[Beemo]], who does not see her at all. He is the coven's target and the one member she leaves out.

See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]].

## Known abilities
[[Thol]] used Know Your Enemy on her. She is immune to blinded, charmed and frightened.

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.

## Connections
- [[Beemo]], her nephew and the coven's target
- [[Festra]], the hag who raised [[Beemo]]; the family relation is the obvious link
- [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]], the coven circle casting with her
- [[Torin Turen]], possibly lured out by her or one of her sisters
