# Ogzuuk Torah'kor

**Title:** Lord of War, one of the four [[Vampire Lords]]
**Called by his peers:** The Brute

Held [[Bloodraven Keep]] with hundreds of Red Orcs out of [[Rorinden]]. Rather than see his warriors die needlessly, he emerged and challenged [[The Sons of Thunder]] to honorable single combat, asking only that [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] take his soldiers as his own if he fell.

On the brink of death he bit himself and drank his own blood, driving himself into feral bloodlust. Six against one, he was still terrifying. He fell, and [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] kept the bargain.
