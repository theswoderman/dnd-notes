# Queen Nocticula

Ruler of [[Dolghast]] and the driving force behind the war. [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] first observed her through a scrying orb in conversation with [[Kyron Delinor]], learning of the planned attack on [[Stonehold]].

After [[The Sons of Thunder]] killed [[General Vadahm]], she puppeted the general's corpse on the battlefield to taunt them and inform them the war was far from over.

Her true purpose, uncovered through [[Melvin]], is to feed deaths to [[Aeralath]].
