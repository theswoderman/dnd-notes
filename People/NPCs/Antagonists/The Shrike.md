# The Shrike

A serial killer [[The Sons of Thunder]] passed on the road a month before he became relevant. Hired by [[Melvin]] to assassinate [[Queen Marislova Domeshka]] of [[Grekosha]].

He killed [[Melvin]] (or appeared to) by impaling him on the antler rack of a deer trophy, a detail that puzzled the party until [[Melvin]]'s Clone trick came to light. Killed in the [[Whisperwood]].
