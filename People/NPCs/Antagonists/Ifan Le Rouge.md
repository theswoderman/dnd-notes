# Ifan Le Rouge

**Title:** Lord of Death, one of the four [[Vampire Lords]]
**Status:** Destroyed outside [[Nostra]]

Father of [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and [[Casimir]], husband of [[Chessa]]. A vampire of almost inconceivable power, able to grant warlock magic to his own son, a feat that requires something close to godly strength.

He was not a straightforward monster. He kept [[Chessa]] imprisoned for years because she was dying from having borne two vampire children and refused the "gift" of vampirism; he agreed to meet [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in the open, against his own better judgement, because he loved him. He asked only that his family not have to live in fear of being hunted.

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] attacked anyway. When [[Diana Zethros]] turned [[Chessa]] to ash, [[Ifan Le Rouge|Ifan]] flew into a rage, killed [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and nearly killed [[Diana Zethros]], and was finally impaled through the heart on [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s spear and burned away by the setting sun.

See [[The Death of Ifan Le Rouge]].
