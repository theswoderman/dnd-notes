# Callus Halterstock

High-ranking member of the [[Ashes of Kemnan]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s former employer, the man responsible for getting many of [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s friends hanged.

Fought aboard the prisoner ship in the subterranean docks beneath [[Rothefell]], alongside three elite [[Dolghast]] soldiers, and fled. Later found in the [[Whisperwood]] among [[Melvin]]'s conspirators, and killed there.
