# Mohabi

A devil encountered first as a strange man sitting on the porch of a hut in the jungle of the [[Unclaimed Isle]]. He traded information (including that [[Melvin]] and [[Callus Halterstock]] knew each other very well), then sealed [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] in a Force Cage, stole his Ioun Stone, and plane shifted home to the 500th layer of [[The Abyss]], promising vengeance.

He kept that promise, ambushing [[The Sons of Thunder]] in [[Deepforge]] while holding [[King Fargrim]] captive. That time they killed him.
