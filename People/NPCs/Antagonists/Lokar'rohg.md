# Lokar'rohg

**Race:** Empyrean
**Affiliation:** [[Garagos]]
**Status:** Defeated by [[The Sons of Thunder]], presumably slain

## Summary

The son of [[Garagos]] and his mightiest soldier in the eternal war of [[Warrior's Rest]]. His mother was Mordraka, one of the daughters of [[Annam]], god of giants. With divine giant blood on both sides he stood nearly 200 feet tall.

[[Garagos]] sent him out after [[The Sons of Thunder]] routed much of the black army around the [[Tower of Fate]]. He nearly killed [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]], [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] before the party defeated him, and [[Garagos]] came forward in person to avenge him. See [[The Battle of Warrior's Rest]].
