# Empress Vlaakith the Eighth

The Lich Queen, ruler of the [[Githyanki Empire]] from [[Tu'Narath]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] were sold to her people as slaves by [[Captain Banithi]] and thrown into the combat pits for her entertainment, fighting five waves of monsters before their escape.
