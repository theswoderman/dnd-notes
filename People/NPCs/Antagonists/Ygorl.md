# Ygorl

**Race:** Slaad
**Title:** Slaad Lord of Entropy
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

A Slaad Lord, the god of entropy and death, whose realm lies at the heart of [[Limbo]]'s chaos.

He was in written correspondence with [[Vhal Nimzur]], the lich who ruled the [[Tower of Fate]], and he wears [[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]]'s phylactery on a chain around his neck. As long as the phylactery exists, [[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]] lives on and keeps his hold on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s soul. See [[The Ascent of the Tower of Fate]].

See [[Campaign 1 Loose Ends]] for open questions.
