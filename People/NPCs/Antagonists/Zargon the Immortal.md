# Zargon the Immortal

**Title:** First Lord of Hell
**Type:** Baatorian, one of the beings who ruled hell *before* [[Asmodeus]] and the devils

For most of the campaign this being was known only as [[Zargon the Immortal|Sina'Draiel]], [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s mysterious patron deity. Its true identity was uncovered in the library of the [[Ruby Palace]], in a tome called the *Lexicon Primordia*: the symbol of [[Zargon the Immortal|Sina'Draiel]] appears on a page about [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]].

[[Sardior]] confirmed that not even [[Asmodeus]] could kill [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]]. He could only lock him inside his own horn, on the moon. That horn is the [[Horn of Zargon|Spear of Sina'Draiel]].

How to destroy him: carry the horn to the bottom of the ninth layer of hell and cast it into the flames of the deepest pit.

The [[The Exorcism of Zargon|Exorcism of Zargon]] at the [[House of War]] drove most of him out of [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], but what remains is sealed inside [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s soul. If [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] dies before [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]] is destroyed, his soul is forfeit.
