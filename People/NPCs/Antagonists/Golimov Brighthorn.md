# Prince Golimov Brighthorn

Loxodon cleric and prince of [[Ornuth]], found among [[Melvin]]'s conspirators in the [[Whisperwood]]. He surrendered, arguing that he had only ever wanted to preserve [[Ornuth]]'s independence in the war, and that mass murder was a line too far.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] spared him on the condition that he convince [[Ornuth]]'s rulers to abandon [[Dolghast]] and join the opposing side.
