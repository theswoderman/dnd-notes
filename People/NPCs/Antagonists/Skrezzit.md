# Skrezzit

The devil [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] summoned from the [[Nine Hells of Baator]] to break [[The Sons of Thunder]] out of the prison cells in [[Tu'Narath]]. In exchange for distracting the guards, [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] swore to summon it to the material plane and let it do as it pleased — or forfeit [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s soul.

[[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] honored the letter of the deal: he had [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] fly him thousands of feet into the air, summoned [[Skrezzit]] there, and let it fall.
