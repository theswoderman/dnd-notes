# Captain Banithi

Spelljammer captain who picked [[The Sons of Thunder]] up out of the [[Astral Sea]] and offered to carry them to [[Luxaria]], then drugged them and sold them to the [[Githyanki Empire]] as slaves.

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] took a splinter of wood from [[Captain Banithi]]'s ship, which [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] later used to teleport the party out of [[Tu'Narath]] and back aboard it. They took the vessel for themselves.
