# The Peddler

A murderer responsible for a string of killings across [[Alnoria]], hunted by [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]. Cornered at the mayor's house in [[Runswick]], he escaped through a hidden exit and was run down outside town — only for [[Kyron Delinor]] to kill him with a single spell before he could talk.

His corpse carried the first real clue of the campaign: a manifesto tattooed across his back declaring that death and destruction are an offering to "their matron", alongside the symbol of a **black skull crowned with antlers** — the mark of [[The Black Crown]].

See [[The Hunt for the Peddler]].
