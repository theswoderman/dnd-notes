# Sharess the Black

The ghost of an ancient black dragon. Her spirit entered [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] when he crushed a gem taken from an evil dragonborn wizard to borrow a black dragon's power during the [[The Siege of Stonehold|Siege of Stonehold]], and steadily pushed him toward murderous rage.

[[Cauladra Thiltiss]]'s ritual sent [[The Sons of Thunder]] into [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s mind to fight his fear, lust, pain, envy, greed, sorrow, hatred, doubt, rage and finally pride. When pride fell, Sharess was forced out of his body and slain. See [[Bathoz's Mindscape]].
