# Av'orel

A planetar angel guarding a sealed structure on an island that magically trapped [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s ship en route to the [[Unclaimed Isle]], attended by valkyries and the ghosts of Oathsworn knights.

[[Av'orel]] was loyal to [[Asmodeus]], whom it called "the True Light", and had no knowledge whatsoever of [[Asmodeus]] being a devil or an evil being. The implication is that it had been sealed in that tomb, alone, for several thousand years, since before the fall.

See [[Av'orel's Tomb]].
