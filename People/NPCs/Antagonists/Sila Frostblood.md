# Sila Frostblood

**Nature:** Bheur hag
**Affiliation:** [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
The third member of the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]], and the last of the coven to be named. [[Gegga]] and [[Beemo]] placed her from the party's description of the dream she sent.

She has a decaying face and teeth that hang past her chin. In the dream she stood on the far side of a creek, on a frozen snowbank that opened six red eyes, and held three dire wolves in black spiked metal collars on black chains. She spat at the dreamers and they woke.

The next morning the party reached the riverbank she had shown them. See [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]] and [[The Frozen Riverbank]].

See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
