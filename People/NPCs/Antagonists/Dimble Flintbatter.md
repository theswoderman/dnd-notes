# Dimble Flintbatter

[[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]'s academic rival and an ally of [[Krintak Cinderheart]]. He cast the Clone spell that allowed [[Melvin]] to fake his own death, and died with the rest of the conspirators in the cave by the lake in the [[Whisperwood]].
