# Minthur the Mindbreaker

**Type:** Turquoise hybrid dragon (blue-green)
**Lair:** A cave system near a large waterfall overlooking the [[Dreaming Jungle]], on the border of [[Rorinden]] and [[Isembard]]
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[The Sons of Thunder]])

The third hybrid dragon slain by [[The Sons of Thunder]], after [[Golzokroth]] and [[Rendibril]]. Notes on hybrid dragons came from [[Mathias Ohstal]].

[[Minthur]] breathed a psychedelic gas that dealt psychic damage and used hit-and-run tactics from underwater. A tabaxi druid named [[Viridian Sun]] guarded the approaches to his lair, claiming [[Minthur]] was protecting the [[Dreaming Jungle]] from [[Isembard]] and [[Rorinden]] harvesting its resources.

When forced to flee, [[Minthur]] turned invisible and tried to destroy the city of [[Mushala]]. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] spotted his invisible wingbeats disturbing the trees, and the party ran him down. His hoard was worth over 500,000 gold.
