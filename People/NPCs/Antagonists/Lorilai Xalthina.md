# Lorilai Xalthina

**Title:** Mistress of Rot, one of the four [[Vampire Lords]]

A druid vampire. She halted [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s marching army by singing a haunting druidic song from deep within an ancient graveyard, a song that prevented anyone within its reach from gaining any benefit from food, drink or potions. Destroyed in the crypts.
