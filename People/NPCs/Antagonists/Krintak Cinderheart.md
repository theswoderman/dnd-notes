# Krintak Cinderheart

Dragonborn barbarian who first fought [[The Sons of Thunder]] in the [[The King's Tournament|King's Tournament]] and was an ally of [[Dimble Flintbatter]]. He later led the dragonborn force that wiped out leonin tribes in the [[Golden Plains]] and held [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s people hostage at the [[Tulari Hot Springs]].

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] talked him down rather than killing him, and [[Krintak Cinderheart]] tentatively agreed to set aside his hatred in the face of a coming demonic invasion. The truce did not hold: after the [[The Siege of Stonehold|Siege of Stonehold]], [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]], by then under the influence of [[Sharess the Black]], duelled him again and tore him in two.
