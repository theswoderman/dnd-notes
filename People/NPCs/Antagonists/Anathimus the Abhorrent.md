# Anathimus the Abhorrent

Four-headed [[Yuan-Ti]] high priest at the bottom of the central ziggurat on the [[Unclaimed Isle]]. He held [[Raiorma]]'s mate captive and channelled the amethyst dragon's psychic energy to lift the ziggurat's lowest level into the sky.

Combining that energy with the fragment of the ancient blade in his possession, he tore open a small pink rift to [[The Abyss]] and summoned a marilith. Both he and the demon were killed, and [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] snatched the sword fragment as the floor collapsed.
