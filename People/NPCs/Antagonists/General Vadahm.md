# General Vadahm

Commander of the [[Dolghast]]i army at the [[The Siege of Stonehold|Siege of Stonehold]]. Apparently human, yet alive for well over four hundred years and a terror in every battle he ever fought.

The [[Council of Moradin]] sent [[The Sons of Thunder]] through a hidden mining tunnel to flank the enemy lines and kill him, which they did. [[Queen Nocticula]] then animated his body to speak through it.
