# The Consumed

An ancient aberration summoned by three hags on the [[Isle of Eismir]] after [[King Oberin Steeleborn]]'s hunting party killed their sister.

It ate the king, took his form, and sat on the throne of [[Silverwell]], sowing misery and slowly consuming more dwarves and replacing them, one by one. This was the true reason [[Vallenhein]] sealed its borders and expelled the Alnorian ambassador.

Killed in the throne room during the civil war, but not before it killed [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]].
