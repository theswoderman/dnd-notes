# Chronus

**Race:** Modron (Secundi)
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[The Sons of Thunder]])

## Summary

One of four Secundi who report directly to [[Primus]] in [[Mechanus]], governing the aspect of time. [[Chronus]] went rogue and took a quarter of the Modron army with him. He believed he could turn back time to ensure [[Aeralath]]'s defeat, but doing so would have torn reality apart.

He took refuge in the far reaches of [[Mechanus]], past the Wastes of Gravegear. [[The Sons of Thunder]], aided by the formian warrior [[Theh Kha]], tracked him there and killed him in a crumbling clock tower. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] was nearly killed by his 9th-level Time Ravage. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] absorbed his power into [[Queensbane]], attuning it to [[Mechanus]] (the third of six planes).

In [[Chronus]]'s visions of alternate timelines, each party member saw a version of their life where their defining choices went differently.

## Connections

- [[Primus]], his creator and the ruler he rebelled against
- [[The Sons of Thunder]], killed him
- [[Theh Kha]], formian warrior who fought against him
- [[Queensbane]], his power attuned the blade to [[Mechanus]]
- [[Aeralath]], his plan was to reverse time to prevent her return
