# The Unnamed Cleric

**Race:** Dwarf
**Class:** Cleric
**Affiliation:** [[The Five Heroes]]
**Status:** Alive (resurrected)

## Summary
One of [[The Five Heroes]] who fought [[Aeralath]] during [[The Sundering]], roughly ten thousand years ago. She died in the fight. Her name has been lost entirely. The three fallen heroes were brought back to life by [[The Sons of Thunder]] using a true wish at the end of Campaign 1, but her name was not recovered.
