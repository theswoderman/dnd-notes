# Nigel

**Status:** Alive (died before the campaign; restored by [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]])
**Home:** [[Blubberpike]]

## Summary

The brother of [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]. He died before the events of Campaign 1, and [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] was involved in his death, a guilt [[Kovir]] later judged him for. [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] did not speak to their parents again until the eve of the march on [[Dolghast]].

With their parents' agreement, [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] brought him back with True Resurrection in [[Blubberpike]]. He immediately punched [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] in the nuts, then forgave him. See [[Nigel's Resurrection]].
