# Naori

Orphaned leonin girl living in [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s [[Whitehall]] mansion. Her missing father [[Morbach]] was found on the [[Unclaimed Isle]] and sent home to her, only to be killed and raised as undead by [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] months later, then restored.
