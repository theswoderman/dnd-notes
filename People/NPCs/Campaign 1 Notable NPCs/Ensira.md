# Ensira

**Race:** Gith'zerai
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

A Gith'zerai woman, originally from [[Limbo]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] first encountered her as the sole survivor of an airship that fell out of a deep purple rift and crashed into the forest near [[Dogodr]]. Her crew had been transporting a mind flayer when they were attacked. They had just come from Pandemonium, and she plane shifted home to [[Limbo]].

The party met her again months later in [[Limbo]], when her group rescued them from the belly of Kra-Thoon, a massive worm-like creature. She brought them to Lu'Un'Dai, the Gith'zerai capital city.

## Key moments

- Rescued from a [[Githyanki Empire|Githyanki]] ship by [[The Sons of Thunder]] en route to the [[Golden Plains]] — see [[The Siege of Stonehold]]
- Rescued the party from Kra-Thoon in [[Limbo]] — see [[Into Limbo]]
