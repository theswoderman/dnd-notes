# Ensira

A gith woman, sole survivor of an airship that fell out of a deep purple rift and crashed into the forest near [[Dogodr]]. Her crew had been transporting a mind flayer when they were attacked. They had just come from Pandemonium, but were originally from [[Limbo]] — a realm of pure chaos and wild magic — and she plane shifted home.
