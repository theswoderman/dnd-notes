# Cauladra Thiltiss

Archdruid of [[Loradris]]. She guided [[The Sons of Thunder]] partway to the [[Eldergleam Tree]] and owed them a favor afterwards.

She called that favor in when [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] began to be possessed: she led them to a forest altar and performed the ritual that sent the party's consciousness into [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s mind to drive out [[Sharess the Black]]. See [[Bathoz's Mindscape]].
