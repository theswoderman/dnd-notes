# Zalema

Village elder of the Viera hidden in the [[Whisperwood]]. She explained that the young and elderly of her people were dying after drinking from the lake ([[Melvin]]'s poison), and it was she who revealed that [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s birth name was [[Loryn Malcovitch|Anyil]] and that the scout [[Lien]] was his mother.
