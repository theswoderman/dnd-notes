# Grand Magister Elyon Ivasil

Grand Magister of the [[Arcanus Sanctum]] and widely regarded as the most powerful wizard in the world. Former master of [[Kyron Delinor]].

He gave [[The Sons of Thunder]] the full account of [[The Sundering]]: the interplanar gates, the demon horde, the forging of [[Queensbane]], and the severing of the outer planes. He also granted them access to teleportation circles across the world, which transformed how the campaign moves.

He lent [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] his Robes of the Archmagi for the hunt for [[Golzokroth]], who killed several of his closest friends, including [[Mehea Talth]]'s father, forty years ago.
