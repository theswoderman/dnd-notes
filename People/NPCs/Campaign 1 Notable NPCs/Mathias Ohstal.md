# Mathias Ohstal

Owner of the museum of draconic history in [[Whitehall]]. He explained that [[Golzokroth]] is a hybrid dragon, part red, part black, and that other hybrids of different color combinations exist, though they keep hidden to avoid becoming the target of some grand hunt.

His notes on the hybrid dragons guided [[The Sons of Thunder]] to [[Minthur]] the Mindbreaker, a turquoise hybrid laired near a waterfall overlooking the Dreaming Jungle. Of the hybrids, [[The Sons of Thunder]] killed three: [[Golzokroth]], [[Rendibril]], and [[Minthur]].
