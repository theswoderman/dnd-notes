# Lyra Sunstrider

**Race:** Tiefling
**Class:** Druid (Circle of the Moon)
**Affiliation:** [[The Five Heroes]]
**Status:** Alive (resurrected)

## Summary
One of [[The Five Heroes]] who fought [[Aeralath]] during [[The Sundering]], roughly ten thousand years ago. She died in the fight. Her name was lost for millennia until [[The Sons of Thunder]] used a true wish to bring the three fallen heroes back to life at the end of Campaign 1.
