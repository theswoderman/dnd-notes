# Atrios Wyrmslayer

Leonin barbarian, one of the three einherjar guarding the bridge to [[Asgard]], and the **original wielder of [[Lundhaidr]]**. His warriors liberated [[Stonehold]] from an earth titan nearly four hundred years ago.

[[The Sons of Thunder]] relived that exact battle in the [[Pools of Memorium]] — fighting the titan beneath [[Stonehold]] in Atrios's place. That is how [[Queensbane]] was attuned to [[Ysgard]].
