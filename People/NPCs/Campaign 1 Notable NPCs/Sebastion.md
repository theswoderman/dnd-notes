# Sebastion

The oldest of the orphans living in [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s [[Whitehall]] mansion, a young tiefling sorcerer who usually boards at the local magic university, and happened to be home the night [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] came.

He set the undead [[Morbach]] ablaze with his own spell, almost certainly saving every child in the house, including [[Naori]].

He blames [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and the party. In his words, they may be saving the world, but they leave a wake of destruction behind them.
