# Warmaster Hayden Trask

Highest-ranking member of the church of [[The Red Lady]], based at the [[House of War]] in [[Gildurn]].

He devised the exorcism-and-binding ceremony intended to remove [[Zargon the Immortal]] from [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]], knowing full well he could not fully banish an elder evil, hence the Imprisonment spell meant to seal the remainder. He survived [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s [[Zargon the Immortal|Zargon]]-driven rampage and regrew [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s severed arm with Regeneration.

See [[The Exorcism of Zargon]].
