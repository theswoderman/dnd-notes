# Kovir

Guardian of the [[Valley of First Life]] at the foot of [[Heaven's Altar]], and a lightning-fast monk.

He showed each of [[The Sons of Thunder]] a vision of what he judged to be their greatest flaw, and questioned their resolve:

| Character                          | Judged for                                        |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Davynn Brindleknot\|Davynn]]     | Regret and shame over his brother's death         |
| [[Bathoz Manefell\|Bathoz]]        | Over-reliance on his own strength                 |
| [[Loryn Malcovitch\|Loryn]]        | Overindulgence in physical desire                 |
| [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge\|Jakel]] | Stubbornness, and willingness to sacrifice others |
| [[Diggory Psalm\|Diggory]]         | Fear of losing his friends again                  |

They faced the trials, climbed for days, and beat him in a final test of combat, earning the right to pass through the astral gate at the summit.
