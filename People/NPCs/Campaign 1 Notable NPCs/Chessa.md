# Chessa

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s mother. She had been dying for years from having carried two vampire children to term; the only cure was to become a vampire herself, and she refused. [[Ifan Le Rouge]] locked her away and kept her alive until she would accept.

[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] had believed she was a prisoner — which she was, though not for the reason he thought. He knew that killing his father meant killing her too, and attacked anyway. [[Diana Zethros]] turned her to ash.
