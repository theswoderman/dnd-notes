# Ahmeses

An androsphinx guarding the mural and the sixteen-sided gem in the sanctum beyond the lost [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] capital.

He named the symbol from [[The Peddler]]'s back as the mark of [[The Black Crown]], and explained why the mural is so alarming: it shows demons united under a single banner. Demons are chaotic and serve only themselves; demon lords like Orcus and Demogorgon never cooperate. Only a single demon of immense power could command such an army.

He gave [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] radiant magic before sending [[The Sons of Thunder]] on their way.
