# Sorith Menyar-Ag

**Race:** Gith'zerai
**Title:** God-King of the Gith'zerai
**Status:** Alive, in stasis

## Summary

The God-King of the Gith'zerai, who rules from a palace in [[Lu'Un'Dai]] in [[Limbo]]. He is the most powerful psionic "mortal" being in the known universe. He floats in constant stasis, almost mummified, neither living nor dead nor asleep, and his voice booms directly in the minds of those before him. The sheer pressure of his presence forced even [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] to his knees.

When [[The Sons of Thunder]] came asking to be sent to [[Warrior's Rest]], he talked down to them, asking why they did not simply hand [[Queensbane]] to a being of his wisdom and power. [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] stood and talked back, and the God-King was impressed by his pride. He agreed to aid the party, swore to bring the full strength of the Gith'zerai when the final battle came, and teleported them across [[Limbo]].

His parting words: "Zerthimon does not expect us to wait in patience for his return. Instead, we must pave the way for his efforts, so that we can hasten the coming of our golden age."
