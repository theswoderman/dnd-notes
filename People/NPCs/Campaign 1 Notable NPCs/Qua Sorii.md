# Qua Sorii

**Race:** Mercane
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

A powerful interplanar merchant, a sort of black market dealer in magic items. He knows many secret ways in and out of the planes and has contacts across the universe. [[The Sons of Thunder]] met him in the [[Astral Sea]], where he gave them a business card that summons him when called upon.

[[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] used the card after [[The War Council at Gildurn]], when the party needed a faster road to the lower levels of the [[Nine Hells of Baator|Nine Hells]] than the planar gate in [[Dolghast]]. [[Qua Sorii]] appeared within moments and said he knew of a way he could potentially get them into the seventh layer. He needed time to set it up.
