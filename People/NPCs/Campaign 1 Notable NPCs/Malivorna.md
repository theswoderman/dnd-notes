# Malivorna

**Race:** Night hag / Archfey
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[The Sons of Thunder]])

## Summary
Daughter of Baba Yaga and one of the [[Wild Hunt]]'s four lieutenants. She ambushed [[The Sons of Thunder]] on their way to [[Ursis]], summoning two demons and a shadow clone of [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]. The party killed her and recovered the fabled [[Demonomicon of Malivorna]], which they knew they would need for [[The Abyss]].
