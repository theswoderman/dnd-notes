# Mazaïd

A djinn met during [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s accidental detour to the Elemental Plane of Air, who returned with them to the [[Ruby Palace]]. He had visited the palace decades earlier — [[Sardior]] was a known friend of the Grand Caliph of the Djinn, Husam al-Balil ben Nafhat al-Yugayyim.
