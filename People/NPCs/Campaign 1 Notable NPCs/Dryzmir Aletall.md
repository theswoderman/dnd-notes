# Dryzmir Aletall

**Race:** Drow
**Class:** Ranger
**Affiliation:** [[The Five Heroes]], [[The Einherjar]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
One of [[The Five Heroes]] who fought [[Aeralath]] during [[The Sundering]], roughly ten thousand years ago, and one of only two survivors alongside [[Vhal Nimzur]]. He became one of [[The Einherjar]] in [[Asgard]], and was one of the three who guarded the rainbow bridge when [[The Sons of Thunder]] sought entry.

After his defeat on the bridge he revealed who he was. Ten thousand years on, he could no longer remember the names of the three who died: [[Bjorn Sigurdson]], [[Lyra Sunstrider]], and [[The Unnamed Cleric|a dwarf cleric whose name has been lost entirely]].

He remembers [[Aeralath]] only in fragments, and those fragments frighten him: she could be in more than one place at once, and could be whatever she wished at any moment. No preparation would be enough. [[Queensbane]] limited her omnipotence just enough that she could be harmed.
