# Dryzmir Aletall

Drow elf ranger, one of the three einherjar who barred the rainbow bridge into [[Asgard]].

After his defeat he revealed something extraordinary: he was **one of the five mortal heroes who fought [[Aeralath]]** nearly ten thousand years ago, alongside the first wielder of [[Queensbane]]. Only he and [[Vhal Nimzur]] survived.

He remembers her only in fragments, and those fragments frighten him: she could be in more than one place at once, and could be whatever she wished at any moment. No preparation would be enough. [[Queensbane]] limited her omnipotence just enough that she could be harmed.
