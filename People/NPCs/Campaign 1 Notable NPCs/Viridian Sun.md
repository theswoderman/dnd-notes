# Viridian Sun

**Race:** Tabaxi
**Class:** Druid
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[The Sons of Thunder]])

## Summary

A tabaxi druid living in the caves near [[Minthur]]'s lair in the [[Dreaming Jungle]]. He claimed [[Minthur]] was guarding the jungle from being harvested for resources by the [[Isembard]] and [[Rorinden]] governments. [[The Sons of Thunder]] fought and killed him.

His belongings revealed he was the older brother of [[Jade Moon]], a tabaxi bard the party knew. They never told her what happened.
