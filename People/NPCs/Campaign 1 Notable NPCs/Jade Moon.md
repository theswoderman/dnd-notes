# Jade Moon

**Race:** Tabaxi
**Class:** Bard
**Status:** Alive

A tabaxi bard known to [[The Sons of Thunder]]. Her older brother [[Viridian Sun]] was a druid guarding the approaches to [[Minthur]]'s lair in the [[Dreaming Jungle]]. The party killed him during [[The Slaying of Minthur]] and discovered his identity from his belongings. They never told her.
