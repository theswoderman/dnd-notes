# Vhal Nimzur

A [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] elf wizard, and one of the five mortal heroes who drove [[Aeralath]] back into [[The Abyss]]. One of only two survivors, alongside [[Dryzmir Aletall]].

[[The Sons of Thunder]] already knew the name: he was the personal understudy of [[Sardior]], as they learned in the [[Ruby Palace]].
