# Vhal Nimzur

**Race:** [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]]
**Class:** Wizard
**Affiliation:** [[The Five Heroes]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
A [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] wizard, and one of [[The Five Heroes]] who drove [[Aeralath]] back into [[The Abyss]] during [[The Sundering]]. One of only two survivors, alongside [[Dryzmir Aletall]].

[[The Sons of Thunder]] already knew the name: he was the personal understudy of [[Sardior]], as they learned in the [[Ruby Palace]].
