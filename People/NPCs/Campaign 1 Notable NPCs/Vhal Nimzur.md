# Vhal Nimzur

**Race:** [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]]
**Class:** Wizard
**Affiliation:** [[The Five Heroes]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

The highest-ranking member of the mageocracy that ruled [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] society. One of [[The Five Heroes]] who drove [[Aeralath]] back into [[The Abyss]] during [[The Sundering]], and one of only two survivors, alongside [[Dryzmir Aletall]]. He was the personal understudy of [[Sardior]], as [[The Sons of Thunder]] learned in the [[Ruby Palace]].

Before the destruction of the [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]], [[Vhal Nimzur|Vhal]] devised an experiment to preserve a chosen few of his people by transferring their souls into constructed bodies. He knew the experiment would further anger the gods and likely draw divine judgment, but believed the preservation of the [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] was worth it. These survivors became the Reborn (also called the Awoken), who lay dormant on a hidden continent for nearly ten thousand years until [[Queensbane]]'s power began to wake them. See [[The Hidden Continent]].
