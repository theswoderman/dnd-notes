# Rendibril

**Type:** Purple dragon (red/blue hybrid), ancient
**Breath weapon:** Force beam
**Lair:** Mountains south of [[Dragon's Pass]] on the [[Loradris]]/[[Rorinden]] border
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[Golzokroth]], 1017 A.P.)

Sister of [[Golzokroth]]. [[Elyon Ivasil]] pointed [[The Sons of Thunder]] toward her.

Her price for helping kill her brother was the destruction of the cult that had grown up around her and bored her: the cult led by [[Kru'dahs]]. She joined the assault on the volcano in the [[Blackwaste]] and died there alongside [[Golzokroth]], after the party maneuvered him into focusing his attacks on her.
