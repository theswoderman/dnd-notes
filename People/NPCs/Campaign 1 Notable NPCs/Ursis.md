# Ursis

**Race:** Deity (god of werebears)
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
Good-hearted god of werebears, dwelling in Balador within [[The Beastlands]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] sought his aid to set up a defensible position against the [[Wild Hunt]]. With his werebears and three Otter River Deities, they staged a trap in a narrow canyon. [[Ursis]] himself drew the attention of [[Prascilia]] during the battle.
