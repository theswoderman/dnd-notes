# Captain Stella Enrulon

Captain of the [[Blackwater Jewel]], apparently an accomplished astral elf.

She is in truth a **lunar dragon**, cursed with madness by a druid she killed many years ago. The curse took her in the [[Silvered Nebulas]]: she transformed and attacked her own passengers. [[Bürd]] convinced [[The Sons of Thunder]] to save rather than kill her, and [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] dispelled the curse with holy magic.

She shielded the party with her wings when the living spellstorm went supernova, and was badly wounded doing it. She and her crew chose to remain on the material plane afterwards, wanting to learn to sail a "strange blue sea of water".
