# Morbach

A leonin found in [[Siren's Rest]], who turned out to be the missing father of [[Naori]], one of the orphans living in [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s [[Whitehall]] mansion. The party sent him home to her.

During [[The Harbinger's Reprisal]], [[Kyron Delinor]] killed him with a spell and raised his corpse as undead to finish off the children. [[Sebastion]] burned the undead [[Morbach]] before it could. Restoring him required True Resurrection and 25,000 gold in diamonds, which [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] paid.
