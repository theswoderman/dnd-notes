# Astynom Alpha 009

**Race:** Marut (lawful construct)
**Status:** Unknown (defeated, not confirmed destroyed)

## Summary

A Marut, an ultra-powerful lawful construct loyal only to [[Primus]], guarding the gate to [[Mechanus]] in [[Gudjag Canyon]]. A withered red orc priestess of Gruumsh mistakenly believed it was the "Eye of Gruumsh," her people's great champion.

The Marut demanded [[The Sons of Thunder]] surrender their weapons, including [[Queensbane]], before entering [[Mechanus]]. When they refused, it knocked [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] unconscious and teleported them both away to a Modron prison. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] was eventually released to his companions.
