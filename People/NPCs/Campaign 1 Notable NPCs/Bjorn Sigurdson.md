# Bjorn Sigurdson

**Race:** Human
**Class:** Barbarian
**Affiliation:** [[The Five Heroes]]
**Status:** Alive (resurrected)

## Summary
One of [[The Five Heroes]] who fought [[Aeralath]] during [[The Sundering]], roughly ten thousand years ago, and the original wielder of [[Queensbane]]. He died in the fight. His name was lost for millennia until [[The Sons of Thunder]] used a true wish to bring the three fallen heroes back to life at the end of Campaign 1.
