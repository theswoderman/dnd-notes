# Myreth

**Race:** Agathion (elf-like celestial)
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
An agathion met in [[Signpost]] in [[The Beastlands]]. Opalescent color-changing skin, glowing green eyes, a faint rainbow halo, neither man nor woman. When Guardian tree creatures attacked [[Signpost]], [[Myreth]] performed a ritual to calm the spirits causing them to go rampant while [[The Sons of Thunder]] held the Guardians off. [[Myreth]] explained that [[The Beastlands]] was being desecrated by something powerful and directed the party to [[Jungle Sovereign]] in Brux.
