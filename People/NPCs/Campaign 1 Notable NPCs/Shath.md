# Shath

**Race:** Gith'zerai
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

A general in the Gith army and a secret disciple of [[The Red Lady]]. He approached [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in [[Lu'Un'Dai]] with word that [[The Red Lady]] wanted to speak with him in her tower in [[Warrior's Rest]], and that only [[Sorith Menyar-Ag|the God-King]] could send the party there. See [[Into Limbo]].
