# Vilerrus

**Race:** Elf / Archfey
**Status:** Alive (fled)

## Summary
An elven man with fiery green eyes who commanded a pack of spectral wolves. One of the [[Wild Hunt]]'s four lieutenants. He led forces into the canyon ambush alongside [[Prascilia]], but was forced to flee when the River Deities flooded the canyon and the werebears attacked.
