# King Fargrim

Son of [[King Oberin Steeleborn]] and now King of [[Vallenhein]]. It was Fargrim who told [[The Sons of Thunder]] about his father's changed behavior after the trip to the [[Isle of Eismir]], and Fargrim who fought beside them in the throne room against [[The Consumed]].

On taking the throne he swore that when war came, [[Vallenhein]] would come to [[Alnoria]]'s aid — a promise he has kept. He was forging armor from [[Golzokroth]]'s hide for [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in [[Deepforge]] when [[Mohabi]] took him captive; the party rescued him.
