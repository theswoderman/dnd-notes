# Queen Marislova Domeshka

Queen of [[Grekosha]]. [[Melvin]] hired [[The Shrike]] to assassinate her — [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] suspected his father wanted the throne of [[Grekosha]] vacant.

She was instead held captive in her own palace in [[Baristos]] by [[Onias Kane]]. After her rescue, with every ranking officer of the Grekoshan military dead or undead, she named [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] **[[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Raven Lord]]**, High General of the armies of [[Grekosha]].
