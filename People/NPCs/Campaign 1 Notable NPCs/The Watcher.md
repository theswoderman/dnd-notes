# The Watcher

A mysterious spider-like woman who oversees the thread of fate, encountered inside the [[Tower of Fate]] in [[Warrior's Rest]].

She showed [[The Sons of Thunder]] their own threads in the great loom of the universe, all being pulled together by a single greater pink thread. Pink is the color of [[Aeralath]]'s portals, and the party took it as proof that she had been trying to influence their fates from the beginning. See [[The Ascent of the Tower of Fate]].
