# Theh Kha

**Race:** Formian (ant-being)
**Status:** Alive (exploring the material plane)
**Affiliation:** Formerly [[Chitinousa|Queen Regent Chitinousa]]'s hive in [[Mechanus]]

## Summary

One of [[Chitinousa|Queen Regent Chitinousa]]'s finest warriors, sent to aid [[The Sons of Thunder]] against [[Chronus]]. He wields a wrist-mounted crossbow that [[Primus]] enhanced to artifact-level power after the battle.

In the visions [[Chronus]] showed of alternate timelines, [[Theh Kha]]'s other self waited for the traditional age to begin his warrior's trial, was wounded in a hive war, and lived out his days tending larvae.

After [[Chronus]] was defeated, [[Theh Kha]] asked to come to the material plane, tired of hive life. The party returned through the gate at [[Gudjag Canyon]] and he set off on his own.

## Connections

- [[Chitinousa|Queen Regent Chitinousa]] — his queen
- [[Primus]] — enhanced his crossbow to artifact-level power
- [[The Sons of Thunder]] — fought alongside them
- [[Chronus]] — helped defeat him
