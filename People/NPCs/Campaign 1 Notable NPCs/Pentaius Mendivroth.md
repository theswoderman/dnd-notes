# Pentaius Mendivroth

An intelligent but awkward crystal dragonborn guide met in [[Luxaria]]. He had mapped every recent sighting of the [[Ruby Palace]] and calculated, from its trajectory, where it should be, a two-month journey.

He was the only member of the expedition not aboard the [[Blackwater Jewel]] when the spellstorm exploded, and did not survive.
