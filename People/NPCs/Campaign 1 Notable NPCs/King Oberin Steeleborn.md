# King Oberin Steeleborn

King of [[Vallenhein]]. He went hunting on the [[Isle of Eismir]], quarrelled with a hag and killed her, and her three sisters killed his guards and summoned [[The Consumed]].

[[The Consumed]] ate him, took his shape, and returned to [[Silverwell]] wearing his face. The "king" who closed the borders and expelled the Alnorian ambassador was never Oberin at all.

Father of [[King Fargrim]].
