# Captain Magus Trollbeater

Dwarven captain at [[Stonehold]] who recruited [[The Sons of Thunder]] to defend the western wall against [[Dolghast]], and who identified [[General Vadahm]] as the enemy commander.

## Ten years later
A [[Bronze Rams|Bronze Ram]], and the old mentor of [[Brom Stonefist]]. [[Brom Stonefist|Brom]] urged [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] to find him in [[Stonehold]] for a place to stay — **Thalrik has not managed to find him.**

> Spelling unresolved: the vault has **Magus**, the prelude has **Maygus**.
