# Lady Diana Zethros

Grand General of the [[Rorinden Empire]] and a sworn Red Knight. A friend of [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s before she became something more.

She marched with him on the crusade to clear [[Grekosha]] of vampires, turned [[Chessa]] to ash outside [[Nostra]] (which sent [[Ifan Le Rouge]] into the rage that killed [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]), and was nearly killed herself in that fight.

It was Zethros who found [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in the snow afterwards, and whose Oath of the Red Knight he took in place of his abandoned oath of vengeance.
