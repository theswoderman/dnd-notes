# Prascilia

**Race:** Elf / Archfey (winged)
**Status:** Alive (fled)

## Summary
A winged elf archer and one of the [[Wild Hunt]]'s four lieutenants. She led a force into the canyon ambush alongside [[Vilerrus]]. She proved extremely dangerous, firing deadly arrows from hundreds of feet in the air. It took [[Ursis]] the werebear god to draw her attention away from the party, who fled to Karasuthra.
