# Lurue

**Race:** Deity (goddess of unicorns)
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
Goddess of unicorns and intelligent beasts, residing in the third layer of [[The Beastlands]], Karasuthra. The [[Wild Hunt]] was hunting her. If she were killed, it could mean the loss of intelligence for all creatures of [[The Beastlands]].

She healed [[The Sons of Thunder]] to full strength and stood with them in the final battle against the Erdking. After his death, she gave a great deal of her divine power to attune [[Queensbane]] to [[The Beastlands]], bringing the sword to two of six attunements.

## Connections
- [[The Sons of Thunder]] (ally)
- [[Queensbane]] (attuned it to [[The Beastlands]])
- [[Cerrunos]] (hunted by him)
