# Raiorma

An ancient amethyst dragon who rules a lizardfolk village on the [[Unclaimed Isle]] in disguise. She asked [[The Sons of Thunder]] to find her mate, who had vanished investigating the [[Yuan-Ti]] city to the north.

They found him captive beneath the ziggurat of [[Anathimus the Abhorrent]], and she rewarded them with a powerful bow for [[Mehea Talth]] and her friendship. Her village later served as the party's staging point for the journey to [[The Beastlands]].
