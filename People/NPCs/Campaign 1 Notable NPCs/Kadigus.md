# Kadigus

**Title:** Champion of Light

Informal leader of the first village [[The Sons of Thunder]] reached in [[Ysgard]]. He explained that the plane is reserved for heroes who died honorably and were carried there by valkyries, and that living trespassers must leave or be killed.

[[Soraya Hawthorne]]'s intervention — asking the hall how many present had slain a dragon, then two, then a greatwyrm, a beholder, four vampires, a demon, an elder brain — is what changed his mind.
