# Nugasu

An ancient celestial tiger-like dragon guarding the long-dormant interplanar gate beneath the [[Tulari Hot Springs]]. First met while [[The Sons of Thunder]] were sneaking through the caves to free [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s people; he fought alongside them against the dragonborn.

That gate turned out to lead to [[Ysgard]]. He warned the party of danger ahead and let them pass.
