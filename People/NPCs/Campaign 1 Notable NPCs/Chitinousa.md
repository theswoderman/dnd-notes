# Chitinousa

**Race:** Formian (ant-being)
**Status:** Alive
**Title:** The Hive Mother, Queen Regent

## Summary

Queen Regent of a formian hive in [[Mechanus]]. Her throne sits at the bottom of an inverted tower-like termite mound structure hanging thousands of feet below a giant gear, past the Wastes of Gravegear.

She gave [[The Sons of Thunder]] her blessing, commanded her warriors to clear a path through [[Chronus]]'s rogue modrons, and sent [[Theh Kha]] to fight alongside them.
