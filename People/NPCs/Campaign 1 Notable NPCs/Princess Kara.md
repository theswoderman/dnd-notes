# Princess Kara

Daughter of [[King Berrigan]]. Kidnapped during her own birthday celebration in [[Whitehall]]; the trail led through [[Xinar]] in [[Isembard]] to [[Umbra]], and through [[Umbra]] to [[Kyron Delinor]]. Recovered and returned home.
