# King Berrigan

Ruler of [[Alnoria]], seated in [[Whitehall]]. He rewarded [[The Sons of Thunder]] for stopping [[The Peddler]], watched them win the [[The King's Tournament|King's Tournament]], and sent them to [[Vallenhein]] to find out why the border had closed.

On the evidence they brought back from the [[Ashes of Kemnan]] hideout he formally declared war on [[Dolghast]] and its allies. He later knighted every member of the party and granted them property in [[Whitehall]] — the mansion that would become their home base.

Father of [[Princess Kara]].
