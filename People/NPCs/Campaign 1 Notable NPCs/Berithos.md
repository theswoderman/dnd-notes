# Berithos

A strange extraplanar being met in the ruins beneath the [[Neziri Desert]], who accompanied [[The Sons of Thunder]] through the labyrinth of undead and identified the pristine city at its center as the lost capital of the [[Nezerai Empire]] — the 9,000-year-extinct desert elves rumored to be the first civilisation in all of [[Estrana]].
