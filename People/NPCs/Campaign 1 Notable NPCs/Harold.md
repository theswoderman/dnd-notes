# Harold

Caretaker of the orphans in [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s [[Whitehall]] mansion. Cut down by [[Kyron Delinor]] along with his sister [[Patrice]], and later resurrected by [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]].

He wanted to leave afterwards but could not bring himself to abandon the children. He did ask for a raise.
