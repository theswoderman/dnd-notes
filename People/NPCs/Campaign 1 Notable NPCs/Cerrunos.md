# Cerrunos

**Race:** Archfey / God
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[The Sons of Thunder]])

## Summary
An immense Archfey warrior who led the [[Wild Hunt]] into [[The Beastlands]] to hunt and pillage. He killed [[Nobanian]], god of lions and great cats, and was hunting [[Lurue]], goddess of unicorns. He held the heart of Yggdrasil hostage and threatened to burn the tree and destroy the plane.

He challenged [[The Sons of Thunder]] to a 24-hour hunt: evade capture and the Hunt would leave forever, or be caught and surrender [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s sword [[Razor's Grace]] and [[Darrius]]. The party chose to fight. Over the 24 hours they killed three of his four lieutenants ([[Malivorna]], [[Gaidus]], and later cornered [[Vilerrus]]).

In the final stand in a temple [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] created, the Erdking was forced to reveal his true form: Cerrunos, god of hunting and savagery. Despite his overwhelming power, [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] struck the killing blow with True Telekinesis. His death broke the curse on [[Darrius]].
