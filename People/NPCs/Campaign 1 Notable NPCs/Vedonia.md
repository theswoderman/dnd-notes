# Vedonia

Wife of [[Kyron Delinor]], dead of a mysterious illness he could not cure. Her name, learned from the elves of [[Thalonir]], has since been used twice by [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] to enrage [[Kyron Delinor|the Harbinger]] — the second time with catastrophic consequences.
