# Lien

The Viera scout who emerged from the trees to lead [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] to the hidden village in the [[Whisperwood]]. She is his birth mother.

As [[The Sons of Thunder]] left, [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] spotted two more Viera watching from the branches: a woman his own age, and an older male who bore a striking resemblance to him.
