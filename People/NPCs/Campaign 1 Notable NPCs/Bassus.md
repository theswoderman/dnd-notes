# Bassus

**Race:** Ape (divine/celestial beast)
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
The Ape King of [[Jungle Sovereign]] in Brux, the second layer of [[The Beastlands]]. He convened a gathering to discuss the [[Wild Hunt]]'s threat. He proposed penning everyone inside [[Jungle Sovereign]] to force the Hunt to attack on their terms, but other animals reported that [[Lurue]] was being hunted and that the Hunt held the heart of Yggdrasil hostage.
