# Darrius

**Race:** Unknown (appears as a black cat; true form is an Archfey)
**Affiliation:** [[The Sons of Thunder]] (companion), the Feywild
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
An intelligent talking black cat who joined [[The Sons of Thunder]] near [[Mudbend]] early in the campaign. Under every spell the party tested, he appeared to genuinely be a cat who had been one for decades, with no memory of anything before. Clerics and wizards guessed it might be a curse or an enhanced familiar. He rode on [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]]'s shoulder, then lived with the orphans at the [[Whitehall]] mansion. He asked to come along to [[The Beastlands]], sensing it was familiar.

When [[The Sons of Thunder]] killed the Erdking, the curse broke. Darrius was revealed to be Abrovand, the King of Fools, the Jester in Red Green and Gold, Archfey of Trickery and Illusions. He had been a friend of the Erdking, but when the Erdking first spoke of hunting and pillaging [[The Beastlands]] fifty years ago, Abrovand knew it was wrong. He stole the crystal blade [[Razor's Grace]] and lured the Erdking to the material plane, where he gave the sword to a kind man who helped him: Johan Malcovitch, [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]]'s grandfather (who claimed he won it in a duel). The Erdking caught up and cursed him into cat form.

After his identity was restored, Abrovand swore to aid [[The Sons of Thunder]] always and promised to return to [[Whitehall]] regularly as Darrius to check on the orphans.
