# Qadus Ifnir

**Race:** Human
**Status:** Dead (killed by [[The Sons of Thunder]])

## Summary

An elderly wizard and the previous owner of [[The Sons of Thunder]]'s mansion in [[Whitehall]]. He disappeared a few years before the campaign when he accidentally opened a portal to [[Mechanus]] during an experiment in the mansion's basement.

The party found him among a group of stranded, clearly insane beings living underground in the Wastes of Gravegear. The group would not let the party leave, and [[The Sons of Thunder]] were forced to fight and kill them.

## Connections

- [[The Sons of Thunder]], previous owner of their mansion in [[Whitehall]]
- [[Whitehall]], where his mansion stood
- [[Mechanus]], stranded there after his experiment went wrong
