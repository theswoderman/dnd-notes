# Kelara

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]]'s sister, held captive at [[Jiren's Rock]] by [[Krintak Cinderheart]]'s dragonborn. Freed by [[The Sons of Thunder]], she revealed she was pregnant — and that the father was [[Kuzo Clawborn]], the leonin who beat [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] in the duel that got him exiled.

She then guided the party to the [[Tulari Hot Springs]].

## Ten years later
Recorded in the Panrelta prelude as **Lioness Kalara Clawborne**, sister of [[Bathoz Manefell]]. She welcomed [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] and [[Dwendall Dhormas]] to the [[Tulari Hot Springs]] as pilgrims and hosted them for nearly a week — see [[Thalrik's Road to Stonehold]].

> Spelling unresolved: the vault has **Kelara**, the prelude has **Kalara Clawborne**.
