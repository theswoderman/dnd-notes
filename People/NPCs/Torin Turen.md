# Torin Turen

**Race:** Gnome
**Role:** High priest of [[Illmater]]; leader of [[The Bettermans Guild]]
**Mount:** [[Lightfoot]], a pegasus
**Status:** Alive — alone in the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]], and failing

## Summary
Leads [[The Bettermans Guild]] from [[The Iron Oak Woods]], sheltering refugees and supporting those trying to do good. Under his leadership the camp has grown from roughly 100 people to 300, with about 250 of them in the guild proper.

**He is coming apart.** While [[The Good Ones]] were in the deep mines he held the camp against [[Mossclaw]] incursions and barely slept. [[Ky'ar Lastet]] thinks he may die soon.

## What is wrong with him
Told to the party himself, over a drink at their tower after the feast — see [[Return to the Bettermans Guild]].

- **His relationship with [[Illmater]] has worsened.** He can still reach his god when he actively tries. When he is not actively communing, he feels **another presence**
- [[Gegga]] cast identify on him. It returned no real information, but she felt **raw spiritual and divine energy pouring out of him**
- His dreams have gotten worse and they **drain** him. He dreams of a **black grail above a stone tomb**. When the grail meets the tomb the stone cracks, red blood comes out, and it bursts into green flames that melt the black grail. **Every night the flames burn brighter and longer than the night before**
- Something is eating at him

[[Beemo]] healed him with lesser restoration. He refused anything further — [[Illmater]] would want him to bear his suffering.

## What he asked for
- To be taken to the **temple of [[Illmater]] in the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn Forest]]**
- That the party **call to [[Illmater]] when they are at their lowest**

He also said he is confident in [[Ky'ar Lastet|Ky'ar]]'s ability to lead [[The Bettermans Guild]].

## "Little shadow"
As he left the tower he called [[Beemo]] **"little shadow"** — a name [[Beemo]] has only ever been called by hags. The party chased him down and put him under zone of truth. **Torin does not know why he knows the name, and has never spoken to a hag.** He was telling the truth.

So something else put it in his head. The obvious candidate is the **other presence** he feels where [[Illmater]] should be.

## He left without them
Before dawn he rode out on [[Lightfoot]], leaving a pegasus feather and a note addressed to [[Ky'ar Lastet|Ky'ar]] — leaving him the camp, and saying [[The Good Ones]] had already left for the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]] and that [[Illmater]] bid him in a dream to catch them. **They had not left.** The party thinks the hag lured him out.

[[Falstad Firebeard]] scried on [[Lightfoot]] and found him digging up a corpse with a shovel and casting speak with dead on it — a soldier of [[Bane]]. He then wandered deeper into the forest. See [[Torin's Flight into the Sagethorn]].

## The grail may be inside him
Questioned after death, [[The Collector]] was asked whether [[The Black Grail]] was in the temple of [[Illmater]] and answered **"it is within the small gnome."** [[Torin Turen|Torin]] is the only gnome in play. Both [[Malar]]'s pack and [[Bane]]'s followers want the grail, and both intend to carry it to [[The Abbey]]. See [[Speak with Dead with the Collector]].

That recasts almost everything already recorded about him. The grail he has dreamed of every night is not a symbol, the presence displacing [[Illmater]] has a reason to be there, and two rival faiths have a reason to want him alive and portable.

A gnomish scream came out of the moving corpses at [[The Frozen Riverbank]]. Nobody has confirmed it was his.
