# Zarisk

**Also known as:** Zeke, Ceris
**Race:** Silver dragon
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
A silver dragon who has taken many forms over the course of [[The Good Ones]]' journey, guiding the party's path.

As Ceris, a wood elf ranger, he rescued [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] from his goblin clan, the [[Mossclaw]], when Gribnik was young, and raised him. He has been cultivating [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]]'s growth ever since, and has grown more interested in his exploits over time.

As Zeke, a guard in [[Saltport]], he freed [[The Good Ones]] during their time there, before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined them.

The fat silver sparrows that have appeared to the party, going back to before [[Khulask Hollow]], were Zarisk or his familiars. They are one of the ways he has kept an eye on the party.

## At the abbey
On the night of the full moon he attacked the werewolves outside [[The Abbey]], destroying most of the abbey in the process and defeating every lycanthrope in the vicinity. They were members of the [[Black Blood Pack]]. The blue haze left ringing the ruins is from his cold breath.

He came to the blocked tunnel where the party was resting, knocking as Zeke and speaking in the voice of Ceris, and outside revealed that the two were one and that his name is Zarisk.

He granted [[Twitchtail]], [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]]'s beast companion, a draconic form, and [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]]'s eye gained a draconic silver coloration. He then carried the entire party, and [[Torin Turen]] with them, to [[Temple Trueheart]]. See [[The Destruction of the Abbey]].

The spellings of all three names are unconfirmed.
