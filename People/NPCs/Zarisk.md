# Zarisk

**Also known as:** Zeke, Ceris
**Race:** Silver dragon
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
A silver dragon who has taken many forms over the course of [[The Good Ones]]' journey, guiding the party's path. Two of the forms are known. As Ceris, a wood elf ranger, he raised [[Gribnik Mossfoot]]. As Zeke, a guard in [[Saltport]], he freed [[The Good Ones]] during their time there, before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined them. He has also been cultivating [[Beemo]]'s growth over the years.

## At the abbey
On the night of the full moon he attacked the werewolves outside [[The Abbey]], destroying most of the abbey in the process and defeating every lycanthrope in the vicinity. He came to the blocked tunnel where the party was resting, knocking as Zeke and speaking in the voice of Ceris, and outside revealed that the two were one and that his name is Zarisk.

He granted [[Twitchtail]], [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]]'s beast companion, a draconic form, and [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]]'s eye gained a draconic silver coloration. He then carried the entire party to [[Temple Trueheart]]. See [[The Destruction of the Abbey]].

The spellings of all three names are unconfirmed. See [[Panrelta Loose Ends]] for open questions.
