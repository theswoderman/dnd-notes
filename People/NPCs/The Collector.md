# The Collector

**Status:** Dead — killed by [[Gegga]]
**Nature:** Not celestial, fiend, or undead. Werewolf, cured. Self-declared servant of [[Malar]]

## Summary
A merchant encountered on the road back to [[The Bettermans Guild]], traveling with a cart full of animal body parts — including **centaur hooves**, which is what set the party against him. He called himself a "collector" of them. The cart smelled horrible and the whole encounter felt wrong.

Centaur hooves are exactly what [[The Maiden]]'s poachers were harvesting in [[The Centaur Poachers]], which suggests that operation is still running.

He said he had encountered [[The Maiden]], and that she claimed to have acquaintances "on yorrisk upon the dawning". **[[Yorrisk]] has since been identified** as a developing town that [[House Keroskav]] and [[House Dufrey]] are at war over, and [[The Dawning]] is the celebration marking [[Aeralath]]'s defeat — the tenth of which is approaching. He said he was headed for [[Starfall Clearing]].

[[Beemo]] cast detect thoughts on him; probing deeper returned nothing but a wolf growling. He then transformed into a werewolf. The party buffed [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] with magic weapon and haste and nearly killed the Collector in a single turn, then cured his lycanthropy and knocked him out.

See [[The Collector on the Road]].

## The morning after
He woke up **furious that they had cured him**. He called himself a **servant of [[Malar]]** — the Beastlord, god of hunters and lycanthropes — and warned that the **[[Black Blood Pack]]** would come for them.

[[Gegga]] killed him.

The party then found **boxes of black dragon scales** in the back of his cart, packed like a smuggling run. The only black dragon anyone knows to have died was killed by the [[Blood Raven|Blood Ravens]], and a second dragon death seems unlikely — so the scales are probably from that one.

His body went into the bag, alongside [[The Abyssal Orb]]. The party's own assessment: probably a horrible idea.
