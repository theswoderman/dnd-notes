# Aeroth

**Race:** High elf
**Affiliation:** [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]], [[Temple Trueheart]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
A high elf of [[Temple Trueheart]]. Aeroth and [[Diggory Psalm]] greeted [[The Good Ones]] when [[Zarisk]] brought them to the temple. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].

Aeroth and [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]] fought the party first in the order's sparring match, and [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] joined once [[Gilead Dragonsong|Gilead]] was defeated. The party knocked Aeroth down soon after. See [[The Sparring Match at Temple Trueheart]].

A member of [[The Order of the Golden Gauntlet]].

Spelling of the name is unconfirmed.
