# The Rockspeaker

**Race:** Stone giant
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
A stone giant found deep in the mines, guarding [[The Garden of Dreams]], a place sacred to his people where the ancient memories of giants are stored and protected.

[[The Good Ones]] were sent to retrieve miners who had delved too greedily and too deep. The Rockspeaker had taken them and was wiping their memories so they would not return, and would have done the same to [[The Good Ones]] had they not beaten him in battle.

He surrendered, then asked for their help against the purple worm infesting the mountain. See [[Exploration of the deep mines]].
