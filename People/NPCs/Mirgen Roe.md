# Mirgen Roe

**Affiliation:** [[Khulask Hollow]], later [[The Bettermans Guild]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
Took over leadership of [[Khulask Hollow]] after the death of [[Crucius Ilyanich]]. When the town was burned by [[The Drow]], he traveled with [[The Good Ones]] to [[The Bettermans Guild]] along with the other survivors.

He told [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] that he is always welcome back, because of how [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] treated him and his son before the overthrow.

## Connections
- [[Thalrik Lastfeast]], owes him goodwill
- [[Crucius Ilyanich]], his predecessor
