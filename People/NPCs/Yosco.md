# Yosco

**Status:** Alive, whereabouts unknown
**Appears in:** both campaigns — [[The Sons of Thunder]] and [[The Good Ones]]

## Summary
The same Yosco appears in both campaigns, making him one of the confirmed points of contact between them.

## In Campaign 1
Scrying on Yosco aboard the boat revealed that [[Dolghast]] is planning something soon. [[King Berrigan]] ordered [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] to find him and lead Alnorian forces to [[Dolghast]] to end the war — still an open thread on the [[Campaign 1 Timeline]].

## In Panrelta
Told [[The Good Ones]] about [[The Maiden]], and said she is also known as the Laughing Princess.

## Why he matters
So far he is the only figure who has given both parties information about the other's side of the world — [[Dolghast]] to one, [[The Maiden]] to the other.

## Connections
- [[The Maiden]] — he identified her as the Laughing Princess
- [[Dolghast]] — subject of the Campaign 1 scrying
- [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] — ordered to find him
