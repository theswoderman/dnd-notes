# Orithian Blackspire

**Status:** Unknown

## Summary
A name from a dream. [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] dreamed of [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|the Laughing Princess constellation]] and came out of it with this name and some connection to [[The Drow]].

Orithian blackspire is the drow who built the abbey, in an attempt to bring the drow above ground before there was a mutiny against him.

See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]].
