# Twitchtail

**Affiliation:** [[Gribnik Mossfoot]]'s beast companion
**Status:** Alive

## Summary
The beast companion of [[Gribnik Mossfoot]], which he can summon as a beastmaster ranger.

Outside the ruins of [[The Abbey]], the silver dragon [[Zarisk]] granted Twitchtail a draconic form. See [[The Destruction of the Abbey]]. According to [[Zarisk]], it took that form because in some way [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]] has the heart of a dragon. See [[Arrival at Temple Trueheart]].
