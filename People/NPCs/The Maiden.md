# The Maiden

**Also known as:** The Laughing Princess
**Race:** Dark elf
**Role:** Boss of the poaching operation that was harvesting centaur hooves
**Status:** Alive, whereabouts unknown

## Summary
[[The Good Ones]] met her in their first few sessions, before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined. She led the poachers who were killing centaurs for their hooves. See [[The Centaur Poachers]].

Her introduction was simple: she showed up, beat [[The Good Ones]], and told them to do what she said if they wanted to live. She seems extremely dangerous, but she is not indiscriminately hostile. She let them live when killing them would have been easier.

## The debt
The party had killed a great many of her men. She spared them on two conditions:

- 10,000 gold
- The heart of an apex griffon, which was killed in [[The Apex Griffon Hunt]]

Neither condition has been discharged. [[The Good Ones]] have not encountered her again since, so the heart is undelivered and the gold unsettled. They are expected to meet her again.

## Reported sightings
- [[Yosco]] identified her as the Laughing Princess
- [[The Collector]] said he had encountered her, and that she claimed to have acquaintances "on yorrisk upon the dawning". His cart carried centaur hooves, which suggests the poaching operation is still running. He is now dead, since [[Gegga]] killed him

## Yorrisk: identified
[[Yorrisk]] is a developing town that [[House Keroskav]] of [[Grekosha]] and Iron Oak [[House Dufrey]] are at war over. [[Falstad Firebeard]] recognized the two heraldries after a shared nightmare and remembered the dispute. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]].

That resolves the "yorrisk upon the dawning" line, and it lines up with something the party already knew: she told them she would meet them in [[Yorrisk]], before [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] joined. [[The Dawning]], the tenth of which is approaching, reads as the date.

The debt is still outstanding.

## The constellation
[[The Laughing Princess Constellation|A constellation]] carries her other name, and the party takes the two to be connected. It matters because a map they picked up early in the campaign places [[The Abbey]] and the lost temple beneath those stars, which is where [[Bane]] and [[Malar]]'s followers are both taking [[The Black Grail]]. Her constellation hangs over the place they are walking toward. See [[The Climb to the Sagethorn]].
