# Session 30 notes

## Last session (Session 29)

The session opened with a speak with dead on [[The Collector]]'s corpse. [[Beemo]] disguised himself as a packmate and got five answers. The key reveals: [[The Black Grail]] is inside [[Torin Turen]], [[Malar]] and [[Bane]] are rivals not allies, and both want the grail taken to [[The Abbey]] near [[The Tree of Pain]]. The corpse was answering someone it thought was friendly, so how much of this is true is an open question. [[Beemo]] dropped the disguise as the spell ended so the corpse knew it was deceived.

The party left their horses at [[The Bettermans Guild]]'s camp, took two weeks of supplies, and [[Beemo]] used treestride to cover sixty miles toward the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]]. Along the way [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] climbed into the bag of holding and [[The Abyssal Orb]] spoke for the first time: "Free me." He got out fast and told the others.

They scaled an 800-1000 foot cliff with [[Gegga]]'s fly spell, fought off two rounds of siren song from caves in the rock (only [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]] was charmed, couldn't act on it), and [[Beemo]] dumped [[The Collector]]'s body off the cliff. [[Gegga]] raised a tower to shelter from a storm. That night, [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]], [[Thol]] and [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]] dreamed of the third hag: [[Sila Frostblood]], a bheur hag with dire wolves on chains. First time she has appeared in anyone's dreams.

The next morning they used find the path and treestride to reach the frozen riverbank from the dream. Corpses everywhere: [[Bane]]'s soldiers and all kinds of beasts, not fresh but not old. The corpses started moving, a gnomish scream came from them, and [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]], [[Thol]] and [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] were pulled toward the water. Werewolves and an enormous panther attacked. The fight ran to the end of the session.
