### The World of Estrana 

This campaign is set in the homebrewed world of [[Estrana]] (many thanks to [[Reed|Reed Miller]]). It’s largely compatible with the classic lore of the Forgotten Realms we’ve played in prior campaigns and seen in BG3, with a few notable changes driven by the player choices made in our party’s previous campaign.

  

A little more than a decade ago a long dormant primordial evil, [[Aeralath|Aerolath]], reawakened. One of the original forces of creation and the mother of all demons, this presented a not so fun problem for all things outside of [[the abyss]]. [[Aeralath|Aerolath]]’s reawakening spurred on a chain of events as her minions and devotees spread her influence across the planes. This resulted in a war breaking out between all the nations of the two major continents in [[Estrana]]--[[Anendal]] and [[Panrelta]]. Map of [[Panrelta]]: 

[https://cdn2.inkarnate.com/1190460-8131bf12-bb55-11f0-aab4-ba97be869f98?disposition=attachment](https://cdn2.inkarnate.com/1190460-8131bf12-bb55-11f0-aab4-ba97be869f98?disposition=attachment)

  

This became the world’s first Great War and the following nations took part in it. On the “Good Side” were…

- [[Alnoria]]: The classic fantasy setting. This is where the party first joined together.
    
- [[Rorinden]]: Former rival and current ally of [[Alnoria]]. Got some dope griffons.
    
- [[Loradris]]: Inset elves here. Big time forest folk. 
    
- [[Vallenhein]]: Insert dwarves here. Big time mountain folk.
    
- [[Nilstraad]]: Technically neutral until [[Dolghast]] forces attacked [[Stonehold]], They won the battle with the help of [[the Sons of Thunder]].
    

  

On the “Bad Side” were the following nations…

- [[Dolghast]]: Russia to [[Alnoria]]’s America. Dolgahst has strong ties to [[the Drow]] of the underdark and their Queen is heavily influenced by the return of [[Aeralath|Aerolath]].
    
- [[Grekosha]]: Think Transylvania. Many levels of leadership and nobility were vampires or under vampiric influence until that scourge was cut out by [[the Sons of Thunder]]. Once the vampire menace had been eradicated, [[Grekosha]] allied itself with the Alnorian forces.
    
- [[Ornuth]]: Not much to say here, they didn’t play much of a role. After [[Grekosha]] flipped and [[Dolghast]] failed to take [[Stonehold]], [[Ornuth]] opted out of the war.
    

  

Ultimately, [[The Demon War|the Great War]] was ended by [[the Sons of Thunder]]… just in time for [[Aeralath|Aerolath]]’s army of demons to arrive, starting [[the Demon War]]. To make a long story short, [[the Sons of Thunder]] defeated [[Aeralath|Aerolath]] and saved existence itself (level 20 do be crazy like that). This victory was marked by a new celebration throughout the material plane known as [[The Dawning]]. As the tenth celebration of [[The Dawning]] draws near, we find a new adventure unfolding in the Panreltan continent…

  

### The Pilgrimage

[[Estrana]] is a decade into restoring what was lost to [[the Demon War]]. [[The Sons of Thunder]] restored much of what was lost, but the foundations of the world that was known before have been cracked by the great cataclysm. Nearing the tenth celebration of [[the Dawning]], an intrepid group of adventurers finds themselves joining one another in a historical pilgrimage created to honor the unification of so many different peoples joined together in [[the Dawning]].

  

The same year that [[Aeralath|Aerolath]] was defeated, [[Bathoz Manefell]] (Tyler’s PC) returned home to the [[Golden Plains]] and finished his peace talks with the Dragonborn clans his people once warred with. Together, they worked out a lasting truce between their peoples that allowed the Dragonborn to remain near their sacred Tulari Hotsprings along with the Leonin tribes. 

  

Having learned many lessons from his travels across the world and its great patchwork of cultures, [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] also sought to bring this opportunity for "enlightenment" to both Dragonborn and Leonin clans so that they might have the same chance to wander the world and still have a safe, uncontested home to return to.

  

[[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] made an agreement with the newly crowned Grekoshan King ([[Cameron]]’s PC) to allow these people to travel from the [[Golden Plains]] to lands once known as the [[Blackwaste|Black Waste]], now a verdant haven. Many travelers walked this path that year hoping to find that same wisdom in themselves and it soon became a recognized rite amongst their people… a pilgrimage made holy by the desire to better one’s self and people.

  

Tenets of [[The Pilgrimage]]: Travelers who embark on this pilgrimage must abide by the following tenants.

- End the journey with a better soul than when you began. The journey is a physical challenge, but it will not bestow any blessings upon you. You undertake this journey because you believe you can be better--you will make yourself better.
    
- Help those who ask for it--protect those who cannot protect themselves.
    
- Travel on the ground by foot or by saddle. The longer the journey the more you stand to learn of yourself.
    
- If you leave the road you should return to where you left it before resuming your journey. Travel the entire path.
    

  

Character Creation: The journey needs a reason. What about the tenets of [[The Pilgrimage]] have drawn your character in? Other basic creation rules detailed below:

- Use either 5e or 1dnd rules, just let me know which
    
- Point Buy for Stats
    
- Roll for Health (re-rolling 1’s)
    
- One (sensible) common Wondrous Item
    
- Provisions… maybe start with more? 2-3 extra pieces of equipment just run them by me
    
- ENCUMBERANCE will be enforced (excluding coin)
    

### Thalrik Lastfeast’s Origin

[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] hails from the small mercantile port town of [[Hor Garam]] (SE of [[Stonehold]]). Somewhere in his youth he demonstrated an aptitude for the combative arts and managed to gain tutelage from a former member of the [[Bronze Rams]]--[[Brom Stonefist]]. 

*Brom was disfigured in the Battle of [[Stonehold]] and was granted a semicentury release from his brotherhood, leading him to retire to a quiet life of recovery in [[Hor Garam]].

  

In time, [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] found a higher calling and took his Oath of Devotion to [[Torm]], and eventually found that call pulling him away from his home and into the wider world. With the Tulari Hotsprings so near to home, [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] set out to follow the Panreltan Pilgrimage and “walk the lion’s path”. 

*[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] was joined by a childhood friend and fellow adventurer, [[Dwendall Dhormas]] (M, Dwarf, ~120, Champion Fighter) who was also eager to see the world.

  

**When Brom learned [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] was setting out on [[The Pilgrimage]], he urged him to make contact with an old mentor of his, [[Magus Trollbeater|Maygus Trollbeater]]--he would see that [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] at least had a place to stay in the city. 

  

[[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] and Dwendall traversed southwest through [[the Shiftweave]] (plains of Nilstrad) for nearly a week before reaching the Tulari Hotsprings, facing more than one dangerous encounter on the way. Welcomed as Pilgrims by the Leonin tribes Lioness [[Kelara|Kalara Clawborne]] (sister of [[Bathoz Manefell]], Tyler’s PC and the hero who brokered the peace between Dragonborn/Leonin tribes and inspired [[The Pilgrimage]]), the two dwarves were hosted by the tribe for nearly a week before a few more pilgrims had gathered--three more adventurers joined [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] and Dwendall. 

- [[Sven]], weak human wizard
    
- [[Hansel]], disgraced noble man seeking atonement
    
- [[Clara]], a lost and wandering druid
    

  

Traversing across the northern banks of the [[Skyfang Lake]] and the borders of [[the Shiftweave]], the party faced many challenges but ultimately reached the western road to [[Saltport]] in one road-weary piece. Tragedy, however, is never far away from an adventurer. While camping along the road one night, an ambush by a Bulette claimed the lives of [[Sven]], [[Hansel]], and [[Clara]]. Clearly outmatched and unprepared to handle this foe on their own, [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] and Dwendall fled to [[Saltport]], unable to save their fellow pilgrims.

  

The two friends took shelter in the coastal town for a few days before Dwendall, realizing that he had neither the stomach nor the nerve to face such slaughter again, decided he would not finish his pilgrimage nor would he cross the road back home--he chose to stay in [[Saltport]] and save up enough gold to by passage over sea back to [[Hor Garam]]. He bid farewell to [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] and sent him on his way to [[Stonehold]].

  

Feeling unmoored by the loss of recent companions and close friend, [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] finds himself adrift in the massive Dwarven city. Try as he might, he has not yet been able to find the Bronze Ram member Brom urged him to find. For nearly a fortnight [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] has meandered through the city looking for odd jobs and minor holy responsibilities to keep himself busy while he gathers his spirits to embark on the second leg of [[The Pilgrimage]].

### Glossary/Appendix

Important People

- [[The Sons of Thunder]]: The intrepid band of heroes who ended the war between Dolgahst and the allied nations by defeating the primeval [[Aeralath|Aerolath]], [[Aeralath|Mother of Demons]]. 
    

- [[Bathoz Manefell]]: Leonin, Totem Barbarian, Dreadclaw
    

- The moral heart of [[the Sons of Thunder]] and [[Aon|the one]] who wielded [[Queensbane]], the only weapon strong enough to kill [[Aeralath|Aerolath]]. After the war’s end, [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]] returned to his tribe for a time and, whether he meant to or not, inspired the Panreltan Pilgrimage. He also adopted a son--a half-orc orphan from [[Whitehall]] the party met early in their travels.
    

- [[Davynn Brindleknot]]: Gnome, Evocation Wizard, loves fire and tiefling baddies
    

- [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] is among the most preeminent wizards of his time and may not have a true rival in the material plane. 
    
- After the war he founded an arcane college in the restored and verdant lands of the [[Blackwaste]]. *College name tbd. 
    

- [[Diggory Psalm]]: Satyr, Eloquence Bard/Cleric, [[Hand of Truth]], current mortal champion of [[Torm]]
    

- [[Diggory Psalm]] was raised in the woods of [[Thalonir]] before he found his way into [[the Sons of Thunder]]. Having been claimed by [[Torm]] during their adventures, [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] now acts as counsel to the King of [[Grekosha]], occasional neutral diplomat (he serves the god of truth after all), and in the last five years has constructed a holy temple in the [[Dragonspine mountains]] where he trains a new band of heroes under the banner of [[Torm]].
    

- [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge]]: Half-Elf Paladin (originally Dhampir) of The Red Knight, current mortal champion of [[The Red Lady]].
    

- Born to the vampiric lord of [[Nostra]], overthrew his father with [[the Sons of Thunder]] and ascended to the title of [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Raven Lord]] (General), and then to royalty. Following the defeat of [[Aeralath|Aerolath]], [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] became king of [[Grekosha]] and wed Queen/General [[Diana Zethros]] of [[Rorinden]] (also a Paladin of The Red Knight). He fathered three children, two of whom were born as aasimar/tiefling twin sons (it’s a long story).
    

- [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn Malkovitch]]: 
    

- Descended from the Viera people (homebrewed race or rabbit-people from Final Fantasy I think) of the [[Whisperwood|Whispering Wood]] before being banished and eventually  adopted by a wealthy family from [[Zardoba]]. [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] eventually made a career for himself as a detective in [[Whitehall]] and now serves under [[King Berrigan]] as chief inspector (or something like that). After the first Dawning, [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] founded a restaurant. He still serves the Alnorian crown. 
    

  

Important Places

- Tulari Hotsprings: The ancestral home of many Leonin and Dragonborn clans regarded as sacred to both parties. After the peace brokered by [[Bathoz Manefell|Bathoz]], this is now the primary home of the Leonin tribe, though Dragonborn are still welcomed whenever they choose to visit. This is one of the two places the Panreltan Pilgrimage can begin.
    
- [[Ishtir Marsh|Ishtar Marsh]]: The current home of the chief dragonborn clan. This is the other location from which [[The Pilgrimage]] can begin and is where the current party started.
    
- [[Hor Garam]]: A coastal port town well suited for trade. You can find a little bit of everything here.
    
- [[Saltport]]: In many ways a sister-city to [[Hor Garam]], as if it had been transplanted on the western coast of [[Nilstraad]]. The primary difference is the presence of an abundant salt mine.
    
- [[Stonehold]]: A massive dwarven city carved directly into a mountain and the site of an important battle in [[The Demon War|the Great War]].
    

  
  

Relevant Histories and Cosmology

- The Refugees of [[Dolghast]]: The once powerful empire of [[Dolghast]] is defeated. With the death of the fearsome [[Queen Nocticula]] a great power vacuum emerged and spurred on an endless string of power-grabbing despots trying to establish a new foothold for themselves. The country fell to ruin and many of the common folk who had long been crushed under the cruel heel of Nocticulan rule have fled anywhere they can, mainly to [[Grekosha]] and [[Nilstraad]]/[[Stonehold]]. Their arrival has stirred a heated debate in both countries--some welcome them as abused refugees while others still see the faces of their recent enemies. 
    
- Interplanar Relations: Aerloath took advantage of a once-a-millenia planar convergence to open a multitude of gates to [[the Abyss]] from which her armies surged forth. In defeating her, these gates were sealed and so were many others. Direct travel to other planes is now next to impossible for mortal beings.
    
- The Battle of [[Stonehold]]: A decisive battle in [[The Demon War|the Great War]]. Although the battle began with [[Kyron Delinor|The Harbinger]] leveling a portion of the city’s wall (he cast earthquake and meteor swarm back to back, shit was nutty), [[the Sons of Thunder]] managed to push back the [[Dolghast]] forces and slayed an enemy leader, [[General Vadahm]], forcing the enemy into retreat.
