While he sleeps, Thalrik is granted a dream by Torm…  Just as you feel your sense giving way to a deep rest, your mind and body jolt awake. You do not wake by your friends, but in an endless, bountiful field of grain. The brilliant rays of the day's last golden hour shine across the field from behind you, reaching all the way to the foot of a foreboding mountain range. The slate ranges and snowy peaks before you are mired in cold shadow--high above these hills sit dark, boiling thunderheads so tall you cannot see their end.  You feel the earth begin to tremble--the thunderheads collapse beneath their own weight and let pour torrential winds, shadow, and, impossibly, an army of embers. Grain catches flame, the soft soil splits beneath your feet--imperilled, you brace yourself for a fall.  With one great final lurch you feel the earth steady itself… and begin to shrink beneath you. You’re rising--you look down and see the ground you stand on has become an earthen gauntlet the size of a castle! Clods of the stone and dirt are jettisoned from the gauntlet, revealing rusted and scarred metal as you ascend above the mountains, then the clouds, then the highest heights of this atmosphere and into the cold emptiness of the stars the light of which glows gold against the armored hand.   The hand closes, darkening your sight completely before it opens again and reveals before you a colossal golden dragon coiled around a mountain peak made of bright celestial light. The dragon rises from its perch atop this heavenly body with a triumphant roar that shakes through your body even in this dream state--its eyes burst into blazing golden light as its wings unfurl, blanketing the night like constellations. Its eyes level on you. Its mouth opens, but only to release a mote of stardust that begins to float towards you, pooling upon beneath your feet.  "Golden Pilgrim, your devotion has led you many miles, healed many wounds, and brought judgement to many foes. Good has been made of your oath, Thalrik Lastfeast… and Torm is pleased. Carry now in your hands what you wield in your heart--a brave star of valor. Let this fierce light lead you to Temple Trueheart and see what awaits those of the golden soul."  The Dragon swoops down to the gauntlet and hovers before him, bearing its chest which is opened by a seam of white light. This seam parts the dragons the chest and opens a portal to a snowy mountain peak and The Hand of Truth, Diggory Psalm, who beckons him forth through the portal.  Diggory Psalm tells him that Jäkel informed him of the threat in the Dragonspine, something that went unseen by both Diggory and Torm.  Torm bid him to impart a gift to Thalrik to ensure his safe passage and eventual invitation to the Knights of the Golden Gauntlet.  Diggory tells Thalrik that when he reached out to Torm for more than strength, Torm granted him his unique abilities at the cost of his soul--having sensed its purity, Torm could see this is always where his path would have taken him.
