# Panrelta Loose Ends

Unresolved threads from the [[00 - Panrelta Index|Pilgrims of Panrelta]] campaign.

## Immediate

- [[The Black Grail]] is a ritual, and it consumed part of [[Torin Turen|Torin]]'s soul to make [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]] a death knight before [[The Good Ones]] interrupted it. Whether the lost part of his soul can be restored is unknown
- [[The Abyssal Orb]] spoke for the first time. It told [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] "free me"
- [[Torin Turen]] was revived by the clerics at [[Temple Trueheart]] and has been seen only briefly since. What state he is in with part of his soul consumed is unknown. The party thinks a hag lured him into the forest, and what he wanted with the soldier of [[Bane]] he dug up is also unknown
- The true nature of the [[Right of Return]] is unknown, and the magical source of the blessing has not been divined. [[Diggory Psalm]] asked the party about it at [[Temple Trueheart]], and none of them could say
- [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]] carries the curse of the Dead Three, laid on him by [[Oraecheon Blackspire|Blackspire]]. [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] lost his divine connection at the moment it set in, though he can still cast. Whether [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s loss is the same curse, and whether cutting off [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]]'s arm ended it, are unknown
- [[Berrenar]] suggested [[Falstad Firebeard|Falstad]] draw his power from another god and named [[Illmater]]. The choice is his and has not been made
- [[Aeroth]], [[Gillead Dragonsong]] and [[Diggory Psalm]] are to challenge [[The Good Ones]] to a sparring match at [[Temple Trueheart]]. [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] secretly holds the [[Golden Hourglass]], to use in it or not, and must return it before the party leaves
- [[Ky'ar Lastet]] has the camp and wants the guild. [[Torin Turen|Torin]] endorsed him, but the ambition predates the endorsement. Whether he can hold it against the [[Mossclaw]] without [[Torin Turen|Torin]] is unclear
- The [[Mossclaw]] raids on [[The Bettermans Guild]]'s camp, and the one with the black mohawk who is expected back with reinforcements. The [[Mossclaw]] are [[Gribnik Mossfoot]]'s former tribe, and the raider [[Ky'ar Lastet]] saw matches a goblin the party once spared. Whether they know [[Gribnik Mossfoot|Gribnik]] is with [[The Good Ones]] is unknown. Whether the raids are connected to the [[House Dufrey]]--[[House Keroskav]] dispute over [[Yorrisk]] is also unclear
- One of the guild's stonemasons never came back from the deep mines. What the guild expects of the party for it is unclear. [[Aisha]]'s role in [[The Bettermans Guild]] is also unrecorded

## The hags

- [[Mirna Gribwood]], a dusk hag and [[Beemo]]'s aunt, is in the party's dreams, and the whole coven is circle casting it together. Spelling of her name unconfirmed. See [[The Dusk Hag's Dreams]]
- She appears to everyone except [[Beemo]], who is the one they want dead. No explanation
- "I like this mind of yours, I'll stay a while." Whether she is now lodged in someone, and whose
- [[Torin Turen]] called [[Beemo]] "little shadow", a name only hags have used for him, and then said under zone of truth that he does not know why he knows it and has never spoken to a hag. The other presence he feels where [[Illmater]] should be is the obvious candidate
- After weeks of the coven's dreams, the party had good dreams for once, and [[Beemo]] woke feeling lighter. Whether the coven's hold has slipped, or something else changed, is unclear
- The [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] are trying to kill [[Beemo]], and may also be working to resurrect a dragon. Both he and the shade they sent after him are their creations, one "successful" and one not, and it is unclear which is which. Why the coven wants him dead, and what they were trying to create, are unknown. See [[The Shade Sent for Beemo]]
- Whatever the [[Sisters of the Gnarled Veil]] are planning is happening in the [[Dragonspine Mountains]], the same range where [[Diggory Psalm]] built his temple to [[Torm]]. It went unseen by both [[Diggory Psalm|Diggory]] and [[Torm]] until [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jäkel]] warned them
- [[Sila Frostblood]], a bheur hag, is the coven's third member and entered the party's dreams for the first time on the fourth night, showing them a riverbank they reached the next morning. Why now, whether it was warning or bait, what her dire wolves are, and whether she shares the coven's interest in killing [[Beemo]]
- [[Oraecheon Blackspire]]'s name came from [[Gribnik Mossfoot]]'s dream of [[The Laughing Princes Constellation|the Laughing Princes constellation]]. Why the coven delivered it is unknown
- Whether [[Torin Turen]]'s worsening dreams are the same coven's work. The party thinks a hag lured him into the [[Sage Thorn Forest|Sagethorn]], but his dream is different in kind: a black grail and a stone tomb, no hag

## The Maiden and Yorrisk

- The party is supposed to meet back up with [[The Maiden]]. The debt is still outstanding: they have not seen her since [[The Centaur Poachers]], so the 10,000 gold is unsettled and the apex griffon heart undelivered, though the griffon itself is dead
- [[Yorrisk]] is identified, a developing town that [[House Keroskav]] of [[Grekosha]] and Iron Oak [[House Dufrey]] are at war over. [[The Maiden]] said she would meet the party there, and "upon the dawning" points at the tenth [[The Dawning|Dawning]], which is approaching. Where [[Yorrisk]] actually is remains unknown
- Whether the battle for [[Yorrisk]] the hag showed [[Thol]], [[Beemo]] and [[Gribnik Mossfoot]] is memory, prophecy or fabrication
- Whether [[The Maiden]] is backing one of the two houses
- What the beams of light above the dragon in the [[House Keroskav]] vision stand for
- Whether [[The Bettermans Guild]] has any standing relationship with [[House Dufrey]]

## From the Collector

- Boxes of black dragon scales in his cart, packed like a smuggling run. The only known black dragon kill was the [[The Blood Ravens|Blood Ravens]]', so the scales are probably from that one. Who was buying is unknown, and whether the [[Black Blood Pack]] is involved
- [[Starfall Clearing]] was his destination, confirmed by his corpse but never explained. Whether it is still worth visiting
- Whether he was working for [[The Maiden]]. The centaur hooves in his cart suggest yes
- His body is out of the bag and off a cliff. [[The Abyssal Orb]] is alone in there now
