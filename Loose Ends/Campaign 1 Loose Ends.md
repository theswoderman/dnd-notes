# Campaign 1 Loose Ends

Unresolved threads from Campaign 1. [[Reed]]'s summary now covers through the party's arrival in [[Limbo]]; the endgame is still unrecorded. See [[Campaign 1 Timeline]].

- [[Dolghast]] is planning something drastic against [[Alnoria]]. [[King Berrigan]] ordered [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] to find [[Yosco]] and lead Alnorian forces into [[Dolghast]] to end the war after they return from [[Limbo]]. See [[The Voyage South]]
- The flames that guided [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] through his father's burning castle were pink, the same shade as portals to [[The Abyss]]. The party suspects [[Aeralath]] caused the fire and may have been steering other events in their lives. See [[The Voyage South]]
- [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]]'s brother has been revived (mentioned in Reed's earlier notes but not yet covered in the detailed summary)
- Two Viera watched [[Loryn Malcovitch|Loryn]] leave the [[Whisperwood]]: a woman his age, and an older male who looks like him
- ~~Who "the child of the stars" is~~ — resolved: [[Yosco]] is the child of the stars that [[Melvin]] mentioned. See [[The Voyage South]]
- The elder brain knew [[Aeralath]]'s name and spoke of "two siblings playing god"
- The Star Spawn Voidling: "the progenitors of this world would return to their rightful place over all things"
- [[Casimir]] is still in the [[Cube of Carrying]]
- [[Zargon the Immortal]] is sealed inside [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]]'s soul. If [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] dies first, the soul is forfeit
- Why [[Aeralath]] wanted [[The Sons of Thunder]] to collect the pieces of [[Queensbane]]. She was helping them gain power specifically so they would reassemble the one weapon that can harm her. The reason is unrecorded
- The Reborn (Awoken) on the hidden continent: [[Nezerai Empire|Nezerai]] souls in constructed bodies, created by [[Vhal Nimzur]]. More are waking each day. What becomes of them? See [[The Hidden Continent]]
- [[The Sons of Thunder]] owe Ghara J'boi a prison break in Cape Durk, [[Dolghast]], as payment for the voyage. See [[The Voyage South]]
- What does [[The Red Lady]] want to tell [[Jäkel Aleister le Rouge|Jakel]] in Warrior's Rest? See [[Into Limbo]]
- Name of the arcane college [[Davynn Brindleknot|Davynn]] founded in the restored [[Blackwaste]], marked TBD in the prelude
