# Suggestion — Stop writing Nic as the main character

**Received:** 2026-08-12
**Status:** Incorporated

## Suggestion

> another style update - stop writing with me (Nic) being the "main character" / main user with the exception to context about early parts of the campaign being missing

## Incorporated into

- [[Grug]] — the summary no longer opens by naming whose character he is, and the connection to [[Thalrik Lastfeast]] is gone. That link existed only because the same person played both, which no other one-shot note does
- [[index]] — the Panrelta section now names all six of [[The Good Ones]] rather than [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] alone, and the missing early record is attributed to the sessions never being written down rather than framed around who joined when. Note count refreshed to roughly 370

## Already covered

## Notes

Scope was settled before anything was written. Two other readings were offered and declined: rewriting CLAUDE.md's own single-user voice, which names Nic sixteen times as the person giving instructions, and rebalancing [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]]'s structural prominence in [[Panrelta Timeline]], where he gets a full pre-party section against one bullet for the rest of the party. Both stand as they are.

The exception is deliberate and now written into the rule: the early Panrelta record is genuinely missing because [[Thalrik Lastfeast|Thalrik]] joined partway through, so [[Panrelta Timeline]] and [[00 - Panrelta Index]] keep saying so. What changed on [[index]] is that the published homepage explains the gap without needing to single anyone out.

[[Loryn Malcovitch]]'s "[[Nick]]'s old character" line and the other Campaign 1 equivalents were left alone. Those follow a convention that applies to every player equally, which is a different thing from writing one player as the protagonist.
