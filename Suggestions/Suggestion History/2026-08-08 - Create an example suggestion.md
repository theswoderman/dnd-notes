It doubles as the reference format for future entries. A normal suggestion adds or corrects campaign information, so its **Incorporated into** section will list actual campaign notes, and the **Already covered** section will point at wherever the information already lived if nothing needed changing.

Conventions settled while processing this one:

- History notes are tagged `ignore` so they stay out of the graph, but keep real wikilinks so they remain clickable
- The suggestion text is quoted verbatim, typos included — this is a record of what was said, not a cleaned-up version
- Campaign notes get **no** attribution lines; the links above are the audit trail
- A suggestion that conflicts with canon or invents a fact is not applied — it gets raised with Nic first
