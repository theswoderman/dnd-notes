## Notes

The scoping line matters as much as the style. Without it a future pass would read the rule as a mandate to sweep the vault, and [[index]] and the rest would get rewritten without anyone asking for it. The section says plainly that the older notes are in the older style on purpose.

Two carve-outs are written in: raw session notes are archive and only ever gain link syntax, and quoted text stays exactly as said. That second one covers the verbatim quotes in this folder, in-world quotes on event notes, and [[Prelude for Thalrik Lastfeast]].
