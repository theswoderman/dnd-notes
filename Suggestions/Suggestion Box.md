Some major events:
  we were on a trip to explore an unexplored island whose location always seems to be changing. we were hit with a bad storm and washed up on the shore and got captured by the locals who seemed to intend to sacrifice us. we broke free of our chains and defeated those who had captured us, some died and others ran away back to their village as a giant crab emerged from the sea, we fought it briefly before running. we were informed that the island had a volcano that would erupt in ~6 hours and that we needed to get to the otherside to catch the main boat back home.
  we chased after the villagers who ran away and went into a cave where we fought some cave creatures before emerging in the locals village to see them performing some sort of ritual. in the village we fought a shaman and a fire elemental, who we later found out functioned as the heart of the volcano, and defeating it accelerated the eruption. we managed to escape safely by working together to find a good path down to the beach and as we rode away we saw the whole island start to move and a giant turtle head emerge from the ocean, revealing that the whole thing was some sort of enormous turtle shaped elemental.
